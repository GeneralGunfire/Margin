Newton's laws describe how forces change the motion of objects.

## Newton's Second Law

The net force on an object equals its mass times its acceleration:

$$F_{net} = ma$$

An object of mass $m = 5\,\text{kg}$ with $F_{net} = 20\,\text{N}$ accelerates at $a = 4\,\text{m·s}^{-2}$.

## Summary

| Law | Statement | Key idea |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | Object stays at rest or at constant velocity unless a net force acts | Inertia |
| Second | $F_{net} = ma$ | Force causes acceleration |
| Third | Forces come in action–reaction pairs | Equal and opposite |
