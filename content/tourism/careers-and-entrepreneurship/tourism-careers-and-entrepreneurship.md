> **What you'll learn:** diverse **job and career opportunities across all six CAPS tourism sectors** (*transport, hospitality, attractions, events & conferences/MICE, tourism services, travel trade*), **academic/training requirements and inherent personal qualities**, matching **personality types to tourism roles**, and **entrepreneurship in tourism** (*entrepreneurial skills, spotting real niche opportunities, and starting a viable tourism enterprise*).
>
> **Prerequisites:** Grade 10/11 tourism sectors and communication/customer care.
>
> **Why this matters:** Section D (`50 marks`) tests **matching learners' personality traits and qualifications to real tourism careers** AND **evaluating small-business tourism ideas** (`identifying gaps in local communities rather than copying saturated businesses`)!

## The idea in plain language

Tourism is one of the few industries in South Africa where **an entry-level passion for people, nature, food, design, or logistics can grow into a lifelong profession OR your own business** — from certified wildlife guides and hotel general managers to event planners and shuttle entrepreneurs!

## Part 1: Jobs Across the Six CAPS Tourism Sectors

| CAPS Sector | Example Careers | Typical Qualifications & Training |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Transport** | Cabin crew/flight attendant, commercial pilot, airport ground operations, tour coach driver (`PrDP!`), cruise-ship purser, Gautrain station host. | Flight/cabin safety licence, **Code + PrDP (`Professional Driving Permit`)** for passengers, tourism diploma. |
| **2. Hospitality (`Accommodation & Food`)** | Hotel general manager, front-desk receptionist, executive chef, concierge, housekeeping supervisor, sommelier, lodge manager. | National Diploma / Degree in **Hospitality Management** or Culinary Arts; on-the-job apprenticeships (`Micros-Fidelio Opera` skills!). |
| **3. Attractions** | Registered **tourist guide** (`nature/FGASA, culture, adventure — CATHSSETA registered!`), game ranger, museum curator, theme-park ops manager. | **CATHSSETA / FGASA** tourist guide registration + provincial badge, conservation/heritage studies, first-aid licence. |
| **4. Events & Conferences (`MICE`)** | Conference organiser, festival coordinator, wedding/incentive planner, AV/exhibition manager. | Event management / PR diploma, project-budgeting and supplier-negotiation skills. |
| **5. Tourism Services** | Municipal **VIC (`Visitor Information Centre`)** officer, destination marketing manager (`SA Tourism`), tourism lecturer, heritage compliance officer (`SAHRA`). | Tourism/Marketing degree or diploma, strong research, writing, and public-speaking skills. |
| **6. Travel Trade (`Intermediaries`)** | Retail travel consultant (`GDS: Amadeus/Galileo`), inbound tour operator, wholesale package designer, corporate travel manager. | Travel & Tourism diploma + **GDS certification** (`Amadeus/Galileo`), destination & forex mastery. |

## Part 2: Inherent Qualities & Personality Fit

Qualifications open the interview door — **personality keeps you thriving in tourism!**

## Part 3: Entrepreneurship in Tourism (`Creating Your Own Opportunity`)

An **entrepreneur** is someone who **spots an unmet tourist need, takes calculated financial risk, gathers resources, and builds a new business for profit and community impact**.

```steps
title: Key entrepreneurial skills & spotting viable tourism opportunities
step: **1. Spot a real gap (`Don't copy the tenth souvenir stall!`):** Look around your town or province: What do visiting tourists *complain is missing*? (*Example: safe township bicycle food tours, luggage storage near a bus terminal, wheelchair-accessible nature walks, authentic craft-making workshops, reliable local airport shuttles*).
step: **2. Core entrepreneurial qualities:** **Creativity/innovation**, **calculated risk-taking**, **self-discipline & perseverance**, **financial literacy** (`budgeting, cash flow, pricing`), **marketing/networking**, and **customer obsession**.
step: **3. Legal & registration basics in South Africa:** Business registration (`CIPC`), **SA Tourism / TGCSA (`Tourism Grading Council of South Africa`)** star grading for accommodation, **PrDP & operating permits** for shuttles, **CATHSSETA guide registration** for guiding, and public-liability insurance!
step: **4. Start lean & grow sustainably:** Partner with existing B&Bs (`commission/referral swaps`), collect glowing online reviews from day one, and keep strict records of income vs expenses.
```

## Worked example: Matching a Candidate & Evaluating a Start-Up Idea

**Scenario:** *Amahle loves spreadsheets, geography, and comparing flight connections calmly behind the scenes, though she gets exhausted crowds of 200 party guests. Meanwhile, her cousin Sipho loves meeting strangers, speaks three languages (`isiZulu, English, German`), and notices visitors in his town struggle to find authentic guided heritage walks.*

1. *Recommend ONE ideal tourism sector and job for **Amahle** (`3`).* -> **Travel Trade / Tour Operations (`Wholesale Tour Planner or GDS Flight Specialist`)**, because it suits her analytical, detail-focused personality, numerical accuracy, and love of geography/connections without overwhelming large-crowd hosting!
2. *Explain why **Sipho** has strong entrepreneurial potential for a **guided heritage walking tour** (`4`).* -> He spotted a **clear unmet local market gap** (`no existing heritage walks`), holds rare **multi-lingual communication skills (`German` reaches a prime inbound SA market!)**, and enjoys face-to-face people interaction (*must register legally as a **registered tourist guide** with public liability insurance!*).

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Thinking anyone who knows their town can charge tourists as a guide without credentials — in South Africa, guiding for reward legally requires **registered tourist guide certification (`CATHSSETA` / provincial registrar)**!
> - Describing an entrepreneur as *"someone who gets rich easily with zero risk"* — real tourism entrepreneurs face **seasonality, start-up costs, permits, and hard planning** to earn sustainable profits.

## Practice

```questions
Q: Name FOUR of the six CAPS tourism career sectors and give ONE specific job title in each. (4)
A: Any four of: **Transport** (`e.g. flight attendant / shuttle driver`), **Hospitality** (`e.g. executive chef / front-office manager`), **Attractions** (`e.g. nature guide / curator`), **Events & Conferences** (`e.g. conference planner`), **Tourism Services** (`e.g. VIC officer`), **Travel Trade** (`e.g. retail travel consultant`).
---
Q: Distinguish between **academic qualifications** and **inherent qualities** for a tour guide, giving one example of each. (4)
A: **Qualifications** are formal credentials earned through study/training (e.g. **CATHSSETA/FGASA registered guide certificate & First Aid**), whereas **inherent qualities** are personal character traits you bring naturally or cultivate (e.g. **enthusiasm, patience, storytelling confidence, punctuality**).
---
Q: List THREE essential skills or qualities a young South African needs to succeed as a **tourism entrepreneur**. (3)
A: Any three of: **opportunity recognition/creativity**, **financial/budgeting management**, **calculated risk-taking & resilience**, **customer service excellence**, and **marketing/networking skills**.
```
