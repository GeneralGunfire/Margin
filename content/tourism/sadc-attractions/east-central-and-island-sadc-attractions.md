> **What you'll learn:** the main CAPS attractions across **Angola, Democratic Republic of the Congo (`DRC`), Malawi, Mozambique, Tanzania, Madagascar, Mauritius, and Seychelles**, their **World Heritage Site (`*`)** designations, **why each is internationally famous**, and key **activities and travel practicalities**!
>
> **Prerequisites:** Southern African SADC attractions and SADC capitals/gateways (`Lessons 221 & 711`).
>
> **Why this matters:** CAPS Section C exams regularly pull **coastal, mountain, island, and rainforest SADC sites** (`Kilimanjaro, Serengeti, Ngorongoro, Zanzibar, Island of Mozambique, Virunga gorillas, Tsingy de Bemaraha, Lake Malawi, Aldabra/Mauritius`) alongside southern parks!

## The idea in plain language

Beyond South Africa's immediate borders, **SADC stretches north to equatorial rainforests and the Great Rift Valley lakes, and east across warm Indian Ocean islands!** Mastering these northern and island SADC attractions turns a good Tourism essay or map quiz into a guaranteed distinction.

## Part 1: Central & East Mainland SADC (`Angola, DRC, Malawi, Mozambique, Tanzania`)

| Country | Key CAPS Attraction (`* = WHS`) | Why It Is a Top Attraction | Activities & Tourist Info |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Angola** (`Luanda`) | **Kissama (`Quiçama`) National Park** | Coastal-savannah park just ~70 km south of Luanda showcasing post-war **wildlife rewilding (`Operation Noah's Ark`)** where elephants and giraffes were restored near the Atlantic! | 4x4 game drives, Kwanza River boat safaris, birdwatching. |
| **DRC** (`Kinshasa`) | **Virunga (`*`), Kahuzi-Biega (`*`) & Salonga (`*`) National Parks** | Home to rare endangered **mountain gorillas** (`Virunga`), **eastern lowland gorillas** (`Kahuzi-Biega`), active Nyiragongo volcano, and Africa's largest tropical rainforest reserve (**Salonga** — bonobos & forest elephants). | Guided gorilla-trekking permits (`strict hygiene/mask distance rules to protect apes from human illness!`), volcano trekking (`check current travel/security advisories before booking`). |
| **Malawi** (`Lilongwe`) | **Lake Malawi (`*` Lake Malawi National Park)** | *"Lake of Stars"* — deep clear Great Rift Valley freshwater lake housing **more endemic fish species (`hundreds of colourful cichlids`) than any other lake on Earth**! | Freshwater snorkelling, kayaking, sailing, Cape Maclear beach lodges (`low malaria-precaution awareness needed near shore`). |
| **Mozambique** (`Maputo`) | **Bazaruto Archipelago** | Chain of **unspoiled Indian Ocean coral islands** (`Benguerra, Magaruque`) famous for turquoise lagoons, dugongs, whale sharks, and giant coastal sand dunes. | Scuba diving, snorkelling, dhow sailing, deep-sea catch-and-release fishing. |
| **Mozambique** | **Cahora Bassa Dam & Lake Niassa** | **Cahora Bassa:** One of Africa's largest hydroelectric dams/lakes on the Zambezi. **Lake Niassa:** Mozambique's pristine wild eastern shore of Lake Malawi. | Tiger/bream boat fishing (`Cahora Bassa`), remote lakeside kayaking and birding (`Lake Niassa`). |
| **Mozambique** | **Island of Mozambique (`*` WHS)** | Historic tiny coral island linked to the mainland by bridge — former **Portuguese colonial capital (`16th–19th C`)** blending Swahili, Arab, Indian, and European stone architecture. | Walking tours of Fort São Sebastião, Stone Town vs Makuti Town heritage, local Swahili cuisine. |
| **Tanzania** (`Dodoma`) | **Mount Kilimanjaro (`*` WHS)** | **Highest peak in Africa (`5 895 m`)** and **highest freestanding volcanic mountain in the world**, rising from tropical plains straight to permanent alpine glaciers! | Multi-day guided summit treks (`Marangu, Machame routes — requires acclimatisation days for altitude sickness!`). |
| **Tanzania** | **Serengeti National Park (`*` WHS)** | Vast open plains hosting the planet's largest terrestrial mammal migration — **over 1.5 million wildebeest and zebras** moving in the annual Great Migration! | Hot-air ballooning at dawn, Big Five & cheetah safaris (`best migration timing varies by month`). |
| **Tanzania** | **Ngorongoro Conservation Area (`*` WHS)** | World's largest **unbroken, unflooded volcanic caldera (`crater`)** forming a natural wildlife sanctuary (`plus Olduvai Gorge — famous "Cradle of Humankind" early human fossils!`). | Crater-floor 4x4 game drives, Maasai cultural visits. |
| **Tanzania** | **Stone Town of Zanzibar (`*` WHS)** | Historic Indian Ocean **spice-island port** preserving coral-stone alleys, carved wooden Zanzibari doors, Sultan's palaces, and spice farms (`cloves, nutmeg, cinnamon`). | Walking heritage & Forodhani night-market tours, spice-farm visits, Indian Ocean beach stays. |

## Part 2: Indian Ocean Island SADC Members (`Madagascar, Mauritius, Seychelles, Comoros`)

| Island Nation | Key CAPS Attraction (`* = WHS`) | Why It Is a Top Attraction | Activities & Tourist Info |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Madagascar** (`Antananarivo`) | **Tsingy de Bemaraha (`*` WHS)** | Otherworldly forest of **razor-sharp vertical limestone pinnacles (`tsingy` = *"where one cannot walk barefoot"*)**, home to endemic **lemurs** found nowhere else on Earth! | Suspension-bridge canopy walks, canyon boat trips, wildlife photography (`~80–90% of Madagascar wildlife is endemic!`). |
| **Madagascar** | **Royal Hill of Ambohimanga (`*` WHS)** | Sacred fortified **16th–19th century Merina royal citadel**, burial site, and sacred forest symbolising Malagasy cultural identity (`plus famous Avenue of the Baobabs!`). | Guided cultural history walk, heritage museum visit. |
| **Mauritius** (`Port Louis`) | **Beaches, Coral Lagoons & Cultural Landscape (`Le Morne *`, Black River Gorges)** | Luxury **honeymoon & resort island** encircled by calm coral-reef lagoons, emerald volcanic peaks, **Black River Gorges** rainforest, multi-cultural heritage (`Indian, Creole, French, Chinese`), and **Le Morne Brabant (`*`)** cliff sanctuary for escaped enslaved people. | Reef snorkelling/diving, catamaran cruises, hiking Black River Gorges, cultural food trails. |
| **Seychelles** (`Victoria`) | **Granite Beaches, Vallée de Mai (`*`) & Aldabra Atoll (`*`)** | Exclusive archipelago with iconic pink-granite boulders (`Anse Source d'Argent`), **Vallée de Mai** prehistoric palm forest growing the giant **coco de mer** double-nut, and **Aldabra** (`giant tortoises`!). | Eco-island hopping, diving, tortoise/palm sanctuary walks. |
| **Comoros** (`Moroni`) | **Mount Karthala & Moheli Marine Park** *(Newest SADC member)* | *"Perfume Islands"* (`ylang-ylang blossoms`), active Mount Karthala volcano on Grande Comore, sea-turtle nesting beaches. | Volcano rim treks, sea-turtle & reef eco-snorkelling. |

## Worked example: Matching International Client Profiles to East/Island SADC Sites

Recommend the **best SADC country AND specific attraction** for each brief (`and explain why`):
1. *"An adventure couple wants to trek the highest freestanding volcano in the world before watching the Great Migration."* -> **Tanzania: Mount Kilimanjaro (`*`)** (`5 895 m highest freestanding mountain/Africa's tallest peak`) + **Serengeti National Park (`*`)** (`annual wildebeest/zebra Great Migration`).
2. *"A botanist and wildlife photographer want a destination where nearly 90% of species—especially lemurs—are endemic, featuring dramatic limestone spires."* -> **Madagascar: Tsingy de Bemaraha (`*`)** (`sharp limestone pinnacles + endemic lemurs`).
3. *"A marine biologist wants crystal-clear freshwater snorkelling among hundreds of endemic cichlid fish inside the Rift Valley."* -> **Malawi: Lake Malawi (`*` National Park)**.

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Placing **Mount Kilimanjaro** or **Serengeti** in Kenya (`Kenya is NOT a SADC member! Both Kilimanjaro and Serengeti sit inside **Tanzania**, which IS a SADC member!`).
> - Confusing **Lake Malawi / Lake Niassa** (same Rift Valley lake bordered by Malawi and Mozambique!) with **Lake Kariba** (`man-made dam on the Zambezi River`).
> - Forgetting to check **altitude/health/safety conditions** (`e.g. gorilla permits & security checks in DRC; acclimatisation days on Kilimanjaro; malaria prophylaxis in tropical coastal/lake parks`).

## Practice

```questions
Q: Name the country AND ONE key feature for each CAPS World Heritage attraction: *(a)* **Tsingy de Bemaraha**; *(b)* **Island of Mozambique**; *(c)* **Ngorongoro Conservation Area**. (6)
A: *(a)* **Madagascar** — jagged limestone needle formations hosting endemic lemurs. *(b)* **Mozambique** — historic fortified coral-stone port blending Swahili, Arab, Indian, and Portuguese heritage. *(c)* **Tanzania** — world's largest unbroken unflooded volcanic caldera teeming with resident wildlife (`next to Olduvai Gorge`).
---
Q: Why are **gorilla-trekking visitors in DRC (`Virunga / Kahuzi-Biega`)** required to obey strict health and group-size rules? (2)
A: Mountain and eastern lowland gorillas share ~98% of human DNA and can catch **fatal human respiratory viruses (`colds, flu`)** easily; small guided groups and masks/distance rules protect endangered populations.
---
Q: Explain why **Mauritius** and **Seychelles** attract very different spending profiles compared to budget backpacker overland routes. (4)
A: Both island nations focus heavily on **fly-in resort, honeymoon, marine-eco, and boutique luxury tourism** protected by strict lagoon/island conservation limits (`higher airfares and resort rates per night`), yielding high spend per tourist while guarding fragile coral and palm ecosystems.
```
