>
> **Prerequisites:** Grade 10 domestic vs international tourism and tourist profiles.
>
> **Why this matters:** Section E (`40 marks`) tests **why domestic tourism is South Africa's backbone**, and regularly asks you to **match a real family/couple profile to the right CAPS domestic segment AND product offering**!

## The idea in plain language

International tourists bring valuable foreign currency — **but** international arrivals drop fast whenever airlines strike, global recessions hit, or health crises close borders!

**Domestic tourism** (`South Africans travelling inside South Africa for leisure, VFR, or business`) is the industry's **shock absorber**: it keeps guesthouses, restaurants, guides, and rural attractions open every month of the year! The **Domestic Tourism Growth Strategy (`DTGS`)** is government's master plan (`"Sho't Left"` campaigns, provincial marketing, affordable packages) to grow local travel fairly across all nine provinces.

## Part 1: Objectives of the DTGS & How South Africa Meets Them

### Core DTGS Objectives (`CAPS`)
1. **Increase domestic tourism revenue (`GDP contribution`)** and job creation.
2. **Increase domestic holiday volumes (`number of trips and bed-nights`)** — moving South Africans beyond only travelling to visit friends and relatives (`VFR`).
3. **Foster transformation and inclusivity** — making holidays accessible, affordable, and welcoming to previously disadvantaged South Africans (`domestic travel culture`).
4. **Promote sustainable growth across all nine provinces.**

```steps
title: Six practical ways South Africa meets DTGS objectives
step: **1. Increase expenditure per trip:** Encourage VFR travellers staying with relatives to also book **day tours, restaurants, museums, and theme parks** nearby!
step: **2. Reduce seasonality:** Offer **Sho't Left winter/off-peak specials** (`pensioner discounts, mid-week school-term deals`) so resorts survive outside December and Easter peaks.
step: **3. Improve geographic spread:** Market lesser-visited provinces (**Northern Cape, Free State, North West, Limpopo, Eastern Cape**) instead of overcrowding only Cape Town and Kruger.
step: **4. Increase trip volumes:** Run awareness campaigns showing local families affordable **long-weekend getaways** inside their own province.
step: **5. Leverage festivals & events:** Use music festivals, marathons (`Comrades, Two Oceans`), cultural heritage gatherings, and expos to pull domestic visitors into host towns.
step: **6. Improve safety & service perception:** Visible tourist policing, cleaner signage, reliable road/rail links, and friendly hospitality encourage families to explore confidently.
```

## Part 2: Domestic Market Segments & Product Offerings (`CAPS`)

*Curriculum note:* **CAPS** details **seven distinct domestic consumer segments** alongside **seven tourism product offerings**; recent **DBEs ATP updates** often condense consumer profiles into **five priority clusters** for streamlined campaigns (`so know the core CAPS profiles clearly!`).

| CAPS Domestic Segment | Typical Traveller Profile | Best-Suited Product Offerings (`From the 7 CAPS Products`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Young and Upcoming** | Young working adults (`~18–34`), mobile, aspirational, trend-driven, active on social media, enjoy festivals and short weekend breaks. | **City breaks**, **Event wonders** (`concerts, sports`), **Coastal getaways** |
| **2. Independent Young Couples & Families** | Early-career couples or young parents seeking affordable, fun, safe outdoor breaks without luxury hotel prices. | **Bush retreats**, **Coastal getaways**, **Countryside meanders** |
| **3. Striving Families** | Working parents balancing tight budgets and school holidays; heavily value family-friendly package deals, self-catering, and VFR add-ons. | **Coastal getaways**, **Mountain escapes**, **Cultural discoveries** |
| **4. Well-off Homely Couples** | Established mid-life professionals/couples with higher disposable income who value comfort, fine food, scenery, and peaceful heritage towns. | **Countryside meanders** (`wine/art routes`), **Mountain escapes**, **Bush retreats** |
| **5. Home-Based Low-Income Couples** | Cost-sensitive households where travel is mainly VFR or religious/community gatherings; need very low-cost day excursions and transport. | **Cultural discoveries**, local **Event wonders** / day outings |
| **6. Older Families with Basic Needs** | Mature multi-generational households prioritising affordability, safety, rest, and accessible facilities for elders. | **Countryside meanders**, **Coastal getaways** (`off-peak`) |
| **7. Golden Active Couples** | Active retirees (`55+`) with free time **outside school holidays** and pension/savings — goldmine for curing off-peak seasonality! | **Mountain escapes**, **Bush retreats**, **Cultural discoveries**, **Countryside meanders** |

### The Seven Domestic Product Offerings (`Know All 7!`)
1. **City breaks** (`urban nightlife, shopping, museums, restaurants — e.g. Johannesburg, Cape Town, Durban`)
2. **Mountain escapes** (`hiking, crisp air, lodges — e.g. Drakensberg, Cederberg, Magaliesberg`)
3. **Coastal getaways** (`beaches, water sports, family resorts — e.g. Dolphin Coast, Wild Coast, Garden Route`)
4. **Bush retreats** (`safari lodges, Big Five game drives — e.g. Kruger, Pilanesberg, Addo`)
5. **Countryside meanders** (`scenic small towns, farm stalls, wine & art routes — e.g. Midlands Meander, Route 62`)
6. **Cultural discoveries** (`heritage villages, rock art, liberation history, festivals`)
7. **Event wonders** (`music festivals, sports events, food/arts expos — e.g. Makhanda National Arts Festival, Cape Town Jazz Festival`)

## Worked example: Matching a Tourist Brief to Segment & Product

**Scenario:** *Thabo and Nomsa (62 and 60, retired teachers) love hiking, birdwatching, and quiet scenic drives in April (`outside school holidays`), staying at clean mid-range country lodges.*

1. *Identify their CAPS domestic market segment (`2`).* -> **Golden Active Couples**.
2. *Recommend TWO suitable CAPS domestic product offerings (`2`).* -> **Mountain escapes** (hiking/scenery) and **Countryside meanders** (quiet scenic drives and country lodges).
3. *Explain ONE DTGS goal this couple helps South Africa achieve (`2`).* -> **Reducing seasonality**, because retirees travel happily during quiet **off-peak April weeks**, keeping rural lodges busy and staff employed outside school holidays!

```tryit
title: DTGS & Product Matching Drill
prompt: Solve both questions:
1. Why is simply getting more South Africans to visit relatives (**VFR**) NOT enough for the DTGS unless spending behaviour changes?
2. Which CAPS product offering fits a 24-year-old group travelling from Pretoria to Durban for a weekend beach marathon and music concert?
hint: Think **spending on commercial tourism products** and **Event wonders / City breaks / Coastal getaways**.
answer: 1. Pure VFR guests often sleep and eat at relatives' homes without paying hotels or restaurants — so DTGS encourages VFR tourists to also buy **paid local attractions, tours, and dining** (*increasing expenditure*). 2. **Event wonders** (paired with **Coastal getaways** or **City breaks**).
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Mixing up the **DTGS objectives/ways to meet them** (*reducing seasonality, geographic spread, increasing spend*) with the **7 market segments** (*Golden Active Couples, Striving Families, etc.*).
> - Naming random activities (*"going to a mall"*) instead of the **exact 7 CAPS product offering titles** (*City breaks, Mountain escapes, Coastal getaways, Bush retreats, Countryside meanders, Cultural discoveries, Event wonders*).

## Practice

```questions
Q: State THREE ways South Africa can meet the objectives of the **Domestic Tourism Growth Strategy (`DTGS`)**. (6)
A: 1. **Reduce seasonality** by promoting discounted off-peak holiday deals (`e.g. Sho't Left specials`). 2. **Improve geographic spread** by marketing lesser-known rural provinces alongside major cities. 3. **Encourage VFR travellers to spend more** on local attractions, restaurants, and events during family visits.
---
Q: Why are **Golden Active Couples** especially valuable to small rural guest houses? (2)
A: Because retired couples have **flexible schedules and disposable income** to travel **mid-week and during off-peak seasons**, smoothing out quiet months when families cannot travel.
---
Q: Name the CAPS domestic product offering described by each clue: *(a)* scenic farm-stall and craft route through small towns; *(b)* liberation museums and traditional music festivals. (2)
A: *(a)* **Countryside meanders**; *(b)* **Cultural discoveries** (or *Event wonders* for major festival tourism).
```
