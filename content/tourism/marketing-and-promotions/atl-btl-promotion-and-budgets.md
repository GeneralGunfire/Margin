> **What you'll learn:** the difference between **Above-the-Line (`ATL`)** and **Below-the-Line (`BTL`)** promotional techniques (`with exact CAPS examples`), how to **match promotional techniques to a target market**, and the core **components of a tourism marketing budget**.
>
> **Prerequisites:** Grade 10 marketing basics and Domestic Tourism market segments.
>
> **Why this matters:** Section C (`50 marks`) tests marketing alongside attractions and heritage — especially classifying **ATL vs BTL** correctly (`beware personal selling & direct mail traps!`) and checking whether a small tourism business budgeted every cost before launching a campaign!

## The idea in plain language

Imagine you own a new eco-lodge in the Cederberg. How do you tell potential guests you exist?
- **Above-the-Line (`ATL`) marketing** is a **loudspeaker aimed at the broad mass public** (`television adverts, radio spots, national newspapers, magazines, highway billboards, cinema ads, broad digital mass media`). Great for brand awareness, **expensive**, hard to target narrowly.
- **Below-the-Line (`BTL`) marketing** is a **targeted conversation aimed directly at specific buyers** (`brochures, flyers, trade expos like Tourism Indaba, direct email/SMS newsletters, personal selling, point-of-sale displays, sponsorships, loyalty coupons`). **Targeted, measurable**, often lower cost per genuine lead!

## Part 1: Above-the-Line (`ATL`) vs Below-the-Line (`BTL`) Techniques

```compare
title: ATL (Mass Media) vs BTL (Direct & Targeted)
left: Above-the-Line (`ATL`) Examples
- **Television & Radio commercials:** Huge national/regional reach (`Sho't Left` TV ads before holidays).
- **Print mass media:** National/regional **newspapers & lifestyle/travel magazines** (`Getaway, Weg!`).
- **Outdoor mass advertising:** **Billboards** on highways (`N1/N2/N3`), airport concourse banners, branded city buses.
- **Cinema & broad online display banners:** High-visibility visual storytelling before movies or across major news portals.
right: Below-the-Line (`BTL`) Examples
- **Sales promotions:** Discount vouchers, *"stay 3 nights pay for 2"* winter specials, competitions, loyalty cards.
- **Direct marketing:** Targeted email newsletters, WhatsApp/SMS offers, direct mail to past guests.
- **Personal selling:** Travel agents or tour reps presenting tailored packages face-to-face to corporate/school clients!
- **Printed collateral & displays:** Brochures at tourist info centres (`VICs`), flyers, pull-up banners at **trade shows (`Africa's Travel Indaba, WTM Africa`)**, social-media influencer partnerships, sponsorships.
```

## Part 2: Matching Promotional Techniques to the Target Market

Never throw a random advert at everyone — match the **channel** to **where your target customer actually pays attention**:

| Target Customer | Best Promotional Choice (`ATL / BTL`) | Why It Works |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **International Tour Operators (`Trade Buyers`)** | **BTL:** Trade exhibitions (*Africa's Travel Indaba in Durban, WTM Africa*), **personal selling**, digital agent brochures. | B2B buyers need contract rates, group capacity, and face-to-face relationship trust — NOT a radio jingle! |
| **Young and Upcoming (`18–34`) Domestic Travellers** | **BTL + Digital:** Short-form social-media reels, influencer collaborations, festival sponsorships, mobile discount codes. | Youth scroll smartphones constantly and respond to visual peer recommendations and instant mobile booking links. |
| **Golden Active Couples (`55+ Retirees`)** | **ATL + BTL:** Travel/gardening **print magazines**, morning radio, and **email specials** for quiet mid-week off-peak stays. | High trust in reputable print magazines/radio plus strong engagement with discounted mid-week newsletters. |
| **Local Passing Road-Trippers** | **ATL + BTL:** Clear **highway/route billboards** (`20 km ahead`) + flyers at nearby fuel stops and Visitor Information Centres (`VICs`). | Captures spontaneous motorists already driving near the attraction. |

## Part 3: Components of a Tourism Marketing Budget (`CAPS`)

A realistic marketing plan fails if costs are guessed or forgotten. Every tourism marketing budget accounts for:

```steps
title: Five cost components inside a tourism marketing budget
step: **1. Market Research & Strategy Costs:** Surveys, customer feedback tools, competitor benchmarking before spending on ads.
step: **2. Creative & Production Costs (`Design`):** Graphic designer fees, copywriting, professional photography/video shoots, website updates.
step: **3. Printing & Promotional Material Costs:** Printing glossy brochures, flyers, business cards, branded banners, conference merchandise.
step: **4. Media Buying & Distribution Costs:** Paying for radio airtime, billboard rental, magazine ad space, boosted social ads, or trade-show stall rental + travel/accommodation for staff!
step: **5. Monitoring, Contingencies & Evaluation:** Tracking promo-code bookings (`ROI`) plus a **5–10% contingency reserve** for unexpected price increases.
```

## Worked example: Choosing & Budgeting Promotion for a New Township Food Tour

**Scenario:** *Lindiwe launches a weekend **Soweto Culinary & Heritage Walking Tour (`R450 pp`)** aimed at young Johannesburg professionals and international hostel guests (`start-up budget: R12 000`).*

1. *Should Lindiwe buy a national prime-time TV commercial (`ATL`)? Explain (`2`).* -> **No!** National TV far exceeds her R12 000 budget and wastes money reaching audiences outside Gauteng; **targeted BTL (`flyers at Sandton/Soweto hostels, social media reels, and concierge partnerships`)** reaches her exact buyers affordably!
2. *Classify TWO promotional tools for her launch as ATL or BTL (`4`).* ->
   - Placing branded **flyers & QR-code posters** at backpacker hostels = **BTL** (targeted direct promotion).
   - Advertising in a regional **Gauteng weekend lifestyle newspaper** = **ATL** (mass print media).
3. *Identify ONE budget item she might forget when hiring a photographer (`2`).* -> **Production/editing costs or model/food-staging expenses** (`beyond just printing flyers!`).

```tryit
title: ATL vs BTL Classification Check
prompt: Classify each technique as **Above-the-Line (`ATL`)** or **Below-the-Line (`BTL`)**:
1. A tour operator exhibits its 2027 safari packages at an **international travel trade expo** and meets agents one-on-one.
2. South African Airways runs a **national radio and television campaign** during December holidays.

```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Classifying **personal selling** or **exhibiting at a travel expo** as *Above-the-Line* — both target specific prospects directly, making them **Below-the-Line (`BTL`)**!
> - Forgetting **design/photography/production** OR **staff travel/stall costs** in a marketing budget (`only listing the printing cost of brochures`).
> - Recommending the **same promotion** (`e.g. "put it on TikTok"`) for *every* market segment without checking whether the target customer actually uses that channel!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Define **Above-the-Line (`ATL`)** and **Below-the-Line (`BTL`)** marketing, giving TWO tourism examples of each. (6)
A: **ATL** uses **mass media** to reach a broad public audience and build general awareness (e.g. **TV/radio commercials, highway billboards, national newspapers/magazines**). **BTL** uses **targeted, direct techniques** aimed at specific prospective customers (e.g. **brochures/flyers, travel trade expos, direct email newsletters, personal selling, discount coupons**).
---
Q: Give TWO reasons why a small rural B&B benefits more from **BTL marketing** than a national TV campaign. (4)
A: 1. **Lower cost / higher affordability** on a limited start-up budget. 2. **Better targeting** — money reaches travellers genuinely interested in that region (`via VIC brochures, travel agents, or local social ads`) instead of paying to advertise to non-travellers countrywide.
---
Q: List FOUR distinct cost categories that belong in a tourism **marketing budget**. (4)
A: Any four of: **market research**, **creative design/photography (`production`)**, **printing & promotional materials**, **media placement / expo stall & staff travel fees**, and **monitoring / contingency allowance**.
```
