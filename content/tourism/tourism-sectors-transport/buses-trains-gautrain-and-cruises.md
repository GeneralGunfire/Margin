>
> **Prerequisites:** Grade 10 modes of land and water transport.
>
> **Why this matters:** CAPS Section D compares **different transport experiences for specific tourist budgets** — especially why a commuter train (`Metrorail`) is NOT a luxury tourist attraction, and why **Gautrain** and **cruise ports (Durban & Cape Town)** solve very different travel needs!

## The idea in plain language

Transport is never just *"getting from A to B"* in Tourism! Sometimes transport is **purely functional** (getting safely from OR Tambo to Sandton in 15 minutes on the **Gautrain**); other times transport **IS the holiday itself** (spending three nights dining in 5-star suites aboard **The Blue Train** or sailing the Indian Ocean on a **cruise liner**!).

## Part 1: The Tourism Bus Industry

| Bus / Coach Type | Who Uses It & Features |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Long-Distance Scheduled Coaches** (`e.g. Intercape, Translux/Eldo`) | Connect major SA cities and SADC capitals on **fixed published timetables**; reclining seats, air-conditioning, reading lights, onboard toilets, and under-floor luggage holds. |
| **Chartered Luxury Tour Coaches** | Hired by tour operators for **organised multi-day group tours** (`Garden Route, Kruger`) with PA microphone systems for tour guides, large panoramic windows, and onboard fridge/Wi-Fi. |
| **Hop-On, Hop-Off Sightseeing Buses** (`e.g. City Sightseeing Cape Town / Johannesburg`) | Open-top double-decker buses looping past top city attractions; multi-language audio commentary lets tourists disembark at stops and catch a later bus (`24h/48h pass`). |
| **Backpacker Coaches** (`e.g. Baz Bus`) | Door-to-door hostel-to-hostel passes along scenic routes (`Cape Town <-> Port Elizabeth <-> Durban`), ideal for independent youth travellers. |
| **Airport & Hotel Shuttles / Minibuses** | Small-group transfers (`10–22 seaters`) between airports, hotels, and conference venues. |

- **Reading a Bus Schedule:** Always check **route direction**, **departure/arrival time (`24h`)**, **pick-up/drop-off points** (`do NOT wait at the wrong depot!`), **days of operation**, and **luggage weight limit** (`commonly 20 kg`).

## Part 2: Rail Tourism — Commuter Trains vs Luxury Tourist Trains

```compare
title: Do NOT Confuse Commuter Rail with Tourist Trains!
left: Commuter Rail (`Metrorail`)
- **Primary purpose:** Affordable **daily urban transport** for workers, learners, and residents (`no luxury suites, dining cars, or guided excursions`).
- Limited scenic focus; runs dense suburban stop patterns during peak hours.
right: Scenic & Luxury Tourist Trains
- **The Blue Train:** World-famous 5-star *"hotel-on-wheels"* (`Pretoria <-> Cape Town` + charters) with private en-suite suites, butler service, fine dining, gold-tinted panoramic lounges.
- **Rovos Rail (*Pride of Africa*):** Edwardian-style vintage luxury (`Pretoria -> Cape Town / Victoria Falls / Durban / Dar es Salaam`) with open-air observation car!
- **Shongololo Express:** Guided rail-cruise (*hotel-on-rails* moving mainly overnight so passengers explore attractions by day).
- **Premier Classe:** Mid-to-upper tourist comfort with dining car, lounge, and sleeper cabins at a lower price point than Blue Train/Rovos.
```

- **Key Rail Terms:** **Compartment** (`4-berth sleeper cabin`), **coupé** (`2-berth smaller sleeper cabin`), **dining car**, **observation/lounge car**, **gauge** (`track width — SA uses Cape Gauge, 1 067 mm`), **off-train excursion**.

## Part 3: The Gautrain (`High-Speed Rapid Rail in Gauteng`)

Built ahead of 2010 to transform Gauteng mobility, the **Gautrain** operates at speeds up to **160 km/h** across two main lines intersecting at **Marlboro** (`plus dedicated feeder buses`):

```steps
title: Gautrain routes, technology & tourism benefits
step: **1. Airport Link & North–South Spine:** Connects **OR Tambo International Airport** directly to **Sandton** (`~15 minutes!`), plus **Johannesburg Park Station — Rosebank — Sandton — Marlboro — Midrand — Centurion — Pretoria — Hatfield** (and **Rhodesfield** near OR Tambo).
step: **2. Smart Technology:** Contactless **Gautrain Gold Card** / bank-card tap-and-go fare gates, dedicated high-security stations with CCTV, biometric/ticket access control, digital next-train displays, and GPS-tracked **Gautrain buses**.
step: **3. Tourism Benefits:** Eliminates highway gridlock for **business/MICE tourists** flying into OR Tambo for Sandton/Pretoria meetings; offers safe, punctual airport transfers; cuts vehicle carbon emissions!
```

## Part 4: The Luxury Cruise Liner Industry

South African ports (**Cape Town** and **Durban** cruise terminals, plus calls at **Gqeberha / Walvis Bay / Maputo / Portuguese Island**) welcome both domestic regional cruises and round-the-world vessels:
- **Key Cruise Terminology:**
  - **Embarkation:** Boarding the ship at the start of the cruise (`check-in, passport/luggage security at passenger terminal`). **Disembarkation:** Leaving the ship at the end of the cruise.
  - **Port of call:** An intermediate harbour stop where passengers go ashore for **shore excursions**.
  - **Decks:** Numbered vertical floors on the ship (`e.g. Pool Deck, Promenade Deck`).
  - **Cabins (`Staterooms`):** **Inside cabin** (*cheapest, no window*), **Oceanview/Outside cabin** (*porthole/window*), **Balcony cabin** (*private outdoor veranda*), **Suite** (*largest living area + VIP perks*).
  - **Facilities:** Restaurants & 24-hour buffets, theatre shows, swimming pools, spas, gyms, casinos, kids' clubs, shops, and medical clinics (`all-inclusive meals usually included; drinks/excursions/gratuities often extra`).

## Worked example: Matching Transport to Tourist Profiles

Match each client to the best South African transport service and give **one reason**:
1. *A German conference delegate landing at OR Tambo at `16:15` needing to reach a Sandton hotel by `17:15` during rush hour.* -> **Gautrain** (`15-minute high-speed Airport Link bypasses N1/R24 highway traffic`).
2. *Retired wealthy British couple wanting a 3-night slow heritage rail journey from Pretoria to Victoria Falls.* -> **Rovos Rail** (`Edwardian luxury suites, observation car, off-train excursions, Pretoria–Victoria Falls route`).
3. *Solo 20-year-old backpacker travelling along the coast between hostels in Cape Town, Mossel Bay, and Durban.* -> **Baz Bus** (`hop-on hop-off hostel-to-hostel network tailored for budget youth travellers`).

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Listing **Metrorail** as a luxury tourist attraction — Metrorail is **urban commuter rail**, whereas **Blue Train, Rovos Rail, Shongololo Express, and Premier Classe** are tourist trains.
> - Saying Gautrain runs **to Cape Town or Durban** — Gautrain operates **only within Gauteng** (`Johannesburg – Sandton – OR Tambo – Midrand – Pretoria – Hatfield`).
> - Thinking cruise ticket prices cover **absolutely everything** — shore excursions, speciality dining, bar drinks, Wi-Fi, and port charges/gratuities often cost extra unless an upgraded package is bought!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Explain TWO benefits of the **Gautrain** specifically for international business tourists arriving at OR Tambo International Airport. (4)
A: 1. The **15-minute Airport Link** avoids Johannesburg peak-hour highway congestion between OR Tambo and Sandton. 2. Clean, CCTV-monitored stations and integrated feeder buses offer reliable, safe door-to-venue connections to Johannesburg and Pretoria business districts.
---
Q: Distinguish between a **hop-on, hop-off sightseeing bus** and a **long-distance scheduled coach**. (4)
A: A **hop-on, hop-off sightseeing bus** loops past major tourist attractions inside a city with onboard audio commentary so tourists can get off and rejoin later on one pass. A **long-distance scheduled coach** travels directly between different cities or provinces on fixed departure/arrival times primarily for point-to-point transit.
---
Q: Briefly explain the cruise terms **embarkation**, **port of call**, and **shore excursion**. (6)
A: **Embarkation** is the check-in and boarding process at the start terminal of a cruise. A **port of call** is a scheduled harbour along the route where the ship docks temporarily. A **shore excursion** is an organised sightseeing or activity tour passengers take on land at a port of call.
```
