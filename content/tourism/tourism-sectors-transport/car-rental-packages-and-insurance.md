> **What you'll learn:** the **conditions for renting a car** (*age, valid licence, creditworthiness*), **rental packages** (*unlimited km vs limited km*), the five essential CAPS **insurance waivers (`CDW, TLW, SCDW, STLW, PAI`)**, **additional and incidental costs** (*airport surcharge, tourism levy, fuel, cross-border fee, drop-off fee, fine admin*), and **payment methods**.
>
> **Prerequisites:** air-travel arrivals at gateways and basic percentage/currency arithmetic.
>
> **Why this matters:** Every year, CAPS Section D (`and sometimes Section B`) gives a **Car Rental Quote / Rate Sheet** asking you to **explain `CDW/SCDW/PAI`**, identify hidden **incidental fees**, and **calculate the exact total rental cost**!

## The idea in plain language

When you rent a car at Cape Town International for R650/day, **R650/day is NEVER the final bill!**
Think of car rental pricing in **three layers**:
1. **Eligibility:** Can the company legally trust you with a R350 000 vehicle? (`Age, driver's licence, credit card deposit`).
2. **Core Daily Package + Waivers:** Base daily rate + distance allowance + insurance waivers reducing your financial risk if the car is damaged or stolen.
3. **Additional & Incidental Costs:** Extras you choose (`GPS, baby seat, second driver, cross-border letter`) plus event-based/location charges (`airport surcharge, fuel refilling, one-way drop-off fee`).

## Part 1: Conditions for Renting a Vehicle (`Who Qualifies?`)

- **Minimum Age:** Usually **21 or 23 years** (some luxury vehicles require **25+**; drivers aged 18–24 often pay a **Young Driver Surcharge**).
- **Valid Unendorsed Driver's Licence:** Original physical card (`held for at least 1–2 years`). Foreign visitors need a valid English-language licence or an **International Driving Permit (`IDP`)** alongside their home licence.
- **Creditworthiness & Security Hold:** Major companies (`Avis, Budget, Europcar, Hertz, First Car Rental, Tempest`) require a **valid credit card in the main driver's name** to place an **authorisation hold (`excess deposit`)**. Debit/cash renters face strict limits or are refused at many counters.
- **Signed Rental Agreement:** Legal contract confirming return date/time (`late returns often trigger an extra full day after a short grace period`), approved drivers, and prohibited roads (`e.g. no unpermitted gravel/4x4 trails in a standard sedan`).

## Part 2: Packages & Payment Options

- **Unlimited Kilometres Package:** Higher daily rate, **zero per-km charge** — best for **long road trips** (`e.g. Garden Route or Cape Town -> Namaqualand`).
- **Limited Kilometres Package:** Lower daily rate with **capped free km/day** (`e.g. 100 km/day or 200 km/day`), plus an **excess charge per extra kilometre** (`e.g. R4.50/km`) — cheaper for **short city business trips**!
- **Payment Options:** Credit card (*standard*), EFT/corporate voucher (*pre-approved travel-agency accounts*), rare cash/debit deposits where permitted.

## Part 3: Insurance Waivers — Do NOT Confuse Standard vs Super!

A **waiver** is NOT ordinary insurance paying you cash — it means the rental company **waives (gives up) its right to charge you the full replacement cost of the car**, capping your liability at an **excess (`deductible`)**!

| Code | Full Name (`CAPS Essential`) | What It Covers & Excess Rule |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **CDW** | **Collision Damage Waiver** | Reduces renter's liability if the vehicle is damaged in a collision (`you STILL pay the standard excess, e.g. R12 000`). |
| **SCDW** | **Super Collision Damage Waiver** | Optional daily upgrade that **sharply reduces or removes (`zero excess`)** your collision excess! |
| **TLW** | **Theft Loss Waiver** | Caps your liability if the vehicle is stolen or hijacked (`you STILL pay the standard theft excess`). |
| **STLW** | **Super Theft Loss Waiver** | Optional upgrade that **sharply reduces or eliminates** your theft excess. |
| **PAI** | **Personal Accident Insurance** | Optional personal cover for **medical expenses, injury, or accidental death** of the driver and passengers inside the rental car! |

- **Watch the Exclusions!** Standard CDW/TLW often exclude **windscreens, tyres, undercarriage, keys, or reckless driving (`drunk driving / speeding / unauthorised driver`)** unless extra **Tyre & Windscreen Waiver** is added and contract rules are obeyed!

## Part 4: Additional & Incidental Costs (`Hidden Budget Killers!`)

```steps
title: Checklist of extra & incidental charges in a rental quote
step: **1. Airport Surcharge & Contract Fee:** Picking up at an ACSA airport adds a percentage or flat **airport concession surcharge**, plus a one-off **contract/admin fee** per rental.
step: **2. Additional Driver Fee:** Charged per extra named driver (`if an unnamed person crashes the car, ALL waivers become void!`).
step: **3. One-Way Drop-Off Fee:** Renting in Johannesburg and dropping off in Cape Town incurs a hefty relocation fee.
step: **4. Equipment & Cross-Border Permit:** Daily hire for **child car seats, GPS, roof racks**, or a mandatory letter/fee to cross into **Namibia, Botswana, Eswatini, or Lesotho**.
step: **5. Fuel Policy & Incidental Fines:** Most rentals use **Full-to-Full**; returning below full incurs fuel + steep **refuelling service fee**. Speeding/e-toll tickets incur a **traffic fine administration fee** charged straight to your card!
```

## Worked example: Calculating a Full Car Rental Cost (`Exam Style`)

A tourist rents a Group B hatchback from OR Tambo International for **4 days** with an **Unlimited km** package under these quoted rates:
- Base rental (`including CDW/TLW`): `R520/day`
- Optional upgrades chosen: `SCDW` at `R110/day`, `PAI` at `R45/day`, `Child seat` at `R80/day`
- One-off fees: Contract fee `R95`, Airport surcharge `R150`, One-way drop-off fee (Cape Town) `R1 150`
- Fuel used on trip (paid at petrol station before return): `R1 420`

1. **Total Daily Charges (`4 days`):**
   `Base (520) + SCDW (110) + PAI (45) + Child seat (80)` = `R755/day`
   `4 × R755` = **`R3 020.00`**
2. **One-Off Rental Fees:**
   `R95 + R150 + R1 150` = **`R1 395.00`**
3. **Total Paid to Car Rental Company:**
   `R3 020.00 + R1 395.00` = **`R4 415.00`**
4. **Total Motoring Cost Including Petrol (`incidental cost`):**
   `R4 415.00 + R1 420.00` = **`R5 835.00`**

```tryit
title: Limited vs Unlimited Package & Waiver Check
prompt: Solve both short problems:
1. A business traveller rents a car for **2 days** to drive **480 km total**. Option A is **R450/day** (`150 free km/day`, then `R4.00/extra km`). Option B is **R680/day** (`Unlimited km`). Which option is cheaper, and by how much?
2. A tourist picks **CDW** (`not SCDW`) and damages the car's bumper (repair cost R18 000; standard excess R9 500). How much does the tourist pay out of pocket?
hint: Free km for 2 days in Option A = `2 × 150 = 300 km`! Excess = max you pay for a covered claim.
answer: 1. **Option A** = `(2 × 450) + (480 - 300) × 4.00 = 900 + 720 = R1 620`; **Option B** = `2 × 680 = R1 360`. **Option B is cheaper by R260!** 2. **R9 500** (`the excess`).
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Multiplying **one-off fees** (`contract fee, airport surcharge, drop-off fee`) by the number of days — read carefully which items are **per day** vs **per rental**!
> - Confusing **CDW** (`car damage`) with **PAI** (`people/medical injury`).
> - Believing **SCDW** covers you even if an **unlisted friend** drives or you drive under the influence — breaching contract rules **voids all waivers**!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Explain the difference between **CDW** and **SCDW** in a car rental contract. (4)
A: **CDW (Collision Damage Waiver)** caps the renter's liability after an accident at a fixed standard **excess**, whereas **SCDW (Super Collision Damage Waiver)** is an extra daily upgrade that **significantly reduces or eliminates (`zero`)** that excess.
---
Q: List THREE standard **rental conditions** a tourist must meet before collecting a rental car in South Africa. (3)
A: 1. Meet the **minimum age requirement** (`commonly 21–23 years`). 2. Hold a **valid unendorsed driver's licence** (plus IDP if foreign). 3. Present a **valid credit card in the main driver's name** for the security/excess deposit.
---
Q: Why would a rental company charge an **incidental traffic-fine administration fee** even though the fine itself goes to the municipality? (2)
A: The fee covers the company's **administrative time and cost** of identifying the driver from contract records, transferring the fine notice, or processing payment on the renter's behalf.
```
