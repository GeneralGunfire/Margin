# Interactive graphics status

All planned interactive diagrams for Grade 11 Pure Mathematics and Physical Sciences have been implemented and wired into lessons:

- Pure Mathematics (`src/components/diagram/`): exponents/surds, quadratics/discriminant, number patterns, trigonometry (CAST, reduction circle, waves), functions (parabola, hyperbola, exponential, trig, average gradient), probability (Venn, 3-event Venn, independence, tree diagram, contingency table), finance (depreciation curves, timelines), geometry (circle geometry theorems, cyclic quads, tangents, trig triangle rules, analytical lines, inclination), and statistics/measurement (histograms, ogives, skewness boxplots, 3D solids).
- Physical Sciences (`src/components/diagram/vectors.tsx`, `physics.tsx`, `chemistry.tsx`):
  - `vector-components-explorer` (Vectors in two dimensions)
  - `incline-forces-explorer` (Forces, friction and Newton's laws)
  - `inverse-square-explorer` (Universal gravitation & 1D Coulomb's law)
  - `coulomb-2d-and-field-visual` (2D Coulomb's law & electric fields)
  - `faraday-lenz-explorer` (Electromagnetism & Faraday/Lenz induction)
  - `circuit-ohms-power-explorer` (Electric circuits, Ohm's law & power)
  - `vsepr-polarity-explorer` (Atomic combinations & VSEPR polarity)
  - `imf-boiling-point-visual` (Intermolecular forces & physical properties)
  - `gas-laws-explorer` (Ideal gases & gas laws)
  - `stoich-roadmap-visual` (Quantitative chemistry stoichiometry roadmap)
  - `enthalpy-profile-explorer` (Energy & chemical change profiles)
  - `acid-base-redox-visual` (Acid-base & redox transfer)
