> **What you'll learn:** how to tackle **Vraestel 1 Afdeling A (Leesbegrip — 30 marks)** efficiently using **Barrett's cognitive levels**, how to answer **Feit vs Mening**, **Waar of Onwaar (met aanhaling/motivering)**, **Eie woorde**, **Gevolgtrekking (Inference)**, and **Meervoudigekeuse-vrae** without throwing marks away!
>
> **Prerequisites:** basic vocabulary and sentence reading skills.
>
> **Why this matters:** Comprehension carries **30 out of 80 marks** in Paper 1 (nearly 40% of the entire paper!). Most FAL learners lose marks NOT because they failed to understand the story, but because they gave **"Waar"** without quoting a motivation, copied a whole paragraph instead of using **eie woorde**, or missed negative question instructions (*"Noem TWEE redes..."*).

## The idea in plain language

A comprehension passage in Afrikaans FAL is usually divided into **Teks A (Prose/Article, ~24 marks)** and **Teks B (Visual/Graph/Infographic, ~6 marks)**. Every question belongs to one of three thinking jobs:
1. **Soek die feit (`Letterlik` — ~40%):** The answer is printed right there in the specified paragraph — read the **paragraph number (`Paragraaf 3`)** carefully!
2. **Dink en lei af (`Afleiding` — ~40%):** Connect two clues, explain *hoekom*, rewrite in **eie woorde**, or interpret a graph/percentage in Teks B.
3. **Evalueer en oordeel (`Waardering/Evaluering` — ~20%):** Give your opinion **backed by a reason from the text**, identify **doelgroep** (target audience) or **skrywer se gesindheid/toon** (attitude/tone).

## The 6 High-Frequency Vraagwoorde & Exam Commands

| Afrikaans Question Word | Meaning | Exact Exam Strategy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Wie / Wat / Waar / Wanneer** | Who / What / Where / When | Short, exact factual answer straight from the stated paragraph. |
| **Haal 'n sin / frase / EEN woord aan** | Quote a sentence / phrase / ONE word | **Use quotation marks (`"..."`) and copy spelling 100% accurately!** Check whether it asks for **`een woord`**, **`'n frase`** (no verb), or **`'n volsin`**! |
| **Gee TWEE opeenvolgende woorde** | Give TWO consecutive words | Must sit **right next to each other** in the text! |
| **Waar of Onwaar? Motiveer** | True or False? Motivate | **0 marks if you ONLY write Waar/Onwaar!** Always add a reason (`Want...`) OR exact quote if asked to `Haal aan`. |
| **In jou eie woorde** | In your own words | **Do NOT lift whole clauses verbatim!** Swap key verbs/nouns into simpler synonyms while keeping the exact facts. |
| **Stem jy saam? Motiveer** | Do you agree? Motivate | Write **`Ja, want...`** OR **`Nee, want...`** — ANY logical opinion earns the mark **ONLY if your motivation makes sense in context!** |

## The Golden 4-Step Reading Routine (45 minutes for 30 marks)

```steps
title: How to ace Afdeling A without running out of time
step: **1. Skim Title, Subheadings & Teks B first (2 min):** Ask: *What is the topic? Is this a news article, biography, or warning?* Look at captions and numbers in Teks B immediately.
step: **2. Read Questions BEFORE Deep-Reading (3 min):** Circle paragraph numbers (`Par. 2`, `Par. 5`), mark caps like `EEN woord`, `TWEE redes`, and notice `NIE` (*Watter bewering is NIE waar nie?*).
step: **3. Read the Passage Paragraph-by-Paragraph with a Pencil:** Bracket where each question's answer sits right in the margin (`V3`, `V4`).
step: **4. Write Crisp, Mark-Matched Answers:** Check the mark allocation! `(2)` means **TWO separate points** OR **1 statement + 1 clear motivation**. Never overwrite a 1-mark quote into a 4-line essay!
```

### Decoding Teks B (Graphs, Tables & Infographics — ~6 marks)
- Always check **axes, units (`%`, `miljoen`, `jare`), and footnotes** before answering!
- Watch comparison questions (`Hoeveel meer...?`, `Watter groep toon die grootste styging?`) — calculate the exact difference if numbers are given!

```compare
title: Costly Comprehension Mistakes vs Full-Mark Answers
left: Loses easy marks!
- **Vraag:** *Is die volgende stelling WAAR of ONWAAR? Motiveer jou antwoord: "Die projek het binne twee weke misluk."*
- **Bad answer:** `Onwaar.` *(0/2 — no motivation!)*
- **Bad quote:** *"Haal EEN woord aan wat wys dat sy bly was."* -> `"Sy was baie verheug."` *(0/1 — quoted 4 words instead of ONE!)*
right: Earns 100% every time!
- **Good answer:** *Onwaar. Die teks sê die projek het "na drie jaar eers groot sukses behaal".* *(2/2!)*
- **Good quote:** `"verheug"` *(1/1 — exact spelling, single word, in inverted commas!)*
```

## Worked example: Answering Inference + "Waar/Onwaar" cleanly

**Mini-Teks (Paragraaf 2):** *"Alhoewel Sibusiso se winkel tydens beurtkrag min kliënte gehad het, het hy geweier om sy drie werkers af te dank. Hy het eerder sy openingsure aangepas sodat hulle soggens vroeër brood kon bak."*

1. **Vraag 1:** *Haal TWEE opeenvolgende woorde aan wat wys dat sake tydens beurtkrag sleg gegaan het.* (1)
   - **Antwoord:** `"min kliënte"` (two consecutive words, copied accurately!).
2. **Vraag 2:** *Waarom dink jy het Sibusiso nie sy werkers afgedank nie? Gee jou eie afleiding.* (2)
   - **Antwoord:** *Hy het omgegee vir sy werkers se gesinne / hy was lojaal en vindingryk en wou liewer 'n praktiese oplossing vind.* (2 distinct valid inferences!)
3. **Vraag 3:** *Waar of Onwaar? Sibusiso het dieselfde openingsure behou. Motiveer.* (2)
   - **Antwoord:** *Onwaar. Hy het "sy openingsure aangepas" om vroeër brood te bak.*

```tryit
title: Spot the question-type trap!
prompt: Read this sentence from a comprehension passage:
> *"Vandag gebruik jongmense gemiddeld vier uur per dag aan skerms, wat slaaptyd dikwels steel."*
Answer both questions accurately:
1. *Haal **EEN woord** aan wat wys dat skerms 'n negatiewe effek op rus het.* (1)
2. *Verduidelik in **jou eie woorde** wat die skrywer hiermee bedoel.* (2)
hint: In #1, ONLY ONE word (personification verb!). In #2, avoid copying `gemiddeld`, `skerms`, or `steel` unchanged!
answer: 1. `"steel"` (1 mark — exact ONE word!).
2. *Tieners spandeer daagliks baie tyd op selfone of rekenaars, en gevolglik kry hulle snags minder rus / gaan hulle te laat slaap.* (2 marks — accurate paraphrase in own words!)
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Skipping the **motivation** on `Waar / Onwaar` or `Stem jy saam?` — in DBE memos, **0 marks** are awarded for a naked `"Waar"` or `"Ja"` without a valid reason!
> - Copying **extra surrounding words** when asked for **`EEN woord`** or **`TWEE opeenvolgende woorde`** — markers mark it **wrong (`0`)** immediately!
> - Answering about the **wrong paragraph** (`Paragraaf 4` instead of `Paragraaf 2`). Always re-verify the paragraph number before moving on!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Wat is die verskil tussen **"Haal EEN woord aan"** en **"Haal 'n volsin aan"**? (2)
A: **`EEN woord`** vereis streng slegs **1 enkele woord** in aanhalingstekens (`"..."`); **`'n volsin`** vereis die **hele sin** van die hoofletter tot die punt.
---
Q: Hoeveel punte verdien jy as 'n vraag sê *"Waar of Onwaar? Motiveer jou antwoord (2)"* en jy skryf net **"Onwaar"**? (1)
A: **0 punte** — sonder 'n korrekte motivering uit die teks word die antwoord nie aanvaar nie.
---
Q: Watter twee goed moet jy altyd nagaan wanneer jy **Teks B** (bv. 'n grafiek of infografika) in Vraestel 1 lees? (2)
A: Kyk na die **opskrifte/asse en eenhede** (bv. persentasies of jaartalle) én lees enige **voetnotas of onderskrifte** onderaan die visuele teks.
```
