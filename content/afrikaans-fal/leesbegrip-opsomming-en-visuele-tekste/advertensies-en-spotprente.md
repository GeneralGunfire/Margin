> **What you'll learn:** how to analyse **Advertensies** using the **ABBA / AIDA** persuasion model (**Aandag, Belangstelling, Begeerte, Aksie**) — covering *teikengroep, slagspreuk, lettertipe, manipulerende taal* — and **Spotprente (Cartoons)** covering *liggaamstaal, gesigsuitdrukking, spraak-/dinkborrels, ironie, satire* in Vraestel 1 Afdeling C.
>
> **Prerequisites:** basic reading comprehension, connotations, and tone recognition.
>
> **Why this matters:** Visual literacy (*Afdeling C*) carries **20 marks** (`10` advert + `10` cartoon) alongside language editing in Vraestel 1! Most questions have predictable phrasing — once you know the exact Afrikaans terms (`doelgroep`, `visuele leidraad`, `spraakborrel`, `ironie`), those marks become reliable!

## Part 1: Advertensies (`Aandag -> Belangstelling -> Begeerte -> Aksie`)

Every advertisement wants to persuade a specific **teikengroep (target market)** to buy a product, attend an event, or change behaviour (`aksie`). Markers check whether you notice **how text AND image work together**:

| Letter | Afrikaans Term | How the Advert Achieves It |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **A** | **Aandag** (*Attention*) | Large bold **lettertipe (font)**, catchy **opskrif**, bright contrast, or surprising **illustrasie / foto** right at the top. |
| **B** | **Belangstelling** (*Interest*) | Relatable problem or promise, short bullet points, **retoriese vraag** (*"Moeg vir duur datapakke?"*), or clear product benefits. |
| **B** | **Begeerte** (*Desire*) | **Emosionele / oorredende taal**, **byvoeglike naamwoorde** (`vars, blitsvinnig`), **spesiale aanbod** (`50% afslag!`), **statistiek/getuienis**, and **`jy / jou`** pronouns! |
| **A** | **Aksie** (*Action*) | Clear instruction in **bevelvorm (imperative)**: *"Skakel nou!"*, *"Besoek www..."*, QR-kode, telefoonnommer, en **slagspreuk (slogan) / logo**. |

### Key Afrikaans Terms to Answer Advert Questions Accurately
- **Teikengroep / Doelgroep:** *Who* is it aimed at? Give a specific group + reason from the advert (*"Jong atlete / studente, want dit adverteer bekostigbare drafskoene en gebruik informele taal"*).
- **Slagspreuk (Slogan):** Short, memorable brand catchphrase (often uses **alliterasie** like *"Vars vir elke vriend"*).
- **Klein skrif / Vrywaring (Fine print):** Warning or hidden condition at the bottom (*"Terme en voorwaardes geld"* — often hides the catch!).
- **Feit vs Mening in advertensies:** `"Bevat 500 ml"` = **Feit**; `"Die lekkerste drankie ter wêreld"` = **Mening** (hyperbool/oortreffende trap!).

## Part 2: Spotprente (Cartoons) — Reading Body Language & Irony

In a cartoon (`spotprent`), **every line drawn by the artist is a clue**:

```steps
title: Four things to inspect inside every Spotprent frame (raampie)
step: **1. Gesigsuitdrukking (Facial expression):** Look closely at **oë** (wide = shocked; narrow = angry/suspicious), **wenkbroue** (raised = surprised; knitted = furious), **mond** (open = shouting; downturned = sad/disappointed).
step: **2. Liggaamstaal (Body language):** **Hande op heupe** (impatient/bossy), **gekruiste arms** (defensive/stubborn), **geboë skouers** (exhausted/defeated), **vuis gebal** (angry).
step: **3. Spraakborrel vs Dinkborrel & Leestekens:** Smooth line + pointer = spoken **spraakborrel**; cloud circles (`ooo`) = unspoken **dinkborrel**; jagged border or **HOOFLETTERS / vetdruk / `!!!`** = shouting, panic, or urgency!
step: **4. Agtergrond & Ironie / Spot (Satire):** Compare **what the character SAYS** with **what the picture ACTUALLY SHOWS** — the clash creates **ironie** or **humor**!
```

```compare
title: Describing a cartoon weakly vs earning full Visual-Clue marks
left: Weak vague description (0–1/2)
- **Vraag:** *Hoe weet ons die ma in Raampie 2 is kwaad? Noem TWEE visuele leidrade.*
- **Bad answer:** *"Sy lyk kwaad en sy skree op hom."* (No specific drawn body clues!)
right: Exact visual evidence (2/2!)
- **Good answer:** *1. Haar **wenkbroue is gefrons / oë skrefies** (en haar mond is wyd oop). 2. Sy **wys met haar vinger** (of sy het haar **hand op haar heup**) en die teks is in **vetdruk/hoofletters met uitroeptekens**.*
```

## Worked example: Answering Advert + Cartoon Exam Questions

**Scenario A (Advert):** A poster for *"EkoBottel"* shows a crystal-clear mountain stream inside a stainless-steel bottle with the headline: **"STOP PLASTIEKMORS! Drink vars, bly groen."** At the bottom in tiny font: *Beskikbaar teen R249 tot Saterdag.*

1. *Hoe trek die adverteerder die leser se aandag?* -> **Die opskrif ("STOP PLASTIEKMORS!") is in groot vet hoofletters en daar is 'n opvallende prent van skoon bergwater binne-in 'n bottel.**
2. *Watter tegniek gebruik "Drink vars, bly groen"?* -> **Bevelvorm (imperatief) wat tot aksie aanspoor / ritmiese slagspreuk.**
3. *Haal 'n voorbeeld van 'n manipulerende/emosionele oorredingswoord aan en verduidelik die doel.* -> **"`MORS`" of "`vars`"** — dit laat plastiek sleg en skadelik lyk sodat die leser skuldig voel en eerder die herbruikbare bottel koop.

**Scenario B (Cartoon):** In Frame 1, a manager boasts: *"Ons maatskappy omarm groen tegnologie!"* Behind his glass office, dark factory chimneys pour black smoke over dead trees.

- *Verduidelik die **ironie** in hierdie spotprent.* (2)
  > **Antwoord:** Die bestuurder **spog dat sy maatskappy omgewingsvriendelik ("groen") is**, **maar in werklikheid** wys die agtergrond dat die fabriek die lug en bome **swaar besoedel**!

```tryit
title: Spot TWO visual clues + explain the irony
prompt: Imagine a learner in a cartoon says in bold jagged speech: *"EK LUISTER MENEER, EK FOKUS 100%!"* — while holding a glowing phone under the desk and wearing earphones with music notes drawn next to them.
1. Noem **TWEE visuele leidrade** wat wys die leerder lieg. (2)
2. Wat wys die **skerp/getande spraakborrel en hoofletters** oor hoe die leerder praat? (1)
hint: Point to concrete drawn objects (`selfoon`, `oorfone` / `musieknote`), NOT feelings!
answer: 1. **1.** Die leerder hou 'n **selfoon onder die lessenaar** vas. **2.** Die leerder dra **oorfone (met musieknote daarby)**.
2. Hy/sy **skree of praat baie hard / paniekerig** om nie gevang te word nie!
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Answering *"Hoe weet ons hy is geskok?"* with *"Want hy is verbaas"* — you MUST name **physical drawn features** (`oop mond`, `groot oë`, `opgeligte wenkbroue`, `hande in die lug`)!
> - Explaining **ironie** by mentioning only ONE side (*"Hy praat van groen tegnologie"*). Always write **BOTH halves**: *"Hy sê X, **maar in werklikheid wys die prent Y**."*
> - Writing `"almal"` as the **teikengroep** of an advert — always identify a realistic target group (`tieners`, `ouers`, `werkende mense`) based on price, tone, and product!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Waarvoor staan die vier letters in die **ABBA-beginsel** vir advertensies? (4)
A: **A**andag (*Attention*), **B**elangstelling (*Interest*), **B**egeerte (*Desire*), **A**ksie (*Action*).
---
Q: Hoe verskil 'n **spraakborrel** visueel van 'n **dinkborrel** in 'n spotprent, en wat dui 'n **getande (jagged)** rand aan? (3)
A: 'n **Spraakborrel** het 'n soliede lyn en 'n pyltjie/stert na die spreker; 'n **dinkborrel** lyk soos 'n wolkie met klein borreltjies/sirkels. 'n **Getande rand** dui aan dat iemand **skree, kwaad is, of geskok reageer**.
---
Q: Hoekom gebruik adverteerders voornaamwoorde soos **jy** en **jou**? (2)
A: Om die leser **persoonlik aan te spreek** en direk betrokke te laat voel sodat hulle die produk wil koop.
```
