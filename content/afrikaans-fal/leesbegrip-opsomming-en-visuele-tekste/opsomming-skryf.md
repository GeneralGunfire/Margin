> **What you'll learn:** the **exact DBE Paper 1 Afdeling B Summary format** (**7 numbered bullet-point sentences**, maximum **70 words**, written strictly in **your own words**), how the **10 marks** are awarded (**7 marks** for facts + **3 marks** for language, WITH penalties for exceeding 70 words or copying verbatim!), and a fast **4-pass technique** to get `9–10 / 10` every time.
>
> **Prerequisites:** STOMPI basic sentence structure and identifying main ideas vs examples.
>
> **Why this matters:** Summary writing is the **easiest 10 marks in Paper 1** once you follow the strict format rules — yet hundreds of learners throw away 3 language marks by writing a paragraph instead of numbered points, exceeding 70 words, or lifting sentences word-for-word!

## The idea in plain language

Vraestel 1 Afdeling B gives you a ~250-word informative article and asks you to list **7 specific tips/reasons/steps** (for example: *"Som SEWE wenke op oor hoe leerders stres voor eksamens kan verminder"*).

Notice the strict DBE Afrikaans FAL rules (**different from Home Language paragraph summaries!**):
1. **Puntsvorm (`1` to `7`)** — ALWAYS write **7 separate numbered full sentences** (one line per point)! Never write a single flowing paragraph!
2. **Maksimum 70 woorde** — NEVER go above 70 words! Aim cleanly for **55–68 words** (7 sentences × ~8–9 words each).
3. **Eie woorde** — Do NOT lift original sentences verbatim! Swap verbs/nouns or condense clauses cleanly.
4. **Geen titel nie** (unless specifically asked — normally the prompt says *"Moenie 'n opskrif skryf nie"*) — and **NEVER write > 7 points**! Markers stop reading after Point 7!
5. **Tel en skryf jou presiese woordetal** in brackets right underneath (`Aantal woorde: 63`). Do NOT lie about your word count — markers count every word if it looks long!

### How the 10 marks are split (DBE FAL Memo Rubric)

- **7 marks (1 per accurate fact):** Each numbered point must match the **exact instruction** (e.g. *stres verminder*, NOT random background history about exams!).
- **3 marks (Taal / Language & Eie woorde):**
  - `3` marks if <= 2 verbatim lifts and clear, grammatical Afrikaans!
  - `2` marks if some lifting/minor errors.
  - `1` mark if mostly copied directly from the passage or weak grammar.
  - **Word-count penalty:** Every word AFTER word 70 is crossed out by the marker — meaning Point 7 gets **0 marks** if you hit 75 words!

## The 4-Pass Method (15–20 minutes -> 10/10)

```steps
title: Step-by-step Opsomming Workflow
step: **1. Read the Instruction TWICE:** Underline the exact target (*"SEWE maniere om water by die skool te bespaar"*). Ignore anything in the passage that does NOT answer that exact focus (introductions, jokes, statistics, stories!).
step: **2. Bracket 7–9 Candidate Facts on the Passage:** Cross out filler examples (*"soos appels, pere en piesangs"* -> summarise simply as *"vrugte"*!), dates, and repeated ideas. Make sure no two points say the exact same thing!
step: **3. Draft 7 Short `S - v1 - O/P` Sentences (`<= 9 words` each!) in Eie Woorde:** Start directly with **`Jy moet...`** OR an imperative/plural subject (**`Leerders moet...`** / **`Slaap agt uur...`** — just stay consistent and write **full grammatical sentences** with a verb!).
step: **4. Edit, Number 1–7, and Count EVERY Word (`<= 70`):** Count hyphenated words (`e-pos` = 1 word) and numbers carefully. Write `Aantal woorde: XX` neatly at the bottom.
```

## Smart Ways to Put Facts in "Eie Woorde" (Without Changing Meaning!)

```compare
title: Verbatim Copying (loses Language marks) vs Clean Own-Words Compression
left: Lifted verbatim (14 words — too long + copied!)
- *Passage:* "Deskundiges beveel sterk aan dat hoërskoolleerders elke aand ten minste agt uur lank moet slaap."
- *Bad point:* `1. Deskundiges beveel sterk aan dat hoërskoolleerders elke aand agt uur moet slaap.`
right: Short own-words sentence (7 words — crisp!)
- *Good point:* `1. Kry elke nag minstens agt uur slaap.` *(Of: `1. Leerders behoort snags genoeg te rus.`)*
- Cuts fluff (`Deskundiges beveel sterk aan...`), swaps `aand -> nag`, keeps the core fact!
```

### Three instant shortening tricks when you are at 78 words:
1. Replace **list examples** with a **category word** (`"selfone, tablette en skootrekenaars"` -> **`"elektroniese skerms"`**).
2. Cut filler openings (`"Dit is ook baie belangrik dat jy..."` -> just start with the verb or **`"Eet..."` / `"Drink..."`**!).
3. Swap `"omdat dit jou help om..."` for **`"vir..."`** or drop the extra reason when ONLY the action is asked.

## Worked example: Condensing 3 passage sentences into crisp summary points

**Instruction:** *Som **DRIE wenke** op om geld vir studente te spaar (eie woorde).*

- **Passage excerpt:** *"Volgens finansiële raadgewers mors baie studente elke oggend geld deur duur koffie by die kafeteria te koop in plaas daarvan om 'n fles van die huis af te bring. Tweedens is dit 'n slim plan om tweedehandse handboeke by ouer matrieks te koop. Laastens help dit om aan die begin van die maand 'n duidelike begroting op papier neer te skryf sodat jy weet waaraan elke sent bestee word."*

**Model Output (24 words for 3 points — right on pace for ~55–60 words over 7 points!):**
1. Maak eerder jou eie koffie tuis. *(6 woorde)*
2. Koop gebruikte skoolboeke by ouer leerders. *(6 woorde)*
3. Stel maandeliks 'n eenvoudige begroting op. *(6 woorde — let op: `stel ... op` is skeibaar!)*
*(Aantal woorde: 18)*

```tryit
title: Compress a wordy 18-word sentence to under 9 words in eie woorde
prompt: Verwerk hierdie sin tot **ONE kort opsommingspunt (`<= 8 woorde`)** oor hoe om die omgewing te beskerm:
> *"Wetenskaplikes waarsku dat mense plastieksakke moet vermy wanneer hulle kruideniersware koop en eerder herbruikbare katoensakke moet saamdra."*
hint: Cut `"Wetenskaplikes waarsku dat"` and condense `"kruideniersware koop"` -> `"inkopies"`!
answer: **Example:** `1. Gebruik katoensakke in plaas van plastiek tydens inkopies.` *(8 woorde!)* — of `1. Dra jou eie herbruikbare sakke winkel toe.` *(7 woorde!)*
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Writing **phrases without verbs** (`1. Genoeg slaap.`) — Grade 11 FAL summaries MUST be **full sentences** (`1. Kry genoeg slaap.`).
> - Exceeding **70 words** — the marker draws a red line right after word 70 and **does NOT mark anything beyond it**!
> - Writing **one big paragraph** when the instruction specifies **puntsvorm (`1`–`7`)** — loses immediate format/language marks.
> - Counting incorrectly or adding a title when told *"Moenie 'n opskrif gee nie."*

## Practice

```questions
Q: Hoeveel punte is Afdeling B (Opsomming) werd, en hoe word daardie punte verdeel? (3)
A: **10 punte** — **7 punte** vir 7 korrekte feite en **3 punte** vir taalgebruik en eie woorde.
---
Q: Wat gebeur as jou opsomming **78 woorde** lank is? (2)
A: Die nasiener tel tot by **woord 70**, trek 'n streep, en **merk niks na woord 70 nie** — dus verloor jy maklik jou laaste punt (en moontlik taalpunte).
---
Q: Verkort hierdie sin tot hoogstens **7 woorde** in jou eie woorde: *"Dit is baie noodsaaklik dat tieners daagliks genoeg skoon water drink."* (2)
A: **Drink elke dag genoeg water.** (5 woorde — of **Tieners moet daagliks baie water drink.** 6 woorde!)
```
