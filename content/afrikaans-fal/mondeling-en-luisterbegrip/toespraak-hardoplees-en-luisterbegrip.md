> **What you'll learn:** how **Vraestel 4 (`Mondeling`, 50 punte)** works in Grade 11 Afrikaans FAL — **Luisterbegrip** (Listening Comprehension), **Voorbereide Toespraak** (Prepared Speech with cue cards), **Onvoorbereide Toespraak / Gesprek**, and **Voorbereide Hardoplees** (Prepared Reading aloud with pronunciation, phrasing & expression).
>
> **Prerequisites:** STOMPI sentence control, Afrikaans vowel pronunciation (`ui, eu, y, aai, ooi`), and speech structure.
>
> **Why this matters:** Speaking Afrikaans aloud feels intimidating when it isn't your home language — yet **Vraestel 4 counts 50 marks**! Smart cue-card preparation, clear pronunciation of tricky Afrikaans sounds, and calm listening strategies turn oral marks into a huge confidence boost.

## The idea in plain language

Teachers grading **Afrikaans FAL Orals** do NOT expect you to speak like a news anchor without an accent — they look for **three clear, learnable skills**:
1. **Verstaanbaarheid & Struktuur:** Can the audience follow your main points clearly from introduction to conclusion?
2. **Natuurlike Aanbieding:** Eye contact with the class/teacher using **sleutelwoord-kaartjies** (NOT hiding behind an A4 page!), steady pace, and clear articulation.
3. **Aktiewe Luister:** Extracting main facts accurately when listening twice to a spoken Afrikaans passage (`Luisterbegrip`).

## Task 1: Luisterbegrip (Listening Comprehension)

Your teacher reads (or plays) a short Afrikaans text **twice**:
- **Eerste luister (Sit die pen neer / skryf net 3–4 kernwoorde!):** Focus 100% on **wie**, **wat**, and **die hooftema** so your brain tunes into the speaker's rhythm.
- **Tweede luister (Aantekeninge langs elke vraag):** Now you have the questions or subheadings! Jot short Afrikaans/bilingual shorthand clues (`redes`, `getalle`, `plekke`), then expand them cleanly into full Afrikaans answers afterward.

## Task 2: Voorbereide Toespraak (`2–3 minute` + Sleutelkaartjies)

```steps
title: Five steps for a confident Afrikaans FAL Speech
step: **1. Kies 'n konkrete onderwerp:** Topics tied to real examples (`My gunsteling-sportheld`, `Die gevaar van kitskos`, `Hoe musiek ons help`) are far easier to speak naturally about than vague philosophy!
step: **2. Skryf die raamwerk (Inleiding -> 2–3 Punte -> Slot):** Keep sentences **short and spoken (`10–14 woorde`)**! Avoid long Groep 3 sentences you might stumble over mid-breath.
step: **3. Maak 4 klein Sleutelkaartjies (Cue cards):** Write ONLY **3–4 bullet keywords + 1 stat per card** (NO full sentences copied off your essay!).
step: **4. Oefen hardop met 'n stophorlosie:** Practise pronouncing tricky Afrikaans sounds (`ui` in *huis*, `eu` in *deur*, `g` in *goed*, `r` clearly rolled/tapped) and check your timing (`2–3 minute`).
step: **5. Oogkontak & Pouses:** Greet politely (*"Goeiemôre juffrou en maats"*), pause 1 second before starting your hook, look up regularly from your small cards, and end cleanly (*"Baie dankie."*).
```

## Task 3: Hardoplees (Prepared Reading Aloud)

In **Voorbereide Hardoplees**, you read a selected prose/dialogue passage aloud for ~2 minutes. Markers score **vlotheid (fluency)**, **uitspraak (pronunciation)**, **stembuiging (expression)**, and **pouses by leestekens**:

```compare
title: Common FAL Reading Mistakes vs Top-Band Oral Delivery
left: Drops fluency marks!
- Racing past **commas and full stops** so sentences crash together.
- Pronouncing **`ui`** (`huis`) like English *"oo"* or **`eu`** (`seun`) like *"you"*.
- Reading **dialogue quotes** (`"Kom gou!" sê sy.`) in the exact same flat monotone as narration.
right: Scores top delivery marks!
- **Pause half a beat** at commas (`,`) and **a full beat** at full stops (`.`).
- Practise problem words beforehand (`verantwoordelikheid`, `gemeenskap`, `ontwikkeling`, `besluit`).
- Change pitch slightly for **questions (`?`)** and **spoken dialogue** while glancing up at the teacher briefly at paragraph ends!
```

## Task 4: Onvoorbereide Gesprek / Toespraak (Short Q&A Rescue Kit)

Keep these **4 Afrikaans bridge phrases** ready so you never freeze silently if asked a follow-up question:
1. **Om tyd te wen:** *"Dit is 'n baie goeie vraag. Ek dink dat..."*
2. **Om 'n voorbeeld te gee:** *"Byvoorbeeld, in ons eie skool sien ons dikwels dat..."*
3. **Om jou mening te staaf:** *"Die hoofrede hiervoor is dat..."*
4. **Om netjies af te sluit:** *"Ten slotte glo ek dus dat..."*

## Worked example: Turning a dense script into clean Oral Cue Cards

**Dense script (BAD on a cue card — forces reading!):**
> *"Volgens navorsing slaap tieners gemiddeld slegs ses uur per nag omdat hulle tot laat op hul selfone kyk en dit veroorsaak swak punte."*

**Clean Cue Card (`Kaartjie 2 — Oorsake`):**
> **2. HOEKOM SLAAP ONS MIN?**
> - *Navorsing: net 6 uur/nag*
> - *Selfone laat in die bed*
> - *Gevolg: moeg in klas -> swak punte*
> `[KYK OP NA KLAS]`

```tryit
title: Pronounce & phrase a dialogue line naturally for Hardoplees
prompt: Read this short extract silently and list **3 specific delivery adjustments** you should make when reading it aloud:
> *"Wag 'n bietjie!" roep Thabo verbaas uit. "Het jy regtig vergeet dat ons vandag die museum besoek?"*
hint: Check exclamation mark tone, pause before narration, and rising pitch on the Yes/No question!
answer: 1. Read `"Wag 'n bietjie!"` with **urgent, surprised energy** (raised volume/expression).
2. Drop briefly to a **calm narrative tone** for `roep Thabo verbaas uit.`
3. Use a **rising questioning intonation** on `"Het jy regtig vergeet..."` ending cleanly at the question mark!
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Holding a full **A4 sheet** in front of your face and reading word-for-word without eye contact — kills presentation marks even if your grammar is perfect!
> - Speaking **too fast** out of nerves (usually makes `uitspraak` blurry and finishes a 3-minute speech in 75 seconds!).
> - Using English filler words (`"uhm, so basically, like..."`) instead of pausing quietly or using **`en dus` / `byvoorbeeld`**.

## Practice

```questions
Q: Wat moet jy op jou **sleutelwoord-kaartjies** skryf vir 'n voorbereide toespraak, en wat moet jy daaruit weghou? (2)
A: Skryf slegs **kort kernwoorde, syfers/feite en oorgangsuitdrukkings** neer; hou **volledige sinne en paragrawe** weg sodat jy nie aflees nie.
---
Q: Hoekom is dit slim om tydens die **eerste lees** van 'n **Luisterbegrip** amper niks neer te skryf nie? (2)
A: Sodoende fokus jy op die **algehele konteks, hooftema en vloei** van die teks, en weet jy presies waar die spesifieke antwoorde lê tydens die tweede lees.
---
Q: Noem TWEE leestekens waarop jy tydens **Hardoplees** duidelik met jou stem en pouses moet reageer. (2)
A: Enige twee van: **Komma/Punt** (pouseer duidelik), **Vraagteken** (stemtoon styg aan die einde), **Uitroepteken** (kragtiger/emosionele klem), of **Aanhalingstekens** (wissel na spreekstem vir dialoog).
```
