> **What you'll learn:** how **modal verbs** (*kan, moet, mag, wil, sal* -> *kon, moes, mog, wou, sou*) behave in present, past, and future tenses, how **separable verbs** (*skeibare werkwoorde* like *opstaan*, *aantrek*, *skoonmaak*) split in STOMPI and stitch back together with `ge-` and `te`, and how **infinite/double-verb constructions** (*gaan, kom, bly, begin*) work.
>
> **Prerequisites:** STOMPI word order and `ge-` past-tense formation.
>
> **Why this matters:** Separable verbs like *opstaan* (*"Hy staan op"* vs *"Hy het opgestaan"* vs *"Hy probeer om op te staan"*) appear in almost every editing, tense, and conjunction question in Grade 11!

## The idea in plain language

In English, two-word verbs like *wake up* stay together (*"I woke up"*). In Afrikaans, a **separable verb (`skeibare werkwoord`)** is one word in the dictionary (*opstaan*, *saamgaan*, *regmaak*, *weghardloop*), **splits apart** in simple Present tense, and **hugs `ge-` or `te` right in its middle** when put in the Past tense or an infinitive!

## Part 1: Skeibare Werkwoorde (Separable Verbs)

Look at the verb **`op + staan`** (*to get up*) across four sentence structures:

| Context | Rule | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Present tense (1 verb)** | Root verb in **v1** (Pos 2); prefix **flies to the END!** | *Sy **staan** elke oggend vroeg **op**.* |
| **With a modal / Future (`kan / sal`)** | Modal in **v1**; **whole word together** at the end! | *Sy **sal** môre vroeg **opstaan**.* |
| **Past tense (`het`)** | **`ge-` slips BETWEEN prefix and root!** | *Sy **het** gister vroeg **opgestaan**.* |
| **Infinitive (`om ... te`)** | **`te` slips BETWEEN prefix and root** (3 separate words or joined? ALWAYS 3 words: **`op te staan`**!) | *Sy probeer **om** vroeg **op te staan**.* |

### How do you know if a prefix is separable or inseparable?
**Test the pronunciation stress!**
- **Stress on the PREFIX (`ÓPstaan`, `ÁÁNtrek`, `ÚÍTgaan`, `ÓÓPmaak`, `SKÓÓNmaak`):** **SEPARABLE!** Splits in present tense (`staan op`), takes **`-ge-`** in the middle (`opgestaan`), takes **`te`** in the middle (`op te staan`).
- **Stress on the ROOT (`beTÁÁL`, `verSTÁÁN`, `ontMÓÉT`, `herHÁÁL`, `geBRÚÍK`):** **INSEPARABLE!** Never splits, NEVER takes `ge-`, and `te` goes in front (*om te betaal*).

```compare
title: Separable ("oopmaak") vs Inseparable ("ontmoet")
left: Separable (`óóp + maak`)
- Present: *Ek **maak** die deur **oop**.*
- Past: *Ek **het** die deur **oopgemaak**.*
- Future: *Ek **sal** die deur **oopmaak**.*
- Infinitive: *Ek gaan **om** die deur **oop te maak**.*
right: Inseparable (`ont + móét`)
- Present: *Ek **ontmoet** my vriend.*
- Past: *Ek **het** my vriend **ontmoet**.* (NO `ge-`!)
- Future: *Ek **sal** my vriend **ontmoet**.*
- Infinitive: *Ek gaan **om** my vriend **te ontmoet**.*
```

## Part 2: Modale Werkwoorde (Modals)

The five core Afrikaans modals express **ability, duty, permission, desire, or future intention**:

| Present Modal | Meaning | Past Form (`NO het!`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **kan** | can / be able to | **kon** |
| **moet** | must / have to | **moes** |
| **mag** | may / allowed to | **mog** *(or `het toegelaat / kon`)* |
| **wil** | want to | **wou** |
| **sal** | will / shall | **sou** |

### Two vital modal traps in Grade 11:
1. **In Past Tense, NEVER write `het` + modal + `ge-` for simple past ability!**
   - Present: *Hy **kan** vinnig **hardloop**.*
   - Past: *Hy **kon** vinnig **hardloop**.* (**NOT** `Hy het kon hardloop` and **NOT** `Hy het gekan hardloop`!)
2. **Modals + `het` (regret / past deduction — *"should have / could have"*):**
   When expressing what someone *should have* done (but didn't!), **`het` sits at the END AFTER `ge-`!**
   - *Hy **moes** sy werk **gedoen het**.* (*He should have done his work.*)
   - *Sy **kon** die wedstryd **gewen het**.* (*She could have won the match.*)

## Part 3: Koppel- & Hulpwerkwoorde (*gaan, kom, bly, begin, probeer*)

- **Instant Future / Movement (`gaan` / `kom` + bare verb):** *Ek **gaan** my kamer **skoonmaak**.* (Past: *Ek **het** my kamer **gaan skoonmaak**.* — Notice **`gaan`** does NOT become `gegaan` before another verb!)
- **Same rule for `kom`, `bly`, `laat`, `hoor`, `sien`, `leer` + verb:**
  - *Hy **bly** rustig **sit**.* -> Past: *Hy **het** rustig **bly sit**.* (NOT `gebly sit`!)
  - *Ek **hoor** die voëls **sing**.* -> Past: *Ek **het** die voëls **hoor sing**.* (NOT `gehoor sing`!)
- **Contrast with `begin` and `probeer`:**
  - *Sy **probeer** om te slaag.* -> Past: *Sy **het probeer** om te slaag.* (`probeer` takes NO `ge-` before `om te`, OR *Sy het **geprobeer**.* when standing alone!)
  - *Dit **begin** reën.* -> Past: *Dit **het begin** reën.* (NO `gebegin`!)

## Worked example: Combining a separable verb + modal + past tense

**Prompt:** *Skryf die sin in die **verlede tyd**:* `Die leerders trek hulle baadjies aan en probeer om betyds klaar te maak.`

1. **`trek ... aan`** (separable! `aan` is at the end) -> past tense uses `het` + **`aangetrek`**.
2. **`en probeer om betyds klaar te maak`** -> in the second clause, **`het` is shared or repeated**: *en het **probeer** om betyds klaar te maak* (remember: `probeer` drops `ge-` when followed by `om ... te` — or stands as `het probeer`!).
3. **Answer:** *Die leerders **het** hulle baadjies **aangetrek** en **het probeer** om betyds klaar te maak.*

```tryit
title: Master all 4 forms of "skoonmaak" (clean)
prompt: Vul die korrekte vorm van **skoonmaak** in:
1. Present: *Ons ___ elke Saterdag die kombuis ___.*
2. Past: *Ons het gister die kombuis ___.*
3. Future: *Ons sal môre die kombuis ___.*
4. Infinitive (`om...te`): *Ons bly tuis om die kombuis ___.*
hint: Watch whether `skoon` and `maak` split, hug `ge-`, join together, or hug `te`!
answer: 1. *Ons **maak** elke Saterdag die kombuis **skoon**.*
2. *Ons het gister die kombuis **skoongemaak**.*
3. *Ons sal môre die kombuis **skoonmaak**.*
4. *Ons bly tuis om die kombuis **skoon te maak**.*
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Joining `op te staan` into one word (`optesaan`) — ALWAYS write **`op te staan`** as three words!
> - Putting `ge-` in front of `aantrek` (*"geaantrek"* instead of **`aangetrek`**) or onto *begin / bly / gaan* before a second verb (*"het gebly sit"* -> WRONG; say **`het bly sit`**!).
> - Using `het` alongside simple `kon / moes / wou` (`"Ek het kon sing"` -> WRONG; say **`Ek kon sing`**!).

## Practice

```questions
Q: Skryf die sin in die **verlede tyd**: *Sy moet haar nuwe skoene aantrek.* (2)
A: **Sy moes haar nuwe skoene aantrek.** (`moet` word `moes`; `aantrek` bly een woord agteraan.)
---
Q: Skryf in die **teenwoordige tyd**: *Die atleet het vroeg opgestaan en het dadelik weggespring.* (2)
A: **Die atleet staan vroeg op en spring dadelik weg.** (Die skeibare voorvoegsels `op` en `weg` skuif na die einde!)
---
Q: Kies die korrekte vorm: *Hy het die hele middag in die biblioteek (gebly sit / bly sit) en het (probeer / geprobeer) om sy opstel te voltooi.* (2)
A: **bly sit** (dubbele infinitief sonder `ge-`), en **probeer** (geen `ge-` voor `om te` nie!).
```
