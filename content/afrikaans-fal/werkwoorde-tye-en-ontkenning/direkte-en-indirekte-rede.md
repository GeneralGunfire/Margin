> **What you'll learn:** how to convert **Statements (`dat`)**, **Yes/No Questions (`of`)**, **Question-word Questions (`waarom / waar / wanneer`)**, and **Commands (`om ... te` / `moet`)** from Direct Speech into Reported Speech (**Indirekte rede**) — including **Groep 3 verb-to-end word order**, **pronoun shifts**, and **time/place/tense changes**.
>
> **Prerequisites:** STOMPI word order, Groep 3 conjunctions (`dat`), and tense rules.
>
> **Why this matters:** *Indirekte rede* tests four skills inside ONE 2–3 mark question: punctuation removal, conjunction insertion (`dat` / `of`), Groep 3 verb movement, and pronoun/time conversion!

## The idea in plain language

When someone speaks directly, we quote their exact words inside inverted commas (`"..."`). When we report what they said to someone else (**Indirekte rede**):
1. Remove the colon (`:`), quotation marks (`"..."`), question mark (`?`), and exclamation mark (`!`).
2. Add the right **bridge word** (**`dat`**, **`of`**, or the **question word** like **`waar`**) — ALL of which act like **Groep 3 conjunctions**, sending `v1` to the **end of the reported clause**!
3. Shift **pronouns** (`ek -> hy/sy`, `jy -> ek/hy`), **time/place words** (`vandag -> daardie dag`, `hier -> daar`), and **tense** when the reporting verb is in the past tense (`het gesê`)!

## The 4 Sentence Types in Indirekte Rede

| Direct Speech Type | Bridge Word | Word Order Rule | Example |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Stelling** (Statement) | **`dat`** | `v1` moves to **END**! | *Hy sê: "Ek **is** moeg."* -> *Hy sê **dat** hy moeg **is**.* |
| **2. Ja/Nee-vraag** (Yes/No Question) | **`of`** (*whether/if*) | `v1` moves to **END**! Drop `?`! | *Sy vra: "**Kom** jy?"* -> *Sy vra **of** ek **kom**.* |
| **3. Vraagwoord-vraag** (`Wie/Wat/Waar/Wanneer/Hoe`) | **Keep the question word** (NO `of`!) | `v1` moves to **END**! Drop `?`! | *Hy vra: "Waar **woon** jy?"* -> *Hy vra **waar** ek **woon**.* |
| **4. Bevel / Versoek** (Command) | **`om ... te`** OR **`dat ... moet`** | Infinitive at end (join separable verb!) | *Ma sê: "Maak jou kamer **skoon**!"* -> *Ma sê ek **moet** my kamer **skoonmaak**.* (Of: *Ma beveel my **om** my kamer **skoon te maak**.*) |

## Crucial Shifts to Check Every Time

### 1. Tense rule: Reporting Verb Present vs Past!
- **Reporting verb in PRESENT (`sê` / `vra`):** Keep the verb inside the quote in its original tense (*"Ek **het** gewen"* -> *Sy **sê** dat sy gewen **het**.*).
- **Reporting verb in PAST (`het gesê` / `het gevra`):**
  - Present inside quote -> shifts to **Past** (*"Ek **is** bly"* -> *Sy **het gesê** dat sy bly **was**.* / *"Ek **leer** hard"* -> *Hy **het gesê** dat hy hard **geleer het**.*).
  - Past inside quote -> stays Past (`het ... ge-` — Afrikaans has NO separate pluperfect like English *"had walked"*! Just keep `het ... + ge-`!).
  - **`sal`** -> **`sou`**, **`kan`** -> **`kon`**, **`moet`** -> **`moes`**, **`wil`** -> **`wou`**.
- *(Note: If the exam specifically writes `Begin so: Hy sê dat...` with `sê` in the present tense, do NOT change the inner tense unless a time word forces it!)*

### 2. Time, Place & Demonstrative Swaps (when reporting from past/later!)
- **nou** -> **toe**
- **vandag** -> **daardie dag**
- **gister** -> **die vorige dag**
- **môre** -> **die volgende dag**
- **hier** -> **daar**
- **hierdie** -> **daardie**

```compare
title: Two classic Indirekte Rede traps
left: Trap 1 — Adding `of` after a question word!
- Direct: *Die toeris vra: "**Waar** is die museum?"*
- **WRONG:** *Die toeris vra **of waar** die museum is.*
- **RIGHT:** *Die toeris vra **waar** die museum **is**.* (`Waar` replaces `of`!)
right: Trap 2 — Word order when there are 2 verbs (`het` + `ge-`)
- Direct: *Thabo sê: "Ek **het** my boek **vergeet**."*
- **WRONG:** *Thabo sê dat hy sy boek **het vergeet**.*
- **RIGHT:** *Thabo sê dat hy sy boek **vergeet het**.* (`v1 = het` goes AFTER `vergeet`!)
```

## Worked example: Past reporting verb + Question + Time word

**Prompt:** *Skryf in die **indirekte rede**:*
`Die onderwyseres het gevra: "Het julle gister hierdie hoofstuk verstaan?"`

1. Remove `:` and `"..."` and `?`.
2. It is a **Yes/No question** -> bridge word is **`of`**!
3. **`julle`** (spoken to learners) -> **`hulle`** (or **`ons`** if the learner is reporting about their own class — usually **`hulle`** in third-person exam answers!).
4. **`gister`** -> **`die vorige dag`**; **`hierdie`** -> **`daardie`**.
5. **`het ... verstaan`** stays `verstaan het` at the end (`verstaan` takes NO `ge-`!).
6. **Answer:** *Die onderwyseres het gevra **of** hulle die vorige dag daardie hoofstuk **verstaan het**.*

```tryit
title: Convert a Direct Command AND a Wh-Question
prompt: Skryf albei sinne in die **indirekte rede**:
1. *Pa het gesê: "Skakel dadelik die televisie af!"*
2. *Lindiwe vra: "Wanneer skryf ons die Afrikaans-toets?"*
hint: For #1, remember separable verb **`afskakel`** rejoins after **`moet`** or **`om ... te`** (`het gesê` -> past `moes` or `het ... beveel om ... af te skakel`!). For #2, do NOT use `of` before `wanneer` — and end with a full stop!
answer: 1. *Pa het gesê **dat ons/hulle dadelik die televisie moet/moes afskakel**.* *(Of: Pa het beveel **om dadelik die televisie af te skakel**.)*
2. *Lindiwe vra **wanneer** ons die Afrikaans-toets **skryf**.* (Geen vraagteken aan die einde nie!)
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Leaving the **question mark (`?`)** at the end of a reported question (*"Sy vra of ek kom?"* -> WRONG! Reported questions are statements and end with a **full stop (`.`)**!).
> - Putting **`dat of`** or **`of waarom`** together (*"Sy vra of wanneer hy kom"* -> WRONG! Use `dat` ONLY for statements, `of` ONLY for Yes/No questions, and the question word ALONE for `W-/Hoe` questions).
> - Leaving `"ek"` / `"hier"` / `"vandag"` unchanged when the reporting speaker is third person (`Hy het gesê...`).

## Practice

```questions
Q: Skryf in die **indirekte rede**: *Die dokter het vir die pasiënt gesê: "Ek sal jou môre weer ondersoek."* (3)
A: **Die dokter het vir die pasiënt gesê dat hy hom/haar die volgende dag weer sou ondersoek.** (`ek -> hy`, `jou -> hom/haar`, `môre -> die volgende dag`, `sal -> sou` skuif na die einde.)
---
Q: Skryf in die **indirekte rede**: *Die polisieman vra: "Het iemand die rooi motor hier gesien?"* (3)
A: **Die polisieman vra of iemand die rooi motor daar gesien het.** (`of` vir ja/nee-vraag, `hier -> daar`, `het` skuif na agter ná `gesien`, punt aan die einde.)
---
Q: Watter drie leestekens verdwyn sodra 'n sin na die indirekte rede omgeskakel word? (3)
A: Die **dubbelpunt (`:`)**, die **aanhalingstekens (`"..."`)**, en enige **vraagteken (`?`) / uitroepteken (`!`)**.
```
