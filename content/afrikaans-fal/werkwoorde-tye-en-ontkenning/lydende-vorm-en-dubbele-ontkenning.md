> **What you'll learn:** how to transform ANY active sentence (*bedrywende vorm*) into passive (*lydende vorm*) across **Present, Past, and Future tenses** using the exact **`word` / `is` / `sal ... word`** formula, plus **dubbele ontkenning** (*nie ... nie*, *nooit ... nie*, *niemand ... nie*, *nêrens ... nie*, *geen ... nie*, and **imperatives** with *Moenie ... nie*).
>
> **Prerequisites:** STOMPI word order, identifying Subject vs Object, and `ge-` past participles.
>
> **Why this matters:** *Lydende vorm* and *Ontkennende vorm* appear as guaranteed conversion questions in **every single Grade 11 Paper 1 exam**! A single wrong auxiliary (*is* instead of *word*, or dropping the final *nie*) costs full marks unnecessarily.

## Part 1: Lydende Vorm (Passive Voice)

In Active voice (*bedrywende vorm*), the Subject does the action:
> *Die seun (**S**) skop (**v1**) die bal (**O**).*

In Passive voice (*lydende vorm*), the **Object steps into Position 1 as the new Subject**, the original doer moves after **`deur`** (*by*), and the verb switches to **auxiliary + `ge-` participle**:

| Tense | Active (`Bedrywend`) | Passive Formula | Passive (`Lydend`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Present** | *Die seun skop die bal.* | **`word`** + `deur [S]` + **`ge-`verb** | *Die bal **word** deur die seun **geskop**.* |
| **Past** | *Die seun het die bal geskop.* | **`is`** + `deur [S]` + **`ge-`verb** | *Die bal **is** deur die seun **geskop**.* |
| **Future** | *Die seun sal die bal skop.* | **`sal`** + `deur [S]` + **`ge-`verb + `word`** | *Die bal **sal** deur die seun **geskop word**.* |
| **Modal (`kan / moet`)** | *Die seun moet die bal skop.* | **`moet`** + `deur [S]` + **`ge-`verb + `word`** | *Die bal **moet** deur die seun **geskop word**.* |

### Pronoun swaps when moving Subject -> `deur ...` and Object -> Front!
- *Ek* -> *deur **my*** (and *my* at front -> ***Ek***)
- *Jy* -> *deur **jou*** (and *jou* at front -> ***Jy***)
- *Hy* -> *deur **hom*** (and *hom* at front -> ***Hy***)
- *Sy* -> *deur **haar*** (and *haar* at front -> ***Sy***)
- *Ons* -> *deur **ons***; *Hulle* -> *deur **hulle***; *U* -> *deur **u***
- **Wait: What about *Iemand* or *Almal* or *Niemand*?**
  - *Iemand* -> stays *deur **iemand*** (or drops off in vague passive).
  - **TRAP:** *Niemand eet die koek nie* (Active) -> *Die koek word **deur niemand** geëet nie* (or *Die koek word **nie deur iemand** geëet nie*).

### Watch STOMPI inside Passive Voice (`T -> deur S -> M -> P -> v2`)!
When moving the original Subject behind `deur`, **keep Time BEFORE `deur S`** (`Die bal word **gister? -> is gister** deur die seun **hard op die veld** geskop`)!

## Part 2: Dubbele Ontkenning (Double Negation)

Afrikaans normally uses **two negative markers**:
1. **First marker (`nie` or a negative word)** sits right after `v1` (or after a pronoun object!).
2. **Second `nie`** sits **right at the VERY END of the sentence**!

### The 5 Negative-Word Swaps (NEVER say *nie ... alles nie*!)

When turning a positive sentence negative, **replace** positive indefinites with their negative partner, and keep ONE closing **`nie`** at the end:

| Positive Word | Negative Partner (Pos 1) | Closing Marker |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **altyd / ooit** (*always / ever*) | **nooit** (*never*) | **... nie** |
| **iets / alles** (*something / everything*) | **niks** (*nothing*) | **... nie** |
| **iemand / almal** (*someone / everyone*) | **niemand** (*nobody*) | **... nie** |
| **êrens / oral** (*somewhere / everywhere*) | **nêrens** (*nowhere*) | **... nie** |
| **'n / baie / sommige / al** (*a / many / some*) | **geen** (*no / none*) | **... nie** |
| **nog** (*still*) | **nie meer ... nie** (*no longer*) | **... nie** |
| **al / reeds** (*already*) | **nog nie ... nie** (*not yet*) | **... nie** |

### Two Crucial Exceptions where there is ONLY ONE `nie`!
1. **Short sentence with ONE verb and NO object/adverb/second verb:**
   - *Hy lag.* -> *Hy lag **nie**.* (ONLY ONE `nie` — NOT `Hy lag nie nie`!)
   - *Ek weet.* -> *Ek weet **nie**.*
2. **Negative word ALREADY sits at the end (no extra words after it):**
   - *Ek hoor iets.* -> *Ek hoor **niks**.* (`niks` is already at the end -> NO extra `nie`? Wait: in standard Afrikaans *Ek hoor **niks nie*** IS required! Wait — let's be crystal clear: **`Ek sien hom nie`** has ONE `nie` when `nie` sits at the end after a single verb + pronoun! Compare:
     - *Ek sien hom.* -> *Ek sien hom **nie**.* (ONLY ONE `nie`, because there are no words left after the first `nie`!)
     - *Ek sien hom gister?* -> *Ek het hom gister **nie** gesien **nie**.* (TWO `nie`s!)
     - *Ek hoor **niks**.* vs *Ek hoor **niks nie**.* -> In DBE Grade 11 FAL memos, **`Ek hoor niks nie`** / **`Sy ken niemand nie`** ALWAYS takes the closing **`nie`**!)

### Commands (Imperatives) & Questions (`Het jy...`)
- **Command:** *Maak die venster oop!* -> ***Moenie** die venster oopmaak **nie**!* (Notice **`Maak ... oop`** joins back into **`oopmaak`** at the end after `Moenie`!)
- **Negative Question:** *Het jy jou boek saamgebring?* -> *Het jy **nie** jou boek saamgebring **nie**?* (Or negative reply: *Nee, ek het **nie** my boek saamgebring **nie**.*)

## Worked example: Double conversion (Passive + Past + Negative!)

**Prompt:** *Skryf eers in die **lydende vorm** en daarna in die **ontkennende vorm**:*
`Die onderwyser het gister alle toetse vinnig nagesien.`

1. **Passive (`het ... nagesien` -> past passive uses `is`!):**
   - New Subject: *Alle toetse*
   - Auxiliary: **`is`** (`het` disappears!)
   - Time -> `deur` Subject -> Manner -> `v2`:
   - **Lydende vorm:** *Alle toetse **is** gister deur die onderwyser vinnig **nagesien**.*
2. **Negative (watch **`alle`** -> **`geen`** or **`nie alle ... nie`**!):**
   - Notice: "the teacher did not mark ALL tests" (`nie alle toetse nie`) vs "marked NO tests" (`geen toetse ... nie`). Usually `almal/alle` -> **`nie alle ... nie`** or **`geen ... nie`** depending on context; for *alle* before a plural noun, **`geen`** works cleanly when negating the whole action:
   - **Ontkennende vorm:** *Die onderwyser het gister **geen** toetse vinnig nagesien **nie**.* *(Of passief: Geen toetse is gister deur die onderwyser vinnig nagesien **nie**.)*

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Using **`het ... geword`** in Past Passive instead of **`is`**: *"Die brief het geskryf geword"* is WRONG! Always write **`Die brief is geskryf`**.
> - Putting **`word`** before the participle in Future Passive (*"sal word geskryf"* -> WRONG!). Put `word` AFTER the participle: **`sal ... geskryf word`**.
> - Forgetting that **`Moenie`** rejoins separable verbs at the end: *Trek jou jas aan!* -> **`Moenie jou jas aantrek nie!`** (NOT `aantrek ... aan nie`!).
> - Leaving **`iets / iemand / altyd / al`** positive inside a negative sentence (*"Ek het nie iets gesien nie"* -> WRONG! Always **`Ek het niks gesien nie`**).

## Practice

```questions
Q: Skryf in die **lydende vorm**: *Sy bak elke Saterdag vars brood in die kombuis.* (2)
A: **Vars brood word elke Saterdag deur haar in die kombuis gebak.** (`sy` -> `deur haar`; teenwoordige tyd -> `word ... gebak`.)
---
Q: Skryf in die **ontkennende vorm**: *Iemand het al my sleutels iewers weggesteek.* (3)
A: **Niemand het nog my sleutels nêrens? Nee — `iemand -> niemand`, `al -> nog`, `iewers -> nêrens` -> Pasop: in Afrikaans vermy ons driedubbele ontkenning waar moontlik; gewoonlik word een negatief (`Niemand`) + `enige / êrens` of twee negatiewes gebruik: `Niemand het my sleutels nog êrens weggesteek nie` OF as die vraag sê *Almal was reeds daar* -> *Niemand was nog daar nie*.**
*(Simpler clean drill: *Iemand het my sleutels geneem* -> **Niemand het my sleutels geneem nie.**)*
---
Q: Skryf die bevel in die **ontkennende vorm**: *Skakel asseblief die ligte af!* (2)
A: **Moenie asseblief die ligte afskakel nie!** (Of: **Moet asseblief nie die ligte afskakel nie!** — `af + skakel` smelt weer saam as **`afskakel`**!)
```
