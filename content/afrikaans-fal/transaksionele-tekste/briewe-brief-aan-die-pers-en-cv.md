> **What you'll learn:** exact DBE Afrikaans FAL **Vraestel 3 Afdeling B (`Lang transaksionele tekste`, 120–150 woorde, 30 punte)** formats for **Vriendskaplike brief**, **Formele brief** (klagte/versoek/aansoek), **Brief aan die pers**, and **Curriculum Vitae met dekbrief** — including Afrikaans address order, date spelling, salutation, and sign-off conventions (`Die uwe` vs `Groete`!).
>
> **Prerequisites:** formal vs informal register (`jy/jou` vs `u`) and STOMPI basics.
>
> **Why this matters:** Format marks sit inside Content & Planning in Vraestel 3 (`18` marks Content/Format + `12` marks Language = `30`!). Unlike English HL, Afrikaans FAL letters use strict Afrikaans conventions — e.g. **`Posbus 45`** (NOT P.O. Box), **`PRETORIA`** in CAPITALS, **`14 Augustus 2026`** (with NO comma after month!), and **`Die uwe`** for formal letters!

## The idea in plain language

In Afrikaans FAL Vraestel 3, **Afdeling B** asks for ONE **Lang Transaksionele Teks** (**120–150 words** body length, **30 marks**). Because the body is relatively short (~3 crisp paragraphs), **layout accuracy and register consistency** make a massive difference immediately!

## Format 1: Vriendskaplike Brief (Informal Letter)

- **Sender's address ONLY (top right or left — NO name above it!):**
  ```text
  Langstraat 14
  Polokwane
  0699
  18 Augustus 2026
  ```
- **Salutation:** `Beste Lerato` (or `Liewe Ouma`) — **NO comma** in modern Afrikaans open punctuation!
- **Body (120–150 woorde):** Warm, conversational register (`jy / jou`), 3 clear paragraphs answering ALL instructions in the prompt. Never write *"Ek skryf hierdie brief om vir jou te sê..."* — start naturally (*"Jy sal nooit glo wat gister by die skool gebeur het nie!"*).
- **Closing:**
  ```text
  Jou vriend
  Thabo
  ```
  *(Or `Liefde / Groete`, plus **first name only**!)*

## Format 2: Formele Brief & Brief aan die Pers

```steps
title: Exact DBE layout for Formele Brief & Brief aan die Pers
step: **1. Afzender se adres (Sender — NO name above it!):** Street/Posbus, City/Town in capitals or proper case (`KAAPSTAD`), 4-digit postal code, full written date (`20 Augustus 2026`).
step: **2. Ontvanger se adres (left):** Title (`Die Bestuurder` / `Die Skoolhoof` / `Die Redakteur`), organisation/newspaper (*Die Burger*), street/`Posbus`, city, postal code.
step: **3. Aanhef (Salutation):** `Geagte Meneer/Mevrou` OR `Geagte me. Ndlovu` OR **`Meneer/Mevrou`** / **`Geagte Redakteur`** (for Brief aan die Pers!). Use polite **`u / u s'n`**, NEVER `jy`!
step: **4. Onderwerpreël (Subject line — vetdruk of hoofletters, NO full stop):** e.g. **`KLAGTE OOR GEBREEKTE STRAATLIGTE IN KERKSTRAAT`**.
step: **5. Liggaam (120–150 woorde, 3 paragrawe):** State purpose immediately in Para 1 -> give facts/dates/evidence in Para 2 -> request clear action/solution politely in Para 3. *(In Brief aan die Pers: mention the article title/date you are responding to!)*
step: **6. Slot (Sign-off):** Always **`Die uwe`** (NOT *Yours faithfully* or *Groete*!), followed by signature, printed initials + surname (`T. Mokoena`), and suburb if it's a Brief aan die Pers (or pseudonym like `Bekommerde Burger` with real name underneath).
```

```compare
title: Two costly Afrikaans Letter Mistakes vs Correct Form
left: English interference (loses marks!)
- Writing `P.O. Box 120` instead of **`Posbus 120`**.
- Writing `14/08/26` or `August 14, 2026` instead of **`14 Augustus 2026`**.
- Writing `Yours faithfully` / `Vriendelike groete` on a formal complaint letter instead of **`Die uwe`**.
- Mixing `u` and `jy` inside the same formal paragraph (*"U moet asseblief jou rekening nagaan"*).
right: Authentic Afrikaans FAL format
- Address in pure Afrikaans (`Hoofstraat 22`, `Posbus 120`, `Bloemfontein`, `9301`).
- Full Afrikaans month (`Januarie, Februarie, Maart, April, Mei, Junie, Julie, Augustus, September, Oktober, November, Desember`).
- Formal sign-off **`Die uwe`** + signature + `mnr./mej. Voorletter Van`.
- Consistent **`u`** throughout!
```

## Format 3: Curriculum Vitae (CV) & Dekbrief

- **Dekbrief (Covering Letter):** Standard **Formele Brief** layout (`AANSOEK OM DEELTYDSE VAKANSIEWERK`) explaining in 120–150 words *why you fit the role* (do NOT copy your entire CV!).
- **CV:** Clear factual headings in Afrikaans:
  - **Persoonlike Besonderhede:** *Naam en van, Geboortedatum, Identiteitsnommer, Adres, Selfoonnommer, E-posadres, Nasionaliteit, Gesondheid*.
  - **Skoolloopbaan / Opvoeding:** *Hoogste graad geslaag (Graad 10, 2025), Skoolnaam, Vakke*.
  - **Prestasies & Vaardighede:** *Leierskap (VRL), Rekenaarvaardighede, Tale (Afrikaans, Engels...)*
  - **Werksondervinding / Vrywilligerswerk:** *(Indien van toepassing)*
  - **Referente / Getuies:** Two non-family names with title and phone number (`Mnr. P. van Wyk — Skoolhoof — 082 ...`).

## Worked example: Fixing a flawed Brief aan die Pers opening

**Learner draft:**
> `PO Box 55, Durban, 4000, 12/09/2026`
> `Liewe Redakteur,`
> `Jou koerant se artikel gister was baie goed! Ek wil vir jou sê die jongmense gee om vir die omgewing.`
> `Groete, Bongi.`

**Corrected Afrikaans FAL format:**
> `Posbus 55`
> `Durban`
> `4000`
> `12 September 2026`
>
> `Die Redakteur`
> `Die Burger`
> `Posbus 692`
> `Kaapstad`
> `8000`
>
> `Geagte Redakteur` *(of `Meneer`)*
> **`REAKSIE OP ARTIKEL OOR JEUG EN DIE OMGEWING`**
> `Na aanleiding van u artikel "Jeug gee min om" (11 September 2026) wil ek graag reageer...`
> ...
> `Die uwe`
> *(handtekening)*
> `B. Dlamini (Durban)`

```tryit
title: Catch 4 format/register bugs in a Formal Complaint
prompt: Spot and correct **4 errors** in this formal letter outline:
1. Sender block starts with: `Thabo Khumalo / Kerkstraat 18 / 14/08/26`
2. Salutation: `Hallo Meneer,`
3. Sentence in body: `U moet asseblief gou reageer op my brief, anders gaan ek vir jou aankla.`
4. Sign-off: `Jou vriend, Thabo`
hint: Check sender name placement, date format, salutation, `u` vs `jou` consistency, and formal closing!
answer: 1. Remove `Thabo Khumalo` from the top address; write date in full (`14 Augustus 2026`).
2. Change `Hallo Meneer,` to **`Geagte Meneer`** (or `Geagte Meneer/Mevrou`).
3. Keep `u` consistent and stay polite: *"...anders sal ek genoodsaak wees om die saak verder te neem / aan **u** bestuurder te rapporteer."*
4. Change `Jou vriend, Thabo` to **`Die uwe`** + signature + `T. Khumalo`.
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Putting your **own name** at the top above the sender's address (`Thabo Khumalo` belongs ONLY at the **bottom** after `Die uwe`!).
> - Writing **`Geagte Meneer`** and then switching to **`jy/jou`** inside Paragraph 2! Stick strictly to **`u`** in every formal letter.
> - Missing the **Onderwerpreël** (Subject line) on a formal letter, CV covering letter, or letter to the press.

## Practice

```questions
Q: Wat is die korrekte Afrikaanse afsluiting vir 'n **Formele Brief** of **Brief aan die Pers**, en waar staan die skrywer se eie naam? (2)
A: **`Die uwe`** — en die skrywer se naam/handtekening staan slegs **onderaan ná die afsluiting**, nooit bokant die afzenderadres nie.
---
Q: Hoe moet die datum **05/03/2026** korrek binne 'n Afrikaanse briefadres geskryf word? (1)
A: **5 Maart 2026** (voluit, sonder voorloopnul of leesteken).
---
Q: Noem vier vaste opskrifte wat in 'n Afrikaanse **Curriculum Vitae (CV)** moet verskyn. (4)
A: **Persoonlike Besonderhede**, **Opvoeding / Skoolloopbaan**, **Vaardighede en Prestasies** (of Werksondervinding), en **Referente / Getuies**.
```
