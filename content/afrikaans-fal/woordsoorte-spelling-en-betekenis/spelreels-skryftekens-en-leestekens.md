> **What you'll learn:** the exact rules for Afrikaans **skryftekens** (**deelteken `¨`**, **kappie `^`**, **koppelteken `-`**, **afkappingsteken `'`**), **samestellings** (compound nouns written as ONE word vs hyphenated), **afkammings & akronieme**, and **leestekens** (komma voor `want/maar`, kommapunt, dubbelpunt, aanhalingstekens).
>
> **Prerequisites:** basic syllable recognition and vowel combinations (`ee, oo, ie, ei, ou`).
>
> **Why this matters:** Vraestel 1 ALWAYS tests *skryftekens* (*reën, sê, foto's, Suid-Afrikaanse*), compound spelling (*hoërskoolleerder*), and punctuation functions — and clean spelling stops careless mark loss in Vraestel 3 essays!

## The idea in plain language

English breaks compound nouns into spaces (*"history teacher"*), and rarely uses accent marks. Afrikaans **stitches compound nouns together as ONE word** (`geskiedenisonderwyser`) and uses **four specific diacritic marks (`skryftekens`)** whenever two vowels clash or a vowel sound must stretch!

## Part 1: Die Vier Skryftekens (Diacritics)

| Skryfteken | Name | Exact Purpose | Must-Know Examples |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **`¨`** | **Deelteken** | Splits **two adjacent vowels into TWO separate syllables** so they don't blend! | *reën* (`re-ën`), *geëet* (`ge-ëet`), *oë* (`o-ë`), *beïnvloed*, *poësie*, *voëls*, *tweeling? NO — `twee-` stays `twee`, `tweeërlei`*! |
| **`^`** | **Kappie** | Makes a short vowel (`e, o, u, i`) **open and long** (`ê`, `ô`, `û`, `î`)! | *sê* (say), *hê* (have), *lê* (lie), *wêreld*, *môre*, *brûe*, *rûe*, *skêr*, *blêr* |
| **`-`** | **Koppelteken** | Connects compounds to prevent **awkward reading**, long pile-ups, numbers/letters, or repeated vowels! | *Suid-Afrikaanse*, *Graad 11-leerder* / *e-pos*, *sewe-uur*, *koffie-uur* (avoids `ie-u` confusion!), *hoë-druk? -> hoëdruk* |
| **`'`** | **Afkappingsteken** | Shows **omitted letters** (`'n`, `s'n`, `hy's`) OR protects **open stressed vowels `a, i, o, u`** in plurals/diminutives! | *'n* (omitted *ee*), *Pa's*, *foto's*, *taxi's*, *foto'tjie*, *ma'tjie* |

### Wait: Koppelteken vs Deelteken — when do we use which?
- Inside **derived words/roots** where **`ge-` / `be-` / `ver-`** meets a vowel (`ge` + `eet` -> `geëet`), use a **deelteken (`¨`)** on the second vowel (`geëindig`, `beïnvloed`).
- Inside **compounds where two DIFFERENT words collide** cleanly OR **vowels clash weirdly** (*radio-uitsending*, *koffie-eter*, *seekoei-ie?*, *ry-oefening*), OR with **abbreviations/numbers/geographical doubles** (*TV-program*, *16-jarige*, *Noord-Kaap*, *Oos-Londen*), use a **koppelteken (`-`)**!

## Part 2: Samestellings (Compound Nouns) — ONE Word!

In Afrikaans, **two nouns describing ONE single concept ALWAYS join as ONE word**!
- `rugby + bal` -> **`rugbybal`** (NOT `rugby bal`!)
- `skool + hoof` -> **`skoolhoof`** (NOT `skool hoof`!)
- `huis + werk + opdrag` -> **`huiswerkopdrag`**

### When does a linking sound (`-s-` or `-e-`) sneak in?
- **`-s-`** (verbindingsklank): *staat**s**hospitaal*, *pad**s**tekens? -> **padtekens**, `liefde**s**brief`, `tyd**s**krif`, `bevolking**s**groei`, `regering**s**beleid`.
- **`-e-`**: *kind**er**boek*, *hon**de**hok*, *blom**me**tuin*, *peer**bome? -> **pereboom**? NO: **peerboom**!* (Note: *perd -> perdekar*; *boek -> boekrak*).

```compare
  - **`te veel`** / **`te min`** (TWO words!), **`darem`** (1 word), **`asseblief`** (1 word)
  - **`dieselfde`** (ONE word!), **`mekaar`** (1 word)
```

## Part 3: Leestekens (Punctuation Functions in Vraestel 1)

Exams frequently ask: *"Wat is die funksie van die hakies / gedagtestreep / kommapunt / dubbelpunt in reël 4?"*

- **Komma (`,`):**
  1. Separates items in a list (*appels, pere en piesangs* — NO Oxford comma before *en*!).
  2. Goes BEFORE **`want`** and **`maar`** (`Hy leer hard, want hy wil slaag.`).
  3. Separates a fronted **Groep 3 clause** from the main verb (`Toe hy opstaan, het die klok gelui.`).
  4. Marks off **parenthetical extra information** (*Die skoolhoof, mnr. Botha, het gepraat.*).
- **Dubbelpunt (`:`):** Introduces **direct speech** (*Sy sê: "..."*), a **list**, or an **explanation**.
- **Kommapunt (`;`):** Joins two **closely linked independent sentences** without a conjunction, OR goes BEFORE Groep 2 conjunctions (`daarom`, `dus`, `gevolglik`).
- **Hakies `( )` / Gedagtestreep (`— ... —`):** Gives **extra explanatory detail or an aside** (*parentese*).
- **Beletselteken (`...`):** Shows **unfinished thought, hesitation, or suspense**, OR omitted words inside a quote.

## Worked example: Explaining punctuation & fixing spelling in context

**Text snippet:** `'N baie bekende rugby speler het gister geeet in die stadskafee en gese: "Ek drink net water!"`

Let's fix **5 errors**:
1. `'N baie` -> **`'n Baie`** (NEVER capitalise `'N` — capitalise `Baie`!).
2. `rugby speler` -> **`rugbyspeler`** (compound noun = ONE word!).
3. `geeet` -> **`geëet`** (deelteken separates `ge-` from `eet`!).
4. `gese` -> **`gesê`** (kappie stretches `ê`!).
5. *(Note: `stadskafee` is already right — `stad + s + kafee`!)*

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Writing **`'N`** at the beginning of a paragraph (`'N Man...`) instead of **`'n Man...`**.
> - Putting a **kappie** where a **deelteken** belongs (`geêet` is WRONG -> **`geëet`**; `reên` is WRONG -> **`reën`**). Remember: `¨` = two separate syllables (`re-ën`), `^` = one stretched vowel (`mô-re`).
> - Splitting English-style compounds with spaces (`"skool uniform"`, `"water bottel"`) instead of joining them (**`skooluniform`**, **`waterbottel`**).

## Practice

```questions
Q: Wat is die funksie van die **deelteken** in *beïnvloed*, en van die **kappie** in *wêreld*? (2)
A: Die **deelteken** (`¨`) dui aan dat twee aangrensende vokale (`e` en `i`) in **afsonderlike lettergrepe** uitgespreek word (`be-ïn-vloed`). Die **kappie** (`^`) maak die kort vokaal (`e`) **lank** (`wê-reld`).
---
Q: Verbind die woorde tot korrekte **samestellings** (met of sonder `-` / `-s-`): *(a) TV + program*, *(b) verjaarsdag + partytjie*, *(c) koning + ryk*. (3)
A: *(a)* **TV-program**, *(b)* **verjaarsdagpartytjie**, *(c)* **koninkryk** (let op: `g` word `k` -> `koninkryk`!).
---
Q: Watter leesteken plaas jy gewoonlik voor die Groep 2-voegwoord **daarom**? (1)
A: 'n **Kommapunt (`;`)** — bv. *Dit reën; **daarom** bly ons tuis.*
```
