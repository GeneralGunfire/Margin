>
> **Prerequisites:** reading comprehension and paragraph justification skills.
>
> **Why this matters:** Vraestel 2 (`70 marks`) consists ONLY of contextual questions (`kontekstuele vrae`, ~20–25 marks per genre) in Afrikaans FAL — NO long literary essay! ## The idea in plain language

Whether your school studies a Grade 11 *roman*, *kortverhale*, or *drama*, examiners test the **same universal story mechanics** on a printed extract from your prescribed book. Instead of just retelling *"what happened on page 42"*, you must name **HOW the writer creates tension and meaning** using the right Afrikaans literary terms!

## Part 1: Intrige (Plot Structure) & Konflik

Every narrative extract sits somewhere along the classic 5-stage plot arc (**intrige**):

```steps
title: Die 5 Stappe van die Intrige (Plot Arc)
step: **1. Eksposisie (Uiteensetting):** Introduces main characters (`hoofkarakter/protagonis`), setting (`milieu`), and initial situation.
step: **2. Motoriese moment:** The **spark/inciting incident** that breaks normality and kicks off the conflict!
step: **3. Ontwikkeling / Verwikikkeling (Rising action):** Obstacles pile up; **innerlike konflik** (moral struggle inside a character's mind) and **uiterlike konflik** (clash between two people, society, or nature) intensify.
step: **4. Klimaks (Hoogtepunt):** The moment of maximum tension where the main conflict MUST break or decide its direction.
step: **5. Afloop / Ontknoping (Resolution):** Consequences settle; mystery resolves; lesson/change becomes clear.
```

## Part 2: Karakterisering & Vertellersperspektief

### How do writers show character (**karakterisering**)?
Never just state *"Hy is 'n goeie ou"*! Point to:
1. **Wat die karakter SÊ en DOEN** (actions + dialogue).
2. **Wat ander karakters oor hom/haar sê.**
3. **Naamgewing en uiterlike voorkoms** (symbolic names or telling clothing/habits).
- **Ronde karakter** (realistic, complex, changes/grows — e.g. *protagonis*) vs **Plat karakter** (one-dimensional stereotype who stays static).

### Die Drie Belangrikste Vertellers (Narrators)

| Verteller | Pronouns Used | Strengths & Limitations (Exam Question!) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Eerstepersoonsverteller** (*Ek-verteller*) | **`ek / my / ons`** | **Intimate & personal** — shares inner feelings honestly, **BUT subjective/one-sided** (only knows what *ek* sees!). |
| **Alomteenwoordige (Alwetende) Derdepersoonsverteller** | **`hy / sy / hulle`** | Knows **everyone's secret thoughts, past, and future** — objective eagle's-eye view! |
| **Beperkte Derdepersoonsverteller** | **`hy / sy / hulle`** | Follows **only ONE character's thoughts/perspective** closely. |

## Part 3: Drama vs Prosa & Ironie

```compare
title: Prosa vs Drama Conventions — and Dramatic Irony
left: Drama-terme (Toneelstuk)
- **Hoofsteks (Dialoog):** What characters say aloud on stage.
- **Neweteks (Toneelaanwysings):** Words in **brackets/italics** telling actors *tone, facial expression, movement, or props* — NEVER spoken aloud!
- **Alleenspraak (Monoloog / Soliloquy):** Character alone on stage speaking secret thoughts aloud to the audience.
- **Bedryf vs Toneel:** *Bedryf* = major act; *Toneel* = smaller scene change (when a character enters/exits or setting shifts).
right: Ironie & Dramatiese Ironie
- **Verbale / Situasie-ironie:** Outcome turns out the **opposite** of what was expected or said (e.g. locking a door to feel safe while the thief is already inside).
- **Dramatiese Ironie:** **The reader/audience KNOWS a vital secret that the character on stage/in the story does NOT know yet!** Creates intense suspense or sympathy.
```

### Ruimte/Milieu, Stemming, Tema & Boodskap
- **Ruimte / Milieu:** Physical place AND social/historical atmosphere (*e.g. 'n koue sinkhuisie, skoolterrein, 1980s*). **Belangrik:** Atmosphere (**stemming**) often mirrors conflict (stormy rain during an argument!).
- **Tema (Theme):** Abstract big idea in 1–3 words (*verraad, vriendskap, vooroordeel, vergifnis*).
- **Boodskap (Message):** What the story teaches us in a **full statement** (*"Ware vriendskap verg eerlikheid selfs wanneer dit moeilik is."*).

## Worked example: Decoding a contextual extract question set

**Extract snippet (Prosa):** *Sipho staar na die toegesluite hek. Sy hande sweet terwyl die koue wind deur sy dun baadjie sny. "Ek kan nie nou omdraai nie," fluister hy vir homself, al weet sy suster glad nie hy het haar spaargeld geleen nie.*

1. *Identifiseer die verteller en motiveer.* (2) -> **Derdepersoonsverteller**, want daar word van `"Sipho"` / `"hy"` gepraat (en alwetend omdat die verteller Sipho se geheime gedagtes/optrede ken).
2. *Watter tipe konflik ervaar Sipho hier? Motiveer.* (2) -> **Innerlike konflik** — hy twyfel en voel skuldig/bang in homself oor sy besluit nadat hy sy suster se geld gevat het.
3. *Noem EEN voorbeeld van hoe die **milieu/weer** by Sipho se **stemming** pas.* (2) -> Die **koue wind** beklemtoon sy eensaamheid, spanning en ongemaklike vrees.

```tryit
title: Distinguish Neweteks vs Dramatiese Ironie vs Tema/Boodskap
prompt: Beantwoord hierdie drie kort letterkundige vrae:
1. Waarom mag 'n akteur **NIE** die woorde tussen hakies — `(kyk senuweeagtig om haar rond)` — hardop lees nie?
2. Wat is die verskil tussen 'n verhaal se **tema** en sy **boodskap**?
3. Gee 'n voorbeeld van **dramatiese ironie** uit enige denkbeeldige toneel.
hint: Think of exact Afrikaans labels (`neweteks`, abstract word vs full lesson sentence, audience knows something the character doesn't!).
answer: 1. Dit is **neweteks / toneelaanwysings** wat slegs die akteur se liggaamstaal, gevoel of beweging aandui.
2. 'n **Tema** is die kernonderwerp in een of twee woorde (bv. *eerlikheid*); die **boodskap** is die lewensles as 'n volsin (*"Eerlikheid wen uiteindelik vertroue."*).
3. Die gehoor weet die brief in die koevert bevat slegte nuus, terwyl die karakter vrolik feesvier omdat hy dink hy het gewen.
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Answering *"Wat is die **tema**?"* with a whole plot summary — keep *tema* short and abstract (`vergifnis`, `armmoede`, `identiteit`) unless asked to explain it!
> - Mixing up **innerlike konflik** (internal emotional struggle/guilt/decision) with **uiterlike konflik** (argument, fight, storm, financial obstacle).
> - Calling an **`ek-verteller`** *"alwetend"* — a first-person narrator can ONLY see through their own eyes (`subjektief`)!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Plaas die volgende drie dele van 'n verhaal se **intrige** in die regte volgorde: *klimaks, eksposisie, ontknoping, motoriese moment*. (2)
A: **Eksposisie -> motoriese moment -> klimaks -> ontknoping.**
---
Q: Hoekom gebruik 'n dramaturg **neweteks (toneelaanwysings)** in 'n drama? Noem TWEE funksies. (2)
A: 1. Om aan te dui **hoe 'n karakter moet praat of beweeg** (stemtoon/gesigsuitdrukking/liggaamstaal). 2. Om die **ruimte, beligting, klankeffekte of rekwisiete** op die verhoog te beskryf.
---
Q: Wat beteken die begrip **karakterontwikkeling**? (2)
A: Wanneer 'n karakter deur die loop van die verhaal **innerlik groei, verander of 'n nuwe insig kry** as gevolg van die gebeure of konflik (kenmerkend van 'n **ronde karakter**).
```
