> **What you'll learn:** how to decode any Grade 11 Afrikaans poem in **Vraestel 2** — **uiterlike bou** (strofes, rympatrone `aabb / abab / abba`, vrye vers, enjambement, refrein), **klankeffekte** (alliterasie, assonansie, klanknabootsing), **beeldspraak & stylfigure** (vergelyking, metafoor, personifikasie, kontras, hiperbool, eufemisme), and **stemming / toon / boodskap**!
>
> **Prerequisites:** literal vs figurative meaning and basic parts of speech.
>
> **Why this matters:** Poetry contextual questions (`~30–35 marks` in Paper 2 across two prescribed poems!) use predictable technical terms. If a question asks *"Wat is die funksie van die **enjambement** in strofe 2?"* or *"Identifiseer die **rymskema**"* and you know the exact answer formula, you pick up marks fast!

## The idea in plain language

A poem compresses maximum emotion into minimum words. To analyse any poem in Vraestel 2 without guessing, always look through **two lenses**:
1. **Uiterlike bou (Outer structure — what your eyes see & ears hear):** stanza length (`kwatryn = 4 reëls`, `koeplet = 2`, `terset = 3`, `sestet = 6`, `oktief = 8`), rhyme scheme (`rymskema`), flow across line breaks (`enjambement`), and sound repetition (`alliterasie / assonansie`).
2. **Innerlike bou (Inner meaning — what the words make you feel/picture):** imagery (`beeldspraak`), contrast (`kontras`), mood (`stemming`), speaker's tone (`toon`), and core message (`tema/boodskap`).

## Part 1: Uiterlike Bou & Rympatrone

### How to determine a Rymskema in 10 seconds
Look **ONLY at the LAST syllable/word of each line** inside a stanza. Label the first end-sound **`a`**. Every subsequent line ending with that exact rhyme gets **`a`**; the first new sound gets **`b`**, and so on:
- **`a a b b`** -> **Paarrym** (Pair rhyme — bouncy, songlike, quick pace).
- **`a b a b`** -> **Kruisrym** (Cross rhyme — balanced, steady rhythm).
- **`a b b a`** -> **Omarmde rym** (Enclosed rhyme — creates a protective/trapped or reflective frame!).
- **`a a a a`** -> **Gebroke? Nee — Slagrym / Monorym**; irregular varied rhyme = **Gebroke rym**; NO rhyme pattern & irregular line length = **Vrye vers (Free verse)** (conversational, modern, free!).

### Five High-Frequency Poetic Structure Terms

| Term | How to Spot It | Exam Function ("Wat is die funksie?") |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Enjambement** | **NO punctuation** at the end of a line — the sentence flows straight onto the next line! | Speeds up tempo (`versnel die tempo`), mimics unbroken movement/breathing, or **connects two ideas** tightly. |
| **Alleenplasing** | A **single word** (or very short phrase) standing on its own line! | Places **heavy emphasis (`beklemtoning`)** on that specific word or creates dramatic silence/isolation. |
| **Herhaling / Refrein** | Exact word, phrase, or chorus line repeated across stanzas. | Reinforces the central emotion/theme and creates musical rhythm. |
| **Wending (Volta)** | Sudden shift in mood, time, or perspective (often signalled by **`Maar...`** or a new stanza!). | Shows contrast between past and present, illusion vs reality, or sadness turning to hope. |
| **Tipografie** | Unusual spacing, italics, capitals, or indentation on the page. | Visually mirrors meaning (e.g. staggered lines looking like falling rain or steps). |

## Part 2: Klankeffekte (Sound Devices) & Beeldspraak

```compare
title: Alliterasie vs Assonansie (and how to quote proof!)
left: Alliterasie (Konsonant-herhaling!)
- Repetition of the **same CONSONANT sound** at the start of nearby words.
- Example: *"Die **w**ilde **w**ind **w**aai..."* (`w`-klank) or *"stille **s**troom **s**laap..."* (`s`-klank).
- **Function:** Soft `s/w/l` slows/calms; hard `k/t/p/b` creates harshness, tension, or violence!
right: Assonansie (Vokaal-herhaling!)
- Repetition of the **same VOWEL sound** inside nearby stressed words!
- Example: *"D**ie** r**ie**t w**ie**g st**i**l..."* (`ie`-klank) or *"h**oo**g b**o**..."* (`oo`-klank).
- **Function:** Long vowels (`aa, oo, ee, ie`) stretch the tempo and create a sad, peaceful, or dreamy mood; short vowels speed things up!
```

### Must-Know Stylfigure (Figures of Speech)
- **Klanknabootsing (Onomatopee):** Words imitating real sound (*zoem, kraak, sis, dreun, ritsel*).
- **Kontras / Antitese:** Two sharp opposites placed side by side (*lig vs donker*, *lag vs huil*) to highlight conflict.
- **Hiperbool:** Deliberate exaggeration for emphasis (*"Ek het 'n miljoen keer gevra"*).
- **Eufemisme:** Soft, gentle phrasing for a harsh/sad reality (*"hy het heengegaan"* instead of *hy is dood*).
- **Retoriese vraag:** A question asked to make the reader think, NOT expecting an out-loud answer.

## Worked example: Answering a 6-mark unseen-style contextual poem drill

**Mini-strofe:**
> *Die stad slaap stil onder swaar silwer lug,* (a)
> *net een straatlig* (b)
> *bibber alleen* (c)
> *teen die naderende nag.* (d)

1. *Identifiseer die **alliterasie** in reël 1 en sê watter stemming dit skep.* (2)
   > **Antwoord:** Herhaling van die **`s`-klank** (`stad slaap stil ... swaar silwer`) — skep 'n sagte, stil, geheimsinnige nagstemming.
2. *Watter twee poëtiese tegnieke kom in reëls 2–3 voor, en wat is die effek?* (3)
   > **Antwoord:** **Enjambement** (tussen reël 2 en 3 is geen leesteken nie — dit laat die gedagte vloei) én **personifikasie** (`"straatlig bibber alleen"` — die lig kry menslike koue/eensaamheid).
3. *Watter verskil maak die kort reël `"bibber alleen"` (alleenplasing/kort reël)?* (1)
   > **Antwoord:** Dit **beklemtoon die straatlig se eensaamheid en kwesbaarheid**.

```tryit
title: Identify Rymskema, Sound Device, and Beeldspraak
prompt: Bestudeer hierdie kort versreëls:
> *Die groot geel son lankal moeg gesak,*
> *die koel aandwind fluister deur elke tak.*
1. Watter **rympatroon** het hierdie koeplet (`aa` of `ab`)?
2. Benoem die **beeldspraak** in *"die koel aandwind fluister"*.
3. Watter **klankeffek** kom in *"groot geel"* voor (`alliterasie` of `assonansie`)?
hint: Check end words (`gesak / tak`), human action given to wind, and starting consonant `g`!
answer: 1. **Paarrym (`aa`)** — *gesak* rym met *tak*.
2. **Personifikasie** — aan die *aandwind* word die menslike handeling van *fluister* gegee.
3. **Alliterasie** van die `g`-klank (*groot geel*).
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Quoting **assonance** when asked for **alliteration** — remember: **A**lliterasie = **K**onsonante (start consonants), **A**ssonansie = **V**okale (matching vowel *sounds*, NOT just spelling — e.g. `o` and `oo` don't match!).
> - Writing `"Enjambement verbind sinne"` without linking to **tempo, vloei, or a specific word/idea** in that poem! Always mention the poem's actual idea for the 2nd mark.
> - Counting **capital letters (`Aa`)** differently in a rhyme scheme — always label rhyme letters in **lowercase (`aabb`)**!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Wat is die naam van 'n vierreëlige strofe, en hoe lyk **omarmde rym** se letterpatroon? (2)
A: 'n **Kwatryn** (4 reëls); **omarmde rym** is **`a b b a`**.
---
Q: Hoe verskil **vrye vers** van 'n tradisionele sonnet of rymende gedig? (2)
A: **Vrye vers** het **geen vaste rympatroon** of vaste rymlengte/metrum nie — dit lees vryer en meer soos natuurlike spraak.
---
Q: Gee die Afrikaanse literêre term vir: *(a)* 'n woord wat 'n regte geluid naboots, en *(b)* twee teenoorgestelde idees langs mekaar geplaas. (2)
A: *(a)* **Klanknabootsing / Onomatopee**, *(b)* **Kontras / Antitese**.
```
