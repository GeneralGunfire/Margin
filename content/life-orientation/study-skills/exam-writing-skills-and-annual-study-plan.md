> **What you'll learn:** the **CAPS school assessment process** (*SBA tasks, PAT/PET, mid-year & final exams, informal vs formal assessment*), proven **examination-writing skills** (*before, during reading time, answering action verbs accurately, pacing marks per minute, managing exam panic*), building an **annual study plan** across Terms 1–4, and setting integrated **personal study, health, and fitness goals** (*CAPS Term 2 · LO*).
>
> **Prerequisites:** Lesson 311 (`study strategies & weekly time management`).
>
> **Why this matters:** Knowing the syllabus is only **70%** of the mark — the other **30%** is **exam craft**! Learners regularly lose huge marks simply by **misreading action verbs** (*writing 1 word when asked to "Discuss", or narrating a story when asked to "Evaluate"*) or **running out of time on the final 15-mark essay**!

## The idea in plain language

Have you ever left an exam room thinking *"Wait — I knew that answer! Why did I only score 55%?!"*
Usually, three sneaky mistakes stole those marks:
1. Writing **what you felt like writing** instead of obeying the **exact command verb** (*Define vs Compare vs Evaluate vs Justify*).
2. Spending **35 minutes polishing Question 1** (`worth 10 marks`), leaving **5 frantic minutes** for Question 5 (`worth 25 marks`)!
3. Waiting until **two weeks before November finals** to open Term 1 notes (**no annual study plan**)!

## Part 1: Understanding the Assessment Process (`Informal vs Formal`)

- **Informal Assessment (`Assessment FOR Learning`):** Daily class quizzes, homework checks, peer marking, oral Q&A (`doesn't enter final promotion marks directly, YET tells you immediately what to fix before test day!`).
- **Formal Assessment (`Assessment OF Learning` — `SBA + PAT/PET + Exams`):** Recorded official promotion tasks across Terms 1–4 (`projects, controlled tests, presentations, mid-year/final exams`, plus **PAT** in Tourism or **PET** in LO!). *Ignoring Term 1–3 SBA tasks ruins your 25% year mark before November even starts!*

## Part 2: Mastering Exam Command Verbs (`The Secret Mark Decoder!`)

| Command Verb | What Markers Actually Expect You to Write | Trap to Avoid |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **List / Name / State** | Crisp numbered items or brief factual statements (`no long essay paragraphs needed!`). | Writing half a page for a 2-mark *"List two"* question and running out of time later! |
| **Define / Explain** | Exact clear meaning (`what it is + why/how it works` in full sentences). | Repeating the word inside its definition (`"accountability is when you are accountable"` = 0!). |
| **Compare / Contrast / Distinguish** | Show **similarities and/or clear differences** side by side (`use linking words: whereas, unlike, however`). | Writing about Option A in Paragraph 1 without ever linking it directly to Option B! |
| **Discuss / Analyse** | Unpack causes, effects, advantages/disadvantages, or how parts fit together using evidence/examples. | Giving a one-word bullet list with zero explanation. |
| **Evaluate / Critically Discuss / Advise / Justify** | **Weigh pros AND cons** (`strengths vs limits`), link directly to the **case study**, and give a **reasoned verdict/recommendation**! | Taking a blind one-sided rant without evidence or practical reasons! |

## Part 3: Exam-Day Technique & Pacing Rule

```steps
title: Five steps from 10-minute reading time to final review
step: **1. Use Reading Time (`First 10 min`) smartly:** Read EVERY instruction (`choose 2 of 3? compulsory?`), underline key command verbs and mark totals, and pick your strongest essay/optional questions right away (`brain starts retrieving quietly in the background!`).
step: **2. Apply the "1 Mark = ~1 Minute" Pacing Rule:** In a **90-minute, 80-mark LO paper** (`or 120-min, 150-mark Business paper`), spend **roughly 1 minute per mark** (`15-mark essay = ~15–18 min max!`), reserving **10 minutes at the end to check blanks & units/calculations**.
step: **3. Tackle easy confident questions early:** Answer high-confidence short items first to build momentum (`unless clearly instructed to answer Section A first`), leaving clear blank space if temporarily stuck — never freeze for 12 minutes on one 2-mark MCQ!
step: **4. Calm exam panic cleanly:** If your mind blanks, close your eyes for **10 seconds**, breathe slowly (`4s in, 6s out`), read the question calmly word by word, and jot key concepts on the margin/back to unlock memory!
step: **5. Final 5–10 minute audit:** Check every sub-question letter (`a, b, c`), currency/decimal units (`2 decimal places!`), and neat clear numbering (`markers can't award hidden points!`).
```

## Part 4: Building an Annual Study Plan & Personal Development Goals

An **Annual Study Plan** zooms out across the entire Grade 11 calendar (**Terms 1 to 4**) so SBA tasks, projects, mid-year exams, and finals never collide at once:
- **Term 1 (`Jan–Mar`):** Master foundational habits (`weekly study slots`), complete Source/Skills SBA early, review Term 1 test gaps during March holidays!
- **Term 2 (`Apr–Jun`):** Start Term 2/3 long Projects early (`LO / History / PAT Phase 1`), build 3-week countdown revision before **June Mid-Year Exams** (`50%+ of the year's syllabus!`).
- **Term 3 (`Jul–Sep`):** Complete major Term 3 **Projects & PAT Phase 2** calmly in July/August before September Controlled Tests hit.
- **Term 4 (`Oct–Nov`):** Follow a balanced **subject-by-subject countdown timetable** combining **past papers + health/fitness goals** (`30–45 min brisk exercise, clean meals, 8h sleep — a fit body protects exam stamina!`).

## Worked example: Fixing Exam Mistakes & Pacing an 80-Mark Paper

**Prompt:** *In an **80-mark (`90-minute`) Life Orientation exam**: **Section A = 20 marks**, **Section B = 30 marks**, **Section C = 30 marks (`two 15-mark essays`)**. A learner spends 45 minutes perfecting Section A and only 10 minutes each on the two 15-mark essays (`scoring 6/30 in Section C!`).*

1. *Calculate a balanced **time budget** for Sections A, B, and C + checking (`4`).* ->
   - **Section A (`20 marks`):** **~20 minutes** (`quick crisp factual answers`)
   - **Section B (`30 marks`):** **~30 minutes** (`source/scenario analysis`)
   - **Section C (`2 × 15 = 30 marks`):** **~32 minutes (`16 minutes per essay`)**
   - **Final check/proofread:** **~8 minutes** (`Total = 90 minutes!`).
2. *Spot the command-verb mistake: A Section C prompt asks **"Critically evaluate THREE strategies to reduce exam anxiety (`3 × 4 = 12 marks`)"**, and the learner writes ONLY *"1. Sleep well. 2. Breathe deeply. 3. Plan early." (`3/12`!). Explain why she lost 9 marks (`3`).* -> She treated **`Critically evaluate`** as **`List`**! Each strategy needed: **(a) clear explanation (`how it works`)**, **(b) positive impact (`why it helps`)**, AND **(c) realistic limitation/condition** (`e.g. breathing calms panic in the room, yet cannot replace weeks of prior revision!`).

```tryit
title: Command Verb & Time Pacing Check
prompt: Answer quickly:
1. You have a **150-mark, 120-minute Business Studies paper**. Roughly how long (`maximum!`) should you allocate to a **40-mark Section C essay**?
2. Which command verb asks you to **show clear differences** between two options, vs **weigh strengths/limitations to reach a justified verdict**?
hint: `(40 / 150) × 120 min`; Compare/Distinguish vs Evaluate!
answer: 1. **~30–32 minutes** (`leaving ~20 min for Section A [30 marks], ~60–65 min for Section B [80 marks], and 5 min review`). 2. **Distinguish / Contrast** vs **Evaluate / Critically discuss**.
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Repeating the exact question words as your answer (`"Define study plan: a plan for studying"`) — always give **concrete, non-circular explanations**!
> - Abandoning **fitness, water, and sleep** during exam months under an **Annual Study Plan** — skipping movement and rest elevates cortisol and causes silly reading errors in exams!
> - Leaving optional choices unanswered (`or answering ALL 3 essays when instructed "Choose ANY TWO"`, wasting 25 minutes on marks the examiner ignores!).

## Practice

```questions
Q: Explain the difference between **informal assessment** and **formal assessment (`SBA + exams`)**, stating why informal class tasks still matter. (4)
A: **Formal assessment** (`controlled tests, projects, PAT/PET, exams`) counts directly toward term and year promotion marks, whereas **informal assessment** (`homework, class quizzes, peer checks`) carries no formal promotion weight yet gives immediate feedback to fix misunderstandings *before* formal exams!
---
Q: Contrast what a marker expects for **"Name TWO..." (`2 marks`)** versus **"Critically evaluate TWO..." (`8 marks`)**. (4)
A: **"Name TWO"** requires only two brief, accurate items without elaboration. **"Critically evaluate TWO"** requires full explanations of **both strengths (`how/why it works`) and limitations/challenges**, ending with a balanced reasoned judgment linked to the context.
---
Q: Suggest THREE practical ways a learner's **annual study plan** can integrate **study, health, and fitness goals** cleanly across Term 4 exam season. (6)
A: 1. Schedule **daily 30–45 minute brisk exercise (`walking, skipping, sport`)** between afternoon study blocks to clear stress hormones and sharpen focus. 2. Set a strict **device-off bedtime (`7.5–8h sleep`)** instead of all-night cramming. 3. Start **spaced past-paper revision 4–5 weeks before finals** (`avoiding last-minute panic and junk-food bingeing!`).
```
