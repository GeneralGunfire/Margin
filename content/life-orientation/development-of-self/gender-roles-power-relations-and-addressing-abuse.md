## The idea in plain language

**Biological sex** refers to physical anatomy; **gender roles** are the **social rules society teaches** about how women and men *"should"* dress, behave, earn, or hold power (*"Men must always dominate and hide tears; women must obey silently and do all unpaid care work"*).

When gender roles hand **unchecked power to one person over another**, healthy partnership breaks down into **control, fear, and abuse** — harming everyone's physical and mental health!

## Part 1: Gender Roles & Health / Well-Being

- **Harm to women and girls:** Financial dependency (`fewer career/leadership openings`), heavy double-burden (`paid work + 100% housework`), pressure to endure mistreatment silently *"to keep the peace"*.
- **Harm to men and boys:** Toxic pressure to prove masculinity through aggression, reckless driving, binge drinking, or **refusing medical/mental-health help** (`higher rates of untreated depression, heart strain, and violence`).
- **Healthy gender equality:** Shared household responsibility, equal pay/educational opportunity, mutual consent, and freedom for **every person** to show empathy, ambition, and vulnerability safely!

## Part 2: Forms of Unequal Power Relations (`CAPS Core Definitions`)

| Form of Unequal Power | What It Means (`Exact CAPS Recognition`) | Legal & Ethical Reality (`SA Law`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Domestic & Physical Abuse** | Patterns of hitting, pushing, trapping, breaking property, **financial control (`hiding money/bank cards`)**, or terrifying threats inside home/dating relationships. | Illegal under the **Domestic Violence Act (`DVA`, amended 2021)** — victims (`any gender`) can obtain an urgent **Protection Order** free at Magistrate's Courts! |
| **Sexual Violence & Rape** | **Any forced, coerced, drugged, unconscious, or non-consensual sexual act** (`even inside marriage or dating!` — *"yes yesterday is NOT consent today"*). | Serious criminal offence (`Criminal Law Sexual Offences Act`); report immediately to **SAPS / Thuthuzela Care Centres** (`get free **PEP within 72 hours** to prevent HIV + emergency contraception + trauma counselling!`). |
| **Incest** | Sexual abuse or intercourse between **close family relatives** (`where trust and dependency are deeply betrayed`), severely damaging youth safety and trust. | Strictly criminal (`report safely to **Childline 116 / social worker / SAPS FCS unit**` — it is **NEVER the child's fault!**). |
| **Workplace Sexual Harassment** | Unwelcome sexual advances, lewd comments, touching, or **quid-pro-quo threats** (*"sleep with me or lose your shift/promotion"*) by bosses, colleagues, or clients. | Illegal under **Employment Equity Act (`EEA`)** — violates dignity and safety; report via internal HR grievance, union, or **CCMA**! |
| **Unequal Cultural Practices** | Customs applied unfairly by gender (`e.g. **strict long mourning periods / widowhood restrictions** imposed only on women while widowers remarry freely; forced early marriage / *ukuthwala* abduction; denying daughters inheritance`). | South Africa's Constitution (**Section 9 Equality & Section 30/31 Culture**) protects living culture **ONLY where consistent with human dignity and non-discrimination**! |

## Part 3: Health Impacts of Abuse & Addressing Power Inequality

```compare
title: Effects on Health vs Breaking the Cycle of Inequality
left: Negative Effects on Health & Well-Being
- **Physical harm:** Bruises, fractures, chronic headaches, STIs/HIV, unintended pregnancy, ulcers/fatigue.
- **Psychological & emotional trauma:** **PTSD**, nightmares, severe anxiety, depression, shame, self-blame, failing grades, substance coping.
- **Social/economic harm:** Dropping out of school/work, losing income independence, isolating from loving relatives.
right: Addressing Power Inequality & Seeking Safety
- **Empowerment & education:** Teach boys and girls **equal dignity, consent, and bystander intervention** early (`never excuse violence with "boys will be boys"`).
- **Financial & legal agency:** Keep own ID, emergency savings, and trusted contacts; apply for a **Protection Order (`DVA`)**.
- **Community & workplace accountability:** Enforce clear zero-tolerance **sexual harassment policies** at schools and workplaces; support survivors warmly **without victim-blaming**!
```

### Urgent, Confidential Support Services (`South Africa`)
- **GBV (`Gender-Based Violence`) Command Centre:** **`0800 428 428`** (`24/7 toll-free`) or SMS *"help"* to `31531`.
- **Childline SA:** **`116`** (`toll-free`).
- **SAPS Emergency:** **`10111`** (`ask for the Family Violence, Child Protection & Sexual Offences [FCS] unit`) or nearest **Thuthuzela Care Centre** at public hospitals (`24/7 medical care, PEP within 72 hours, forensic & social-worker support!`).

## Worked example: Analysing Power Abuse & Advising Safely (`Section C`)

**Scenario:** *Thandi (`17`) takes a weekend cashier job to help her family. Her male supervisor texts late-night messages promising extra overtime pay if she dates him, and threatens to fire her when she refuses (`Situation A`). Meanwhile, her older brother mocks Thandi's classmate Sipho for crying after his grandmother passed away (`Situation B`).*

1. *Identify the **exact power inequality** in `Situation A` (`1`) and cite **TWO lawful steps** Thandi should take (`4`).* -> **Workplace sexual harassment (`quid-pro-quo coercion`)**.
   - **Step 1:** **Screenshot and save every text message, date, and witness note** (`evidence!`).
   - **Step 2:** Report formally to the **store owner / HR manager / union** (`or **CCMA / Department of Labour** if the boss ignores it` — protected under the **Employment Equity Act**) and tell a trusted parent/teacher so she is never isolated on late shifts!
2. *Explain how **Situation B** harms boys' well-being (`2`) and how to challenge that norm (`2`).* -> Mocking boys for normal grief forces men to suppress emotions until distress explodes into anger, depression, or silent crisis; **respecting honest grief teaches that emotional vulnerability is human strength, not weakness**!

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Saying *"abuse only happens between married adults"* or *"only physical hitting counts as domestic violence"* — **emotional, verbal, financial, sexual, and digital stalking/control** are all forms of **abuse** under the Domestic Violence Act (`and teens face dating coercion too`)!
> - Condemning **all cultural mourning traditions** rather than distinguishing between **respectful voluntary grief customs** and **unequal practices that strip women of dignity, land, or safety**.
> - Blaming victims in scenarios (`"she shouldn't have accepted a lift"`) — **responsibility for violence lies 100% with the perpetrator**.

## Practice

```questions
Q: Explain TWO ways rigid **gender stereotypes** negatively affect the health or opportunities of **(a) girls/women** and **(b) boys/men**. (4)
A: *(a) Girls/women:* Expected submissiveness or unequal unpaid household burdens reduce career confidence and trap survivors in financially dependent abusive relationships. *(b) Boys/men:* Pressure to look "tough" discourages seeking medical or mental-health care early and fuels risky aggression.
---
Q: Distinguish between **domestic physical abuse** and **workplace sexual harassment**, naming ONE legal protection for each in South Africa. (4)
A: **Domestic physical abuse** involves bodily harm or intimidation inside home/intimate relationships (`protected via a Magistrate's Court Protection Order under the Domestic Violence Act`). **Workplace sexual harassment** involves unwelcome sexual advances or job-linked coercion at work (`prohibited under the Employment Equity Act / CCMA`).
---
Q: Recommend THREE constructive actions a high school or community can take to reduce **unequal gender power relations**. (6)
A: 1. Run respectful workshops teaching **affirmative consent, bystander courage, and healthy masculinity** to all learners. 2. Display clear, confidential reporting channels (**Childline `116`, GBV Command Centre `0800 428 428`, school social worker**) without stigma. 3. Enforce strict anti-bullying/anti-harassment rules equally across learners and staff.
```
