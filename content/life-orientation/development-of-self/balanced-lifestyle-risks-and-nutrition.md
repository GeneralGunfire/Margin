## The idea in plain language

## Part 1: Negative Factors & Accidents Threatening Well-Being

*(Note: Substance use, sexual health, STIs/HIV, teenage pregnancy, and mental-health crisis support are explored respectfully in depth in Lesson 122!)*

| Factor | Specific Grade 11 Risks (`CAPS`) | Impact on Life & Studies |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Road & Transport Risks** | Refusing **seatbelts** (back or front!), texting/headphones while crossing roads, riding with **drunk or speeding drivers**, unroadworthy taxis/scooters. | Leading cause of youth disability/death (`preventable head/spinal trauma`). |
| **Personal Safety & Accidents** | Walking alone late at night displaying phones, unsafe DIY electrical wiring, skipping protective helmets/lifejackets during recreation. | Severe injury, theft trauma, missed school terms. |

| **Socio-Economic Environment** | Unsafe neighbourhoods without streetlights/parks, overcrowded housing, unemployment stress limiting fresh-food budgets (`food deserts`). | Increases exposure to injury and cheap ultra-processed snacks — *yet community gardens, school sport, and smart budgeting still protect health!* |

## Part 3: Role of Nutrition in Health & Physical Activity

Food is **fuel + building material + immune armour** — especially for active Grade 11 teenagers!

```steps
title: Core nutrition principles for energy, study focus & sport (`SA Food-Based Guidelines`)
step: **1. Balance macronutrients smartly:**
- **Complex carbohydrates (`Energy`):** Wholegrains (`brown rice, oats, wholewheat bread, samp, sweet potato`) release steady glucose for brains and running — unlike sugary drinks that spike and crash!
- **Proteins (`Muscle repair & growth`):** Lean meat, fish, eggs, milk/maas, **affordable dry beans, lentils, chickpeas, soya**!
- **Healthy fats (`Hormones & cell health`):** Avocado, nuts, olive/sunflower oil, fatty fish (`pilchards/sardines`) — limit deep-fried trans fats.
step: **2. Protect immunity with Micronutrients & Fibre:** Eat colourful **vegetables & fruits daily** (`vitamin C, iron, potassium, folate, fibre`) — low iron causes exhaustion and brain fog in class!
step: **3. Hydration first (`Water over sugar`):** Drink **6–8 glasses of clean water daily** (`more during PE/sport`); sugary sodas and high-caffeine energy drinks cause dehydration, jitters, and tooth decay!
step: **4. Meal timing around physical activity:** Eat a light balanced meal `~2–3 hours` before vigorous sport, and rehydrate + combine **carbs + protein** afterwards (`e.g. peanut butter sandwich + banana + water`) to repair muscles!
```

## Worked example: Diagnosing Lifestyle Imbalance & Advising Smart Nutrition (`Section C`)

**Scenario:** *Jared (`16`) plays Saturday rugby and aims for top Matric grades. On weekdays he skips breakfast, buys a pie and a 500 ml high-caffeine energy drink at break, plays video games until `01:30`, and refuses to wear a seatbelt in his friend's car *"because we're only driving 5 km"*.*

1. *Identify **TWO lifestyle facets** harmed in Jared's routine (`2`) and **TWO dangerous risks** (`2`).* -> **Physical & Psychological well-being** are harmed (`chronic sleep deprivation + sugar/caffeine crashes + zero steady breakfast brain-fuel`); **Risks:** Unhealthy ultra-processed nutrition (`energy drinks/pies`) + **unsafe road behaviour** (`skipping seatbelt even on short 5 km local trips where most crashes happen!`).
2. *Suggest **THREE practical, affordable changes** Jared can start Monday (`6`).* ->
   - **Breakfast & Break Swap:** Eat **oats or peanut butter on brown toast** (`slow-release energy`) and pack **water + fruit/boiled eggs** instead of energy drinks (`saves money too!`).
   - **Sleep Hygiene:** Switch screens off at `22:00` to lock in **8 hours' sleep** for rugby recovery and memory consolidation.
   - **Zero-Exception Seatbelt Rule:** Buckle up every trip regardless of distance (`physics doesn't care if a crash happens 500 m from home!`).

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Defining **Spiritual well-being** narrowly as *"going to church only"* — in LO CAPS it covers **values, inner meaning, ethics, and peace** across religious *or* personal philosophical worldviews!
> - Recommending **expensive trendy supplements or protein powders** to active teens instead of **affordable whole South African foods (`beans, eggs, pilchards, oats, spinach, maas`)**.
> - Blaming *only* poverty for poor lifestyle choices — acknowledge real **socio-economic barriers**, **yet** highlight practical low-cost personal agency (`seatbelts, sleep, water, local greens, clinic check-ups`)!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Name the **five facets of a balanced, healthy lifestyle** and briefly explain how **psychological** well-being differs from **emotional** well-being. (7)
A: **Physical, Psychological, Social, Emotional, and Spiritual**. **Psychological** well-being focuses on clear thinking, stress management, and mental resilience, whereas **emotional** well-being centres on recognising, expressing, and regulating feelings (`anger, sadness, empathy`) constructively in relationships.
---
Q: Give TWO reasons why **high-caffeine energy drinks and skipped breakfasts** harm BOTH classroom concentration and sports performance. (4)
A: 1. Skipping breakfast deprives the brain and muscles of steady glycogen fuel, causing fatigue and weak focus. 2. Energy drinks flood the body with temporary sugar/caffeine spikes followed by steep energy crashes, rapid heartbeat, poor sleep, and dehydration during sport!
---
Q: Explain TWO **positive protective factors** (`outside school rules`) that help Grade 11 teenagers resist unsafe road and lifestyle behaviour. (4)
A: 1. **Positive peer & parental support:** Friends who plan safe lift-shares (`no drunk driving`) and parents who keep open honest communication. 2. **Clear personal values & role models:** Admiring disciplined mentors and holding personal integrity keeps long-term goals stronger than momentary peer pressure.
```
