>
> **Prerequisites:** Lesson 511–512 (`career routes & funding`).
>
> **Why this matters:** Hiring managers spend **`6–10 seconds`** scanning a CV before tossing careless applications aside! Term 3 LO tests practical job-hunting documents **plus** the ethical habits (`transparency, accountability, honesty`) that keep you employable once hired.

## The idea in plain language

A **CV and application letter** are **not an autobiography of every birthday since Grade 1**! They are a **tailored marketing toolkit** showing one specific employer in **1–2 clean pages**: *"Here are your advert's exact requirements, and here is real proof (`from school leadership, volunteering, part-time work, and referees`) that I can deliver them reliably."*

## Part 1: How to Dissect a Job Advertisement (`Before Clicking Send!`)

Never blast the exact same CV to 40 adverts without reading the fine print:

| Job Advert Section | What to Look For | Smart Candidate Action |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Job Title & Key Duties (`KPA`)** | Actual daily tasks (`e.g. "capture stock invoices, assist customers at till, reconcile petty cash"`). | Mirror these exact skill keywords honestly in your CV profile and cover letter! |
| **Minimum Requirements (`Non-Negotiable`)** | Mandatory rules (`e.g. "Grade 11/12 passed, fluent English + isiXhosa, valid Code B licence, computer literacy"`). | Apply only if you genuinely meet minimums (or clearly disclose final-year status for learners/internships). **Never forge licences or grades!** |
| **Preferred / Advantageous Specs** | *"Bonus"* qualities (`e.g. "6 months retail cashier experience or volunteer treasury work"`). | Fill experience gaps creatively using **school tuckshop duty, church/mosque committee work, sports team kit management, or weekend family shop help**! |
| **Submission Rules & Closing Date** | Exact email subject line, PDF vs hard copy, required attachments (`certified ID, testimonial`), strict cut-off time. | Missing one requested document or sending after `16:00` on closing day gets your application **auto-rejected**! |

## Part 2: Building a High-Impact Entry-Level CV (`No Experience? No Problem!`)

```compare
title: Amateur CV Mistakes vs Professional Grade 11/12 CV
left: Rookie Mistakes That Kill Interviews
- **Sloppy contact details:** `sexyking99@gmail.com` or unanswered voicemail boxes (`use professional firstname.lastname@gmail.com!`).
- **Fake padded claims & typos:** Claiming *"fluent Excel wizard"* when you can't write `=SUM()`, or leaving spelling errors (`"Hard working teem playor"`).
- **Generic clutter:** Listing primary school athletics ribbons from Grade 3, marital status/weight/religion (`irrelevant/unnecessary!`), or attaching 8 blurry cellphone photos instead of one tidy PDF.
right: Sharp, Honest CV Structure (`Max 2 Pages`)
- **Clean Header & Short Profile:** Name, reliable phone, professional email, suburb/city + `2–3` crisp lines matching the advert (`e.g. "Reliable Grade 11 Accounting learner with weekend retail experience..."`).
- **Education & Real Experience (`Broadly Defined!`):** Current high school + key subjects alongside **job shadowing, RCL/prefect duties, community volunteering, casual/informal work (`car-wash bookkeeping, tutoring, babysitting`)**.
- **2 Reachable Referees + Supporting Proof:** Ask **permission first** from a teacher, coach, or past supervisor, include their current phone/email, and attach **clean certified ID, latest report, and school testimonial** *only if requested*.
```

## Part 3: Application / Cover Letter & Workplace Ethics (`Accountability & Transparency`)

**Four-Paragraph Application Letter Blueprint (`Formal Business Layout`):**
1. **Your contact details + Date + Recipient details** *(addressed to a real name if listed, e.g. `Dear Ms Naidoo`, else `Dear Hiring Manager`)*.
2. **Subject Line:** `APPLICATION FOR WEEKEND JUNIOR CASHIER (REF: JW-04)`.
3. **Paragraph 1 (`Purpose`):** State the exact post, reference number, and where you saw the advert.
4. **Paragraph 2 (`Proof of Fit`):** Connect **2–3 specific advert needs** directly to your real evidence (`"Managing R4,000 weekly stock floats at our school tuckshop trained me in accurate cash reconciliation and polite queue service"`).
5. **Paragraph 3 (`Closing`):** Express willingness for an interview, list attached documents (`CV, certified ID, testimonial`), and sign off formally (`Yours faithfully` if unknown name / `Yours sincerely` if named).

**Ethical Integrity in Applications & the Workplace (`CAPS Focus`):**
- **Honesty & Accuracy (`Zero CV Fraud`):** Falsifying Matric symbols, forging testimonials, or claiming fake jobs is a criminal offence (`fraud`) that destroys your reputation permanently — employers verify certificates with **Umalusi / SAQA**!
- **Transparency:** Being open, clear, and honest in communication (`e.g. disclosing a genuine scheduling clash or conflict of interest rather than hiding mistakes`).
- **Accountability:** Taking **personal ownership** for your actions, deadlines, till errors, or team deliverables without blaming colleagues or hiding damage.

## Worked example: Spotting Errors & Writing a Tailored Pitch (`8 marks`)

**Advert Extract:** *Cornerstone Pharmacy seeks a Part-Time Saturday Dispensary Runner (`Ref: CP-11`). Requirements: `Grade 11+ learner`, strong English/Afrikaans communication, punctual, organised stock handling. Advantageous: first-aid certificate or community health volunteering. Email CV + 1-page motivation letter as ONE PDF to `hr@cornerstonepharm.co.za` by Friday `17:00`.*

**Candidate Draft Email:** *"From: `babe_boy07@gmail.com` | Subject: `job` | Body: `Hey pls find attached my cv.txt, need money urgently thx`."*

1. *Identify **FOUR concrete errors** in the candidate's submission (`4`).* ->
   - Unprofessional email address (`babe_boy07@gmail.com`).
   - Missing advert **Ref number & job title** in the email subject (`"job"`).
   - Wrong file format (`cv.txt` instead of **ONE combined PDF**) and **missing 1-page motivation letter**.
   - Casual slang tone (`"Hey pls"`, `"thx"`) focused on personal need (`"need money urgently"`) instead of value offered to the pharmacy.
2. *Write **TWO tailored bullet points** a Grade 11 learner could honestly place under `Relevant Experience & Skills` for this advert (`4`).* ->
   - **School First-Aid & Sports Desk Volunteer (`2025–Present`):** Organised restocking of bandage kits weekly and recorded incident logs accurately under coach supervision.
   - **Bilingual Customer Support (`Family Spaza / Tuckshop`):** Assisted English- and Afrikaans-speaking customers politely while sorting shelf expiry dates (`FIFO stock rotation`).

```tryit
title: Accountability vs Transparency (`Ethics Quick Check`)
prompt: Identify whether each workplace behaviour demonstrates **TRANSPARENCY**, **ACCOUNTABILITY**, or **UNETHICAL FRAUD**:
1. Thandi notices she miscounted R150 change at the till and reports it immediately to her supervisor with the receipt log rather than hiding the shortage.
2. Mpho edits his PDF report card to raise Maths from `41%` to `61%` because *"I'll learn on the job anyway"*.
3. A school project treasurer publishes the exact tuckshop income/expense spreadsheet openly on the noticeboard for all learners to inspect.
hint: Open sharing = transparency; owning your mistake = accountability; faking records = fraud!
answer: 1. **Accountability** (taking immediate ownership of an error). 2. **Unethical Fraud** (forgery of official qualifications). 3. **Transparency** (open, honest financial visibility).
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Thinking learners *"can't write a CV"* without formal paid jobs — CAPS explicitly rewards **volunteering, job shadowing, school leadership, community service, and informal work** (`tutoring, gardening, family business help`) backed by **testimonials/referees**!
> - Listing a referee's phone number **without warning them first** — an unprepared referee saying *"Who? I don't remember them"* ruins your credibility.
> - Confusing **transparency** (`openness in processes, decisions, and finances`) with **accountability** (`answering for and owning outcomes of your own actions`).

## Practice

```questions
Q: Why should a Grade 11 learner tailor their **CV and application letter** for every specific job advertisement instead of sending an identical generic copy? (4)
A: Tailoring matches your real skills (`volunteering, school leadership, part-time tasks`) directly to the advert's **exact minimum and preferred requirements** (`keywords and duties`). This shows genuine effort, passes quick HR screening (`6–10 seconds`), and proves how you add value to *that* employer rather than appearing desperate for any random job.
---
Q: Suggest **THREE legitimate ways** a Grade 11 learner with **zero formal paid work experience** can strengthen their CV honestly. (3)
A: 1. Complete **job shadowing** (`1–3 days observing a workplace`) and log specific skills learned. 2. Volunteer consistently in **community, charity, religious, or school leadership roles** (`RCL, tuckshop, peer tutoring, first-aid squad`). 3. Include verified **informal/holiday work** (`assisting a family trade, tutoring, sports officiating`) alongside a written **school testimonial** and two prepared referees.
---
Q: Define **workplace accountability** and **transparency**, and give ONE example showing why both protect a young employee's career. (5)
A: **Transparency** means operating openly and honestly so stakeholders can see true facts (`e.g. sharing accurate stock/petty-cash records or disclosing a conflict of interest`). **Accountability** means taking personal responsibility for your tasks, decisions, and mistakes (`e.g. admitting a delivery delay immediately and fixing it`). Together they build **employer trust**, leading to positive references, promotions, and long-term employability instead of disciplinary dismissal.
```
