> **What you'll learn:** the **exact national NSC pass requirements** for **Higher Certificate, Diploma, and Bachelor's Degree** admission, how **Admission Point Scores (`APS`)** and **faculty subject prerequisites** work at South African universities/TVETs, comparing **traditional universities, universities of technology, TVET colleges, and SETA learnerships**, and evaluating post-school institutions critically (*CAPS Term 1 · LO*).
>
> **Prerequisites:** Grade 10 career fields & subject choices.
>
> **Why this matters:** Every year thousands of Matriculants *"pass Matric"* yet get **rejected by universities** because they confused a generic **NSC pass** (`30%–40%`) with **Bachelor's admission**, ignored **Life Orientation exclusion in APS**, or chose **Maths Literacy** when their dream career demanded **Pure Mathematics**! Grade 11 marks are the exact report card used for **provisional university applications early in Grade 12**.

## The idea in plain language

Passing Grade 12 is **not a single finish line** — it is a **four-tier ladder** (`NSC -> Higher Certificate -> Diploma -> Bachelor's Degree`). Furthermore, meeting the **national legal minimum** only puts your foot inside the door; every university faculty adds its **own higher APS target and required school subjects** (`for instance, BSc Engineering won't accept 80% in Maths Literacy!`).

## Part 1: The Three Post-School NSC Admission Tiers (`South Africa`)

*Baseline across ALL NSC tiers: You must take **7 subjects** (`4 compulsory`: Home Language, First Additional Language, Mathematics OR Mathematical Literacy, Life Orientation + **3 chosen electives**) and pass **6 of 7** (`minimum 40% in Home Language, 40% in two other subjects, 30% in three other subjects — failing at most one subject, provided you completed all SBA portfolios!`).*

| NSC Tier | Exact National Minimum (`CAPS / Umalusi`) | Where It Takes You | Crucial Catch (`Reality Check`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Higher Certificate (`NQF 5`)** | **HL `>= 40%`** + **2 other subjects `>= 40%`** + **3 other subjects `>= 30%`** (`pass 6 of 7`). | 1-year vocational/bridging certificates at **TVET colleges**, private colleges, or universities of technology (`often opens access to a Diploma afterwards!`). | Does **NOT** allow direct entry to a Bachelor's degree or most 3-year Diplomas. |
| **Diploma (`NQF 6`)** | **HL `>= 40%`** + **3 other subjects `EXCLUDING LO` `>= 40%`** (`4 total at 40%+`!) + **2 other subjects `>= 30%`**. | 3-year **National Diplomas** at **Universities of Technology (`CPUT, TUT, DUT, VUT`)**, **TVET NATED (`N4–N6`)**, or nursing/teaching colleges. | **Life Orientation (`LO`) does NOT count** among the four `40%+` subjects! Practical programmes still require specific Maths/Science marks. |
| **Bachelor's Degree (`NQF 7`)** | **HL `>= 40%`** + **4 subjects `EXCLUDING LO` `>= 50%`** + **2 other subjects `>= 30%`** (`and 30%+ in Language of Learning & Teaching`). | 3–4+ year **Degrees** at **Traditional & Comprehensive Universities** (`UCT, Wits, UP, UKZN, UJ, Stellenbosch, UWC, NWU, UFS, Rhodes, Nelson Mandela, Unisa, etc.`). | National minimum `50%` almost never gets you into Medicine, Engineering, Actuarial Science, or Law — **check each university's faculty APS & subject rules**! |

## Part 2: Understanding Achievement Levels & Calculating APS

South African NSC results convert percentage bands into **Achievement Levels `1–7`**, which universities sum to form your **Admission Point Score (`APS`)**:

| NSC Percentage | Achievement Level | Description (`CAPS`) | Typical University APS Points (`6 Best Subjects excl. LO`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| `80% – 100%` | **Level 7** | Outstanding achievement | `7` points *(note: a few institutions use an 8-point or weighted scale — always read their prospectus!)* |
| `70% – 79%` | **Level 6** | Meritorious achievement | `6` points |
| `60% – 69%` | **Level 5** | Substantial achievement | `5` points |
| `50% – 59%` | **Level 4** | Moderate achievement | `4` points |
| `40% – 49%` | **Level 3** | Adequate achievement | `3` points |
| `30% – 39%` | **Level 2** | Elementary achievement | `2` points |
| `0% – 29%` | **Level 1** | Not achieved (`Fail`) | `1` point *(often `0` points at strict faculties!)* |

**How Universities Treat Life Orientation (`LO`) & Special Rules:**
- **Most SA universities** calculate APS out of **`42` (`6 subjects x 7`)**, **excluding Life Orientation** completely (though they often still require `>= 50%` or `>= 4` in LO, or award a maximum of `1–3` capped points — *e.g., Wits/UP/UJ/UCT use distinct institutional methods, so ALWAYS check the current official university prospectus!*).
- **Subject Prerequisites Beat High APS:** Having an APS of `38` with **65% in Maths Literacy** still disqualifies you from a `BCom Accounting` or `BEng` degree requiring **`60%–70%` in Pure Mathematics**.

## Part 3: Comparing Post-School Institutions & Evaluating Options

```compare
title: Choosing the Right Post-School Institution (`Not Everyone Needs a Traditional Degree!`)
left: Universities & Universities of Technology
- **Traditional Universities:** Academic theory, research, professional degrees (`Medicine, Law, CA(SA), Teaching, Engineering`). Higher theoretical entry bars.
- **Universities of Technology (`UoTs`) & Comprehensive Universities:** Blend applied theory with mandatory **Work-Integrated Learning (`WIL`)** (`Diplomas -> Advanced Diplomas -> BTech/Degrees`) in hospitality, IT, surveying, engineering tech, and design.
right: TVET Colleges, SETA Learnerships & Critical Checks
- **TVET Colleges (`Public Technical & Vocational Education & Training`):** Affordable **NC(V)** (`NQF 2–4`) and **NATED Report 191 (`N4–N6` + 18 months practical -> National Diploma)** for artisan trades (`electrician, boilermaker, plumbing`), hospitality, office admin, and early childhood care.
- **Evaluating ANY Institution (`Red Flags!`):** Confirm **DHET (`Department of Higher Education and Training`) & CHE/SAQA registration** and **NQF level** *before* paying deposits — unregistered "fly-by-night" private colleges issue worthless certificates! Also compare pass rates, practical lab access, residence safety, and realistic graduate employment.
```

## Worked example: Calculating APS & Checking Subject Fit (`8 marks`)

**Profile:** *Thabo's Grade 11 Term 4 report shows:*
- *English HL: `64%`* | *isiZulu FAL: `73%`* | *Mathematics: `58%`* | *Physical Sciences: `54%`* | *Accounting: `71%`* | *Geography: `68%`* | *Life Orientation: `82%`*

1. *Calculate Thabo's **standard 6-subject APS (excluding LO)** (`3`).* ->
   - English HL `64%` (`5`) + isiZulu FAL `73%` (`6`) + Mathematics `58%` (`4`) + Physical Sciences `54%` (`4`) + Accounting `71%` (`6`) + Geography `68%` (`5`) = **`30` out of `42`**. *(Notice LO's `82%` = Level 7 is NOT added in standard 6-subject APS!)*
2. *Determine Thabo's **national NSC admission tier** (`2`).* -> **Bachelor's Degree admission** (`HL >= 40%`, all 6 non-LO subjects are `>= 50%` — well above the required 4 subjects at `50%+`, and zero failed subjects).
3. *Thabo wants to apply for **BSc Civil Engineering** requiring `APS 34`, `Maths 65%`, `Physical Sciences 65%`. Give **TWO specific action steps** for Grade 11/12 (`3`).* ->
   - Boost **Mathematics (`58% -> 65%+`)** and **Physical Sciences (`54% -> 65%+`)** urgently via past-paper practice to satisfy BOTH the **APS gap (`30 -> 34`)** AND the **faculty subject prerequisites**.
   - Apply simultaneously for a **National Diploma in Civil Engineering** at a University of Technology as a realistic **Plan B** (`which often sets slightly lower entry thresholds and offers articulation later`).

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Including **Life Orientation (`Level 7`)** inside the `4` subjects at `50%+` for Bachelor's admission or inside the `3` non-HL subjects at `40%+` for Diploma admission — **LO is explicitly excluded** from both counts!
> - Thinking **Maths Literacy** locks you out of *every* tertiary study — false! Maths Lit qualifies for many **Law, Education, Humanities, Social Work, Journalism, Tourism, and Hospitality** programmes (`check exact faculty rules`), though it blocks Engineering, Medicine, and CA-stream Accounting.
> - Waiting until **January after Matric results** to apply — most university, residence, and NSFAS applications open between **April and September during Grade 12**, using your **Grade 11 final marks**!

## Practice

```questions
Q: State the exact national **NSC pass differences** between a **Higher Certificate**, **Diploma**, and **Bachelor's Degree** pass. (6)
A: All three require `40%+` in Home Language and passing 6 of 7 subjects (`>=30%`). **Higher Certificate:** at least **2 other subjects `>= 40%`** (`LO may be included`). **Diploma:** at least **3 other subjects `EXCLUDING LO` `>= 40%`** (`4 subjects total at 40%+`). **Bachelor's Degree:** at least **4 subjects `EXCLUDING LO` `>= 50%`** (`plus 30%+ in the Language of Learning & Teaching`).
---
Q: Why might a learner with an **APS of `35`** still be rejected for a **BSc Computer Science** programme whose minimum APS is **`32`**? (3)
A: Because admission depends on **BOTH overall APS AND compulsory subject prerequisites** (`e.g. Pure Mathematics >= 65%–70% or Physical Sciences`). If the learner earned `35` using high marks in History and Maths Literacy, they still fail the **faculty's specific subject entry rule**.
---
Q: List **THREE verification checks** a Grade 11 learner should perform before paying a registration deposit at a private college. (3)
A: 1. Confirm the institution and specific qualification are officially registered with **DHET (`Department of Higher Education and Training`)**, **SAQA**, and **CHE/QCTO** (`verify its SAQA ID and NQF level`). 2. Inspect campus facilities, pass/placement rates, and **hidden extra fees** (`exam/textbook levies`). 3. Read the **cancellation and refund policy** in writing before signing any contract.
```
