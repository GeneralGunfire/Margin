## The idea in plain language

Suppose your **Business Plan** says: *"We will launch a profitable artisanal bakery in Stellenbosch earning R80 000 profit in Year 1."*
Wait — a Business Plan tells investors **WHY** the idea makes money, **not** what Sipho must do on **Monday morning at 08:00**!

An **Action Plan** bridges the gap between **dreaming on paper** and **opening the shop doors**: it breaks the big launch into concrete bite-sized tasks, assigns **one accountable person** to every task, sets **exact start/finish deadlines**, and allocates **resources**!

## Part 1: Business Plan vs Action Plan & Project-Planning Steps

```compare
title: Business Plan vs Action Plan (Never Confuse Them!)
left: Business Plan (`The "WHAT & WHY"`)
- Comprehensive document proving **feasibility & viability** (`executive summary, market research, SWOT, financial projections`) mainly to attract **investors/banks**.
- Broad 1–5 year strategic roadmap.
right: Action Plan (`The "WHO, HOW & WHEN"`)
- Practical operational schedule translating **each goal into step-by-step tasks, exact dates, assigned owners, and required resources (`Gantt chart / WBS`)**.
- Used **daily/weekly by founders and managers** to monitor real execution!
```

```steps
title: Core CAPS project-planning steps to turn a plan into action
step: **1. Define project scope & clear goals:** State the exact launch target (`e.g. "Open Bakery Unit 4 cleanly by 1 March with R350 000 budget"`).
step: **2. Build a Work Breakdown Structure (`WBS`):** Split the giant goal into smaller **manageable work packages & specific activities** (`e.g. Legal -> CIPC, municipal health certificate; Equipment -> oven order, wiring; Staffing -> advert, interview, train`).
step: **3. Sequence tasks & spot dependencies:** Decide **which tasks MUST finish before others start** (`you cannot inspect ovens before electricity wiring is installed!`), while noting **parallel tasks** that can run simultaneously (`staff hiring while painting walls!`).
step: **4. Assign responsibilities & resources (`RACI / Owners`):** Name ONE clear owner, realistic start/end dates (**timelines**), and required cash/tools per task (`"everyone responsible" = nobody responsible!`).
step: **5. Visualise on a Gantt chart & monitor progress:** Track actual progress against baseline bars weekly and fix delays immediately!
```

## Part 2: Understanding Work Breakdown Structures (`WBS`) & Gantt Charts

- **Work Breakdown Structure (`WBS`):** A hierarchical tree or numbered outline breaking one huge project (`Launch Café`) down into major deliverables (`1. Legal, 2. Premises, 3. Equipment, 4. Staff, 5. Launch`) and concrete sub-tasks (`1.1 Reserve Name, 1.2 File MOI`) so **no hidden step gets forgotten**!
- **Gantt Chart:** A **bar chart against a calendar timeline** (`days/weeks/months across the top; WBS tasks down the left`). Horizontal bars span from each task's **start date to finish date**!
  - **Key advantages of a Gantt chart:** Shows **overlapping parallel tasks** at a glance, exposes bottlenecks (`critical path delays`), clarifies exact **deadlines per person**, and makes weekly progress meetings fast and visual!

## Worked example: Building an Action Plan Table / Gantt Schedule (`Exam Style`)

**Task:** *Convert Lindiwe's business plan to launch **EcoWash Car Detailing** in **4 weeks** into a clear action plan with **WBS tasks, timelines, and responsible persons** (`8 marks`).*

| WBS Task (`What`) | Responsible (`Who`) | Timeline (`When`) | Resources / Check (`How`) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Register `(Pty) Ltd` & municipal water permit** | Lindiwe (`Founder`) | **Week 1** | CIPC portal (`R175`), municipal bylaws check |
| **2. Sign bay lease & install water-recycling tanks** | Operations Lead | **Weeks 1–2** | Lease deposit (`R18 000`), plumber quote |
| **3. Buy pressure washers & eco-detergents** | Finance / Buyer | **Week 2** | Supplier invoice (`R42 000`) |
| **4. Recruit & train 3 detailers (`safety/OHS`)** | Lindiwe & Ops Lead | **Week 3** | Job advert, practical wash-bay induction |
| **5. Launch BTL flyers & host opening promo** | Marketing Rep | **Week 4** | Printed flyers (`R2 500`), social media reel |

*(Notice: Tasks 1 & 2 overlap in Week 1, and Tasks 2 & 3 overlap in Week 2 — saving two full weeks compared to doing everything one-by-one!)*

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Summarising the **7 sections of a Business Plan** (`marketing, financial, SWOT`) instead of explaining **action-plan tools (`WBS`, `Gantt chart`, timelines, responsibilities, project-planning steps)**!
> - Drawing a Gantt chart with **missing timelines (`weeks/dates`)** or **missing task owners** — always label the horizontal time axis AND name a responsible person!
> - Scheduling dependent tasks **before their prerequisite finishes** (`e.g. advertising Grand Opening Day before equipment is even ordered!`).

## Practice

```questions
Q: Explain the difference between a **business plan** and an **action plan**, stating why both are essential before launching a venture. (4)
A: A **business plan** outlines *what* the business will do and *why* it is financially viable (`mainly to persuade lenders/investors`). An **action plan** converts those goals into a practical schedule detailing *who* performs each task, *how*, and by *exact deadline* (`timelines`), ensuring the launch actually happens on budget and on time.
---
Q: Define a **Work Breakdown Structure (`WBS`)** and a **Gantt chart**, and give TWO benefits of using a Gantt chart. (6)
A: A **WBS** hierarchically breaks a large project into smaller, manageable work packages and concrete tasks. A **Gantt chart** plots those tasks along a horizontal calendar timeline using bars showing start dates, end dates, and durations. **Benefits:** (1) Visualises overlapping parallel tasks and deadlines instantly; (2) Helps managers spot delays early and track accountability across team members.
---
Q: List FOUR essential columns or pieces of information that must appear in every **action plan**. (4)
A: Any four of: **specific task/activity (`WBS`)**, **responsible person/owner**, **start and end timeline/deadline**, **required resources/budget**, and **progress/completion indicator**.
```
