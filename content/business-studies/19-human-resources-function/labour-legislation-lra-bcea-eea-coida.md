> **What you'll learn:** the purpose, core rules, and HR implications of South Africa's **four compulsory CAPS labour Acts** — **Labour Relations Act (`LRA 66 of 1995`)**, **Basic Conditions of Employment Act (`BCEA 75 of 1997`)**, **Employment Equity Act (`EEA 55 of 1998`)**, and **Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act (`COIDA 61 of 1997`)** (*CAPS Business Operations · Paper 1*).
>
> **Prerequisites:** Lesson 1901 (`HR contracts & benefits`) and Chapter 4 (`LRA strikes/unions`).
>
> **Why this matters:** Paper 1 Section B & C ALWAYS test **matching real workplace violations to the exact Act (`and year/number!`)** — e.g. unpaid overtime = **BCEA**, hurt by unguarded saw = **COIDA** (`compensation`) + **OHS Act** (`prevention`), bypassed for promotion due to race/gender = **EEA**, fired without a hearing = **LRA**!

## The idea in plain language

You have hired great people (`Lesson 1901`). Now how does South African law guarantee neither the employer nor employee gets exploited?
Through **four interlocking pillars of workplace law**:
1. **LRA (`1995`)** = **Fairness & Voice** (`unions, CCMA, no unfair dismissal!`).
2. **BCEA (`1997`)** = **Minimum Hours, Leave & Pay Rules** (`45-hour week, overtime, annual/sick/parental leave!`).
3. **EEA (`1998`)** = **Equality & Affirmative Action** (`banning unfair discrimination!`).
4. **COIDA (`1997`)** = **Financial Compensation When Hurt/Ill on Duty** (`paying medical bills & disability/dependants!`).

## Part 1: The Four Labour Acts Compared (`With Exact Numbers & Rules`)

| Act & Number | Primary Purpose (`CAPS Exam Precision`) | Key Workplace Rules & Worker Rights |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. LRA (`Act 66 of 1995`)** | Regulates **collective bargaining, trade unions, strikes/lockouts, and fair dispute resolution (`CCMA`)**. | Protects freedom to join unions; guarantees **substantive & procedural fairness** before dismissal (`written warning, proper disciplinary hearing — NEVER firing on the spot without process!`*); enforces Workplace Forums. |
| **2. BCEA (`Act 75 of 1997`)** | Protects vulnerable workers by setting **minimum working conditions & leave rules** (`cannot contract BELOW BCEA!`). | **Max 45 ordinary hours/week** (`9h/day for 5-day week; 8h/day for 6-day week`); **overtime voluntary (`max 10h/week`) paid at 1.5× normal wage (`2× on Sundays/public holidays`)**; **21 consecutive days paid annual leave/year**; **30 days paid sick leave per 36-month cycle**; **4 consecutive months maternity leave** (`plus statutory 10 days parental leave`); **3 days family responsibility leave**; bans **child labour (<15 years)**! |
| **3. EEA (`Act 55 of 1998`)** | Eliminates **unfair workplace discrimination** and promotes **affirmative action** for equitable representation. | Bans unfair discrimination (`race, gender, pregnancy, marital status, religion, disability, HIV status, age`); requires equal pay for **work of equal value**; **designated employers (`50+ workers` or turnover threshold)** must submit **Employment Equity Plans** consulting staff! *(Note: **inherent job requirements** like a valid pilot licence for a pilot are FAIR differentiation, NOT unfair discrimination!)* |
| **4. COIDA (`Act 61 of 1997`)** | Compensates employees (or dependants) for **accidents, occupational diseases, or death arising OUT OF and IN THE COURSE OF work**. | **Employer ONLY** pays annual assessment levies into the Compensation Fund (`workers pay R0!`); covers medical treatment, temporary/permanent disability payouts, and dependants' pensions **even if the employee made an honest mistake** (`excludes deliberate wilful misconduct!`); **prevents workers suing the employer separately in civil court**! |

## Part 2: Crucial Exam Distinction — COIDA vs OHS Act!

Many learners lose easy marks mixing up **OHS Act (`Chapter 17`)** and **COIDA (`Chapter 19`)**!

```compare
title: Prevention (OHS Act) vs Compensation (COIDA)
left: OHS Act 85 of 1993 (`Prevention`)
- Focuses on **preventing injuries BEFORE they happen**: machine guards, free PPE, fire exits, safety reps, hazard training!
- Covers **unsafe workplaces** even if nobody has been hurt yet!
right: COIDA 61 of 1997 (`Compensation AFTER Injury`)
- Focuses on **paying medical bills, lost wages, disability, or widow/dependant pensions AFTER an injury or work-related disease happens**!
- Funded by **compulsory employer contributions** to the Compensation Fund.
```

## Worked example: Diagnosing Labour Law Violations in a Case Study (`Paper 1`)

**Case Study:** *At **Apex Furniture Ltd** (`85 employees`): **(A)** Workers are forced to work 54 ordinary hours every week with NO overtime pay; **(B)** A pregnant receptionist is dismissed via WhatsApp without a hearing immediately after announcing her pregnancy; **(C)** A carpenter slices two fingers off on a saw during his normal shift and needs hospital surgery.*

1. *Identify the exact **Act violated** in `(A)` and state TWO legal entitlements owed (`4`).* -> **Basic Conditions of Employment Act (`BCEA 75 of 1997`)** — ordinary hours are capped at **45 hours/week**, and voluntary extra hours must be paid at **1.5× overtime rate**!
2. *Name **TWO Acts** broken in `(B)` and justify each (`4`).* ->
   - **Employment Equity Act (`EEA 55 of 1998`):** Dismissing or penalising someone because of **pregnancy** is **unfair discrimination**!
   - **Labour Relations Act (`LRA 66 of 1995`):** Firing via WhatsApp without a fair hearing is an **automatically unfair / procedurally unfair dismissal** (`she may refer to CCMA!`).
3. *Which Act guarantees the carpenter's **hospital bills and temporary wage payout** in `(C)`, and who funds it? (`2`)* -> **COIDA (`Act 61 of 1997`)**, funded solely by **employer contributions** to the Compensation Fund!

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Saying **COIDA** deducts money from employees' monthly salaries (`like UIF`) — **COIDA is funded 100% by the employer** (`zero worker deduction!`).
> - Confusing **COIDA** (`compensation after workplace injury/disease`) with **UIF** (`unemployment/maternity/general illness insurance under BCEA/UIF`).
> - Claiming **Affirmative Action (`EEA`)** requires firing current competent employees to hit targets — **EEA explicitly prohibits dismissing workers merely to meet equity targets**, relying instead on fair hiring, training, and succession planning!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Compare the primary purpose of the **Labour Relations Act (`LRA 66 of 1995`)** with the **Basic Conditions of Employment Act (`BCEA 75 of 1997`)**. (4)
A: The **LRA** regulates relationships between employers, employees, and trade unions — promoting **collective bargaining, CCMA dispute resolution, and protection against unfair dismissal**. The **BCEA** protects individual worker dignity by setting **minimum working hours (`max 45h/wk`), overtime rates, and paid leave entitlements**.
---
Q: Explain the difference between **fair differentiation (`inherent job requirement`)** and **unfair discrimination** under the **Employment Equity Act (`EEA 55 of 1998`)**. (4)
A: Requiring qualifications, physical fitness, or licences **strictly essential to perform the job** (`e.g. a commercial pilot licence or clear eyesight`) is **fair differentiation**. Rejecting or paying someone less based on **race, gender, pregnancy, religion, age, or disability** unrelated to job capability is illegal **unfair discrimination**.
---
Q: State TWO rights an injured factory worker enjoys under **COIDA (`Act 61 of 1997`)**, and explain ONE advantage COIDA gives the **employer**. (6)
A: **Employee rights:** (1) Full medical hospital/rehab expenses paid; (2) Compensation for lost wages during temporary recovery (`or lump sum/pension for permanent disability or dependants`). **Employer advantage:** Protects the employer from crippling **civil lawsuits** by injured employees (`replaced by pooled statutory fund cover`).
```
