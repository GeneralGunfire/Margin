## The idea in plain language

Hiring the wrong person costs a business **three times more** than taking time to hire the right one!
**Human Resources (`HR`)** manages people cleanly from **"Job Needed"** (`Job Analysis`) all the way to **"First Day & Monthly Payday"**:
- **Recruitment** = casting the net to **attract a pool of qualified applicants** (`encouraging people to apply!`).
- **Selection** = **filtering, interviewing, and choosing the single best candidate** from that pool!

## Part 1: From Job Analysis to Induction (`The HR Pipeline`)

```steps
title: Step-by-step HR hiring & onboarding process (CAPS sequence)
step: **1. Job Analysis (`Job Description vs Job Specification`):**
- **Job Description:** Describes **WHAT the job entails** (*duties, responsibilities, working conditions, reporting line*).
- **Job Specification:** Describes **WHO fits the job** (*minimum qualifications, experience, licences, skills, personal traits*).
step: **2. Recruitment (`Attracting Applicants`):**
- **Internal recruitment** (*notice boards, intranet, promotions, staff referrals*): **Fast, cheap, motivates staff** — *yet limits fresh external ideas & creates another vacancy!*
- **External recruitment** (*online portals, newspapers, agencies, campus expos*): Brings **new skills/diversity & larger pools** — *yet costs more & risks slower cultural fit!*
step: **3. Selection (`Screening & Filtering`):** Sort CVs against the **Job Specification**, shortlist top candidates, conduct **background/reference/qualification checks**, and administer honest job-related skills tests.
step: **4. Interviewing:** Structured panel asks **standardised, job-relevant questions** (`NO illegal discriminatory questions on pregnancy, religion, HIV status, or age under EEA!` — let the candidate speak ~70–80% of the time and ask questions back!).
step: **5. Written Employment Contract & Placement:** Under **BCEA**, give written **particulars of employment** on Day 1 (`employer/employee details, job title, workplace, ordinary hours, wage/payday, leave entitlements, notice period, deductions`). Both parties sign!
step: **6. Induction (`Onboarding`):** Welcome the new hire, tour facilities/safety exits (`OHS`), introduce colleagues/culture, explain HR/payroll policies, and assign a supportive mentor (`cuts early anxiety & rookie resignations!`).
```

## Part 2: Remuneration, Personal Tax (`PAYE`) & Employee Benefits

| Category | Exact Details (`CAPS & South African Payroll`) |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Salary vs Wage** | **Salary:** Fixed monthly pay (`typically admin/professional staff`). **Wage:** Calculated hourly/daily/weekly or piece-rate (`often paid weekly/fortnightly`). |
| **Personal Income Tax (`PAYE` Link)** | Under South African tax law, employers act as tax agents deducting **PAYE (`Pay-As-You-Earn`)** monthly from taxable remuneration (`progressive tax brackets based on earnings`) and paying it directly to **SARS** (`reported annually on an IRP5 certificate!`). |
| **Compulsory (`Statutory`) Benefits** | **UIF (`Unemployment Insurance Fund`):** **1% employee + 1% employer (`2% total`)** protecting workers during unemployment, maternity/parental, or illness leave! **COIDA (`Compensation Fund`):** Employer-only levy covering workplace injury/disease (`Lesson 1902`). **SDL (`Skills Development Levy`):** Funded by qualifying employers (`payroll > R500k/year`) for SETA training! |
| **Voluntary (`Fringe`) Benefits** | **Pension / Provident fund** (`retirement savings`), **Medical aid**, **13th cheque / performance bonuses**, **housing/travel/cellphone allowances**, **paid study bursaries**, or subsidised canteen meals (`retain loyal skilled talent!`). |

## Worked example: Fixing Flawed HR Hiring & Identifying Benefits (`Paper 1 Case Study`)

**Scenario:** *SpeedFreight needs a **Senior Logistics Manager (`BCom Supply Chain + 5 years experience`)**. HR posts the advert only on the warehouse tearoom fridge (`Action A`), asks the sole female finalist during the interview whether she plans to have children soon (`Action B`), and skips tour/OHS orientation on her first morning saying "Figure out the system as you go!" (`Action C`).*

Identify **THREE HR mistakes (`6`)** and recommend **ONE fix per mistake (`6`)**:
1. **Action A — Restricting a senior specialist vacancy to internal recruitment only:** Warehouse operators may lack a BCom Supply Chain degree -> *Fix:* Use **external recruitment** (`LinkedIn/supply-chain portals/agencies`) alongside internal notices!
2. **Action B — Illegal discriminatory interview question (`family/pregnancy`):** Violates **Employment Equity Act (`EEA`)** -> *Fix:* Train interview panels to use **structured, purely job-related competency questions** only!
3. **Action C — Skipping Induction (`Onboarding`):** Causes stress, costly dispatch errors, and OHS hazards -> *Fix:* Run a structured **1-week induction programme** covering software, warehouse safety, team introductions, and signed **BCEA employment contract**!

```tryit
title: HR Pipeline Quick Check
prompt: Distinguish clearly between:
1. **Job Description** vs **Job Specification**
2. **Recruitment** vs **Selection**
3. **UIF** vs **Medical Aid** (`which is compulsory by law, and which is a fringe benefit?`)
hint: Job duties vs person traits; attracting vs choosing; statutory vs voluntary!
answer: 1. **Job Description** = duties/tasks of the post; **Job Specification** = qualifications/skills required of the person. 2. **Recruitment** = attracting applicants; **Selection** = screening, interviewing, and choosing the winner. 3. **UIF** = **compulsory statutory benefit** (`1% employee + 1% employer`); **Medical Aid** = **voluntary fringe benefit**.
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Saying **Recruitment** includes *interviewing and signing the contract* — **recruitment stops once applications arrive**; **shortlisting, interviewing, and choosing** belong to **Selection**!
> - Swapping **Job Description** (`the JOB's duties/hours/location`) with **Job Specification** (`the APPLICANT's degree/licence/skills`).
> - Thinking **UIF (`1% + 1%`)** is optional like a **13th cheque** — **UIF and COIDA are compulsory by South African law**!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Give TWO advantages and TWO disadvantages of **internal recruitment** (`promoting existing staff`). (4)
A: **Advantages:** (1) Faster and cheaper than placing external adverts; (2) Boosts staff morale/loyalty and reduces cultural adjustment time. **Disadvantages:** (1) Limits fresh external ideas and diversity; (2) Leaves another vacancy behind that still needs filling!
---
Q: Explain the purpose of a formal **induction programme** for newly appointed employees. (4)
A: Induction welcomes new employees, introduces colleagues and workplace culture, explains **company policies and OHS safety procedures**, and speeds up productive confidence (`reducing early mistakes and staff turnover`).
---
Q: Distinguish between **PAYE**, **UIF**, and a **Pension/Provident Fund** on a monthly South African payslip. (6)
A: **PAYE** is compulsory progressive **personal income tax** deducted by the employer and paid to **SARS**. **UIF** is compulsory **Unemployment Insurance (`1% deducted from employee + 1% matched by employer`)** covering temporary unemployment, illness, or parental leave. A **Pension/Provident fund** is a **retirement benefit** (`usually co-contributed`) building long-term savings for after retirement.
```
