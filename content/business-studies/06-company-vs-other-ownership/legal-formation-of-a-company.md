> **What you'll learn:** the **exact legal steps and documents** required to establish a company under South Africa's **Companies Act 71 of 2008**: **reserving a company name**, drafting the **Memorandum of Incorporation (`MOI`)**, filing the **Notice of Incorporation (`CoR 14.1`)**, receiving the **Registration Certificate (`CoR 14.3`) & commencement of business**, and the role of a **Prospectus** (*for Public Companies!*) (*CAPS Business Ventures · Paper 2*).
>
> **Prerequisites:** Lesson 601 (`Private vs Public Companies`).
>
> **Why this matters:** Paper 2 exams frequently ask candidates to **sequence the registration steps of a company**, **define the MOI**, explain **what happens if a proposed name breaks legal rules**, and **distinguish an MOI from a Prospectus**!

## The idea in plain language

Before a human starts driving, they need a birth certificate, ID, and rules of the road. Because a **company is a separate "legal person" (`juristic person`)**, you must legally *"birth"* it at **CIPC (`Companies and Intellectual Property Commission`)** using **three core documents**:
1. **Notice of Incorporation (`CoR 14.1`)** — the "birth application form" listing founders, directors, and registered address.
2. **Memorandum of Incorporation (`MOI`)** — the company's **supreme internal constitution** setting shareholder rights, director powers, and share rules!
3. **Registration Certificate (`CoR 14.3`)** — the official "birth certificate" bringing the company legally alive! *(Plus a **Prospectus** ONLY if a Public Company invites the public to buy shares!)*

## Part 1: Step-by-Step Formation Process (`CIPC Under Companies Act 71 of 2008`)

```steps
title: Five legal stages to form and launch a South African company
step: **1. Reserve the Company Name (`CoR 9.1 / CIPC Online`):** Submit up to four ranked names to verify uniqueness (or register quickly using the CIPC registration number as name first!).
- **Name rules:** Must end with **`(Pty) Ltd`** (`Private`), **`Ltd`** (`Public`), **`NPC`** (`Non-Profit`), or **`SOC Ltd`** (`State-Owned`). Cannot be identical/confusingly similar to an existing name or trade mark, deceptive, or offensive!
step: **2. Draft & Adopt the Memorandum of Incorporation (`MOI`):** The **sole governing founding document** (`replaced the old dual Memorandum & Articles of Association under the 1973 Act!`). Founders can use a **CIPC Standard MOI (`short/long`)** or a **Custom MOI** stating share classes, voting rules, director limits, and audit rules.
step: **3. File Notice of Incorporation (`CoR 14.1`) & Pay CIPC Fee:** Submitted by one or more incorporators (`minimum 1 for (Pty) Ltd, 1+ incorporator & min 3 directors for Ltd`), attaching the signed MOI, certified IDs, and registered SA physical address.
step: **4. Incorporation & Registration Certificate (`CoR 14.3`):** Once CIPC accepts the filing, it issues the **Registration Certificate (`CoR 14.3`)** with a unique **registration number (`e.g. 2027/123456/07`)**. From this exact date, the company exists as a **separate legal person**!
step: **5. Commencement of Business & Additional Registrations:** Unlike older laws that required a separate certificate to start trading, once **`CoR 14.3`** is issued, a Private Company can **open a company bank account, register for SARS Income Tax/VAT/PAYE, UIF/COIDA**, and **commence trading immediately** (`while a Public Company issuing shares to the public must ALSO register a Prospectus first!`).
```

## Part 2: Memorandum of Incorporation (`MOI`) vs Prospectus (`Exam Hotspot!`)

```compare
title: Do NOT Confuse the MOI with a Prospectus!
left: Memorandum of Incorporation (`MOI`)
- **Required by:** **EVERY company** (`Private (Pty) Ltd`, `Public Ltd`, `NPC`).
- **Purpose:** Sets out **internal governance rules**, rights/duties of shareholders and directors, share issue rules, and meeting procedures (**the company's constitution**).
- **Must NOT violate:** The **Companies Act 71 of 2008** (`alterable provisions may be adapted; unalterable provisions cannot be weakened`).
right: Prospectus
- **Required by:** **Public Companies (`Ltd`) ONLY** — and specifically when **offering shares/securities to the general public** (`IPO / primary offering`)!
- **Purpose:** Formal **written public invitation & financial disclosure document** giving potential investors honest audited figures, business risks, director track records, and minimum subscription targets so they can decide safely whether to buy shares!
- **Legal protection:** Must be registered with **CIPC** before public share offers open (`misleading statements carry severe criminal/civil director liability!`).
```

## Worked example: Spotting Formation Mistakes in a Start-Up Case Study

**Scenario:** *Lebo wants to register **Apex Solar (Pty) Ltd** as a small 2-shareholder **Private Company**. Her friend tells her: **(1)** "Write both a Memorandum of Association AND Articles of Association"; **(2)** "Print and hand out a Prospectus at taxi ranks to sell shares to strangers"; **(3)** "Start signing lease contracts in Apex Solar's name before CIPC issues `CoR 14.3`."*

Evaluate each piece of advice (`6 marks`):
1. **Advice `(1)` is outdated:** Under the **Companies Act 71 of 2008**, the old dual documents were replaced by **ONE single Memorandum of Incorporation (`MOI`)**.
2. **Advice `(2)` is illegal for a `(Pty) Ltd`:** A **Private Company (`(Pty) Ltd`)** is legally prohibited from offering shares to the general public, and **only a Public Company (`Ltd`)** issues a registered **Prospectus**!
3. **Advice `(3)` is risky:** Until **CIPC issues the Registration Certificate (`CoR 14.3`)**, the company does not legally exist yet (`pre-incorporation contracts require special ratification rules under Section 21, otherwise Lebo becomes personally liable!`).

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Saying every Private Company (`(Pty) Ltd`) must publish a **Prospectus** — a Prospectus applies **only when a Public Company offers shares to the public**!
> - Mentioning old **1973 Companies Act** terms like *"Articles of Association"* or *"Certificate to Commence Business (`CM46`)"* — always state **`Memorandum of Incorporation (MOI)`** and **`Registration Certificate (CoR 14.3)`** under the **Companies Act 71 of 2008**!
> - Forgetting **SARS tax, UIF/COIDA, and business bank account setup** right after incorporation before commencing full trading!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Define the **Memorandum of Incorporation (`MOI`)** and state THREE items typically included in it. (5)
A: The **MOI** is the **sole founding constitutional document** of a company under the Companies Act 71 of 2008 setting out the rights, duties, and powers of shareholders and directors. It typically specifies **(1) company name and main objectives/restrictions**, **(2) number and classes of authorised shares**, and **(3) rules for board composition, voting, and shareholder meetings**.
---
Q: Explain the purpose of a **Prospectus** and state which type of company issues one. (4)
A: A **Prospectus** is a formal regulated invitation published by a **Public Company (`Ltd`)** when offering shares to the general public (`cannot be used by a (Pty) Ltd`), disclosing audited financial performance, risks, director details, and subscription terms to protect investors.
---
Q: Give TWO reasons why **CIPC** may reject a proposed company name during the **Name Reservation** step. (2)
A: 1. The name is **identical or confusingly similar** to an already-registered company or trade mark. 2. The name **falsely implies government endorsement**, uses banned offensive words, or omits the required ending suffix (`(Pty) Ltd` / `Ltd`).
```
