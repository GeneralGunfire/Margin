## The idea in plain language

Knowing *what* sits inside the Micro, Market, and Macro rings (`Chapter 1`) is step one. **Chapter 2** zooms in on **what goes wrong inside each ring** — and teaches exact CAPS vocabulary like **demographics vs psychographics** and **labour restrictions vs strikes** so you never lose marks to vague guesses!

## Part 1: Challenges of the Micro Environment (`Internal Headaches`)

| Micro Challenge | What It Looks Like in Real Life | How It Harms Operations |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Difficult employees** | Absenteeism, gossip, poor attitude toward customers, refusing teamwork or instructions. | Ruins customer reputation, creates tension, slows output, wastes HR disciplinary hours. |
| **Lack of vision & mission** | Management runs day-to-day without clear long-term goals or shared direction. | Departments pull in opposite directions, waste money, and demotivate staff. |
| **Lack of adequate management skills** | Poor planning, weak cash-flow budgeting, failing to delegate or motivate teams. | Costly mistakes, missed deadlines, staff turnover, possible business failure. |
| **Workplace strikes & disputes** | Internal workers down tools (`go-slows / strikes`) over unpaid wages, unsafe conditions, or broken promises (`linked to union activity`). | Production halts completely, orders are cancelled, revenue drops while fixed costs stay due! |

## Part 2: Challenges of the Market Environment (`External Market Pressures`)

```steps
title: Five core market-environment challenges in CAPS
step: **1. Competition:** Rivals cut prices, launch better products, or copy successful ideas (`e.g. low-cost online retailers squeezing local shops`), shrinking market share and profit margins.
step: **2. Shortages of supply:** Key suppliers run out of raw materials, strike, raise prices sharply, or go bankrupt (`stalling your factory even when customers want to buy!`).
step: **3. Changes in consumer behaviour:** Buyers shift suddenly toward **online shopping, eco-friendly packaging, or budget store brands** during tight times.
step: **4. Demographics & Psychographics:** **Demographics** = measurable population traits (*age, gender, income bracket, household size, urbanisation*). **Psychographics** = **attitudes, hobbies, lifestyle values, and beliefs** (*health consciousness, eco-awareness, brand status*). Miss a demographic/psychographic shift and your stock gathers dust!
step: **5. Socio-cultural factors:** Language diversity, religious calendars (`Ramadan, Diwali, Christmas`), dietary norms (`Halal, vegan`), and family customs affect what sells respectfully in each community.
```

## Part 3: Challenges of the Macro Environment (`Economy, Law & Global Forces`)

```compare
title: Macro Challenges — Economic/Global vs Political/Legal/Social
left: Economic & Global Challenges
- **Changes in income levels:** Rising unemployment or inflation shrinks household **disposable income** (`fewer dining-out/luxury sales`).
- **Micro-lending & debt:** High-interest informal/micro loans trap consumers in debt repayments, reducing cash for retail goods (`though micro-credit also helps small traders start up!`).
- **Globalisation:** Cheap foreign imports (`e.g. low-cost clothing from abroad`) undercut local factories, while global supply shocks disrupt local prices.
right: Political, Legal & Social Challenges
- **Political changes:** Policy uncertainty, municipal service failures (`water/electricity`), or unstable trade relations scare off investors.
- **Legislation & labour restrictions:** Strict compliance with **LRA, BCEA, EEA, COIDA, BBBEE, OHS Act, and CPA** raises admin costs and restricts flexible hiring/firing (*non-compliance risks heavy CCMA/court fines!*).
- **Social values:** Rising intolerance of pollution, plastic waste, or unethical labour forces businesses to transform fast or face consumer boycotts.
```

## Worked example: Extracting & Classifying Challenges from a Case Study

**Case Study:** *Mzansi Sneakers (`Pretoria`) has struggled lately. **(1)** Two warehouse supervisors lack basic scheduling skills, causing late deliveries. **(2)** Meanwhile, Gen-Z shoppers increasingly prefer eco-friendly vegan sneakers (`changing lifestyle values`) rather than heavy leather boots. **(3)** Finally, cheap imported shoes flooding from overseas cut Mzansi Sneakers' sales by 20%.*

Complete the exam table:

| Quote from Scenario | Environment | Exact CAPS Challenge Name |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| *"supervisors lack basic scheduling skills"* | **Micro** | **Lack of adequate management skills** |
| *"Gen-Z shoppers increasingly prefer eco-friendly vegan sneakers"* | **Market** | **Psychographics / Changes in consumer behaviour** |
| *"cheap imported shoes flooding from overseas"* | **Macro** | **Globalisation** *(economic/global competition)* |

```tryit
title: Demographics vs Psychographics Check
prompt: Classify each market shift as either **Demographics** or **Psychographics**:
1. A town's population ages significantly as retirees move in (`more residents aged 60+`).
2. Young professionals in Johannesburg increasingly value **zero-waste wellness lifestyles and plant-based dining**.
hint: Measurable population statistics vs lifestyle values/attitudes!
answer: 1. **Demographics** (`age profile`). 2. **Psychographics** (`attitudes, values, and lifestyle choices`).
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Answering *"competition"* when a case study clearly describes **cheap foreign imports from overseas (`globalisation` / `dumping` in Macro)** — check whether the competitor is a local rival (`Market`) or cross-border trade (`Macro`).
> - Confusing **Demographics** (`age, gender, income, location stats`) with **Psychographics** (`mindset, values, hobbies, attitudes`).
> - Quoting **without naming the environment** or naming the environment without **quoting directly** when a question says *"Quote from the scenario..."*!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Explain TWO ways **difficult employees** and **lack of vision** harm a business in the **micro environment**. (4)
A: 1. **Difficult employees** upset customers, fuel workplace conflict, and lower productivity (`often requiring formal disciplinary steps`). 2. **Lack of vision and mission** leaves managers and staff without clear priorities, causing wasted resources and inconsistent service.
---
Q: Distinguish between **demographics** and **psychographics** in the market environment, giving one business example of each. (4)
A: **Demographics** measure objective population statistics such as age, income, and family size (e.g. designing smaller apartments for single young professionals). **Psychographics** study consumers' lifestyle attitudes, values, and interests (e.g. launching recyclable bamboo packaging for eco-conscious shoppers).
---
Q: How can **strict labour legislation (`labour restrictions`)** in the **macro environment** create a challenge for a small expanding factory? (3)
A: Compliance with laws such as the **BCEA, LRA, and EEA** increases payroll administration, limits instant dismissal of underperforming staff without strict procedural fairness, and raises overtime/benefit costs (`making owners cautious about hiring permanent staff`).
```
