> **What you'll learn:** why **Franklin D. Roosevelt (FDR)** swept the 1932 election, how the **First New Deal (1933)** and **Second New Deal (1935–1938)** applied the **Three Rs (Relief, Recovery, Reform)** via key *"Alphabet Agencies"* (`FDIC, CCC, AAA, NRA, TVA, WPA, Social Security, Wagner Act`), who opposed the New Deal (left, right, Supreme Court), whether it **strengthened or weakened capitalism**, and how the Depression reshaped **Germany, Japan, the USSR, and South Africa**!
>
> **Prerequisites:** 1929 Crash, bank runs, and Hoover's laissez-faire failure.
>
> **Why this matters:** Virtually every Paper 1 USA essay asks: *"To what extent was FDR's New Deal a success?"* or *"Did the New Deal save capitalism or introduce socialism?"* Knowing BOTH the **achievements** and **limits** (the 1937 recession, exclusion of Black/women workers, and WWII ending unemployment) makes your argument airtight!

## The idea in plain language

In November **1932**, Democrat **Franklin D. Roosevelt (FDR)** crushed Hoover, promising *"a New Deal for the forgotten man"* and pragmatic bold experimentation. Within days of taking office in March 1933, FDR spoke directly to families on radio (**Fireside Chats**) to calm bank panic, advised by his university expert team (**Brains Trust**).

The core philosophy shifted America **away from pure laissez-faire** toward **regulated welfare capitalism** built around the **Three Rs**:
1. **Relief:** Immediate emergency cash, food, and jobs for starving families.
2. **Recovery:** Restart factories, farming prices, and consumer spending (*pump-priming*).
3. **Reform:** Permanent laws to prevent another banking crash or worker exploitation!

## Key Agencies of the First & Second New Deal

| Phase | Agency / Act | R-Goal | What It Did |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **First 100 Days (1933)** | **Emergency Banking Act (`9 Mar 1933`) + FDIC** | **Relief & Reform** | Ordered 4-day **Bank Holiday**, reopened sound banks only, and insured personal savings (**FDIC**) -> ended bank runs! |
| **1933** | **FERA & CCC** | **Relief** | **FERA** gave cash grants to states; **Civilian Conservation Corps (CCC)** hired young men to plant trees, fight fires, build parks. |
| **1933** | **AAA & TVA** | **Recovery & Reform** | **Agricultural Adjustment Act (AAA)** paid farmers to cut surplus crops/livestock so prices rose (criticised while people went hungry!); **TVA** built dams/electricity in the impoverished Tennessee Valley. |
| **1933** | **NIRA (`NRA` & `PWA`)** | **Recovery & Reform** | **NRA ("Blue Eagle")** set minimum wages/fair codes; **PWA** funded big schools, bridges, dams. |
| **Second New Deal (`1935`)** | **WPA** | **Relief & Recovery** | Built **roads, airports, schools**, and hired **artists, writers, musicians** — creating over 3 million jobs by 1938! |
| **1935** | **Social Security Act & Wagner Act** | **Reform** | Created first national **old-age pensions & unemployment insurance** (`Social Security`); **Wagner Act** legally protected trade unions and collective bargaining! |

## Opposition to the New Deal (Attacked from BOTH Sides!)

```compare
title: Conservative Right vs Radical Left Critics of the New Deal
left: Right-Wing & Business Critics
- **Wealthy Industrialists & Republicans:** Accused FDR of **"creeping socialism"**, raising `"soak the rich"` taxes, boosting debt, and empowering unions (`Wagner Act`).
- **Conservative Supreme Court:** Struck down BOTH the **NRA (May 1935)** and **AAA (January 1936)** as unconstitutional federal overreach! (FDR tried unsuccessfully to *"pack"* the Court in 1937.)
right: Left-Wing & Populist Critics
- **Huey Long ("Share Our Wealth", assassinated Sept 1935), Father Coughlin & Socialists:** Argued FDR was **too timid** and merely patched up big capitalism instead of redistributing billionaires' wealth radically.
- **Civil Rights & Women Advocates:** New Deal relief often **excluded domestic/farm workers** (disproportionately **African Americans and women**), and FDR refused federal anti-lynching laws to keep Southern Democrats' votes!
```

## Did the New Deal Save Capitalism — and End the Depression?

- **How it SAVED & STRENGTHENED capitalism:** By rescuing banks (`FDIC`), regulating Wall Street (`SEC`), providing hope and basic pensions (`Social Security`), FDR **prevented desperate revolution or fascism** in the USA and proved democratic capitalism could reform itself!
- **Why it did NOT cure the Depression on its own:** When FDR cut federal spending in **1937** to balance the budget, unemployment surged right back (**1937–1938 "Roosevelt Recession"**)!
- **What finally ended mass unemployment?** **World War II!** Rearmament from 1938–1940 and US entry after **Pearl Harbor (7 December 1941)** turned American factories into 24-hour war production, creating full employment.

## Global Impact of the 1929 Crisis (Crucial Topic 2 Conclusion!)

1. **USSR:** Largely cut off from Western capital markets, Stalin's Five-Year Plans boasted **no unemployment** — boosting communism's global appeal while hiding famine.
2. **Germany:** Collapse of US loans crippled German banks and sent unemployment to ~6 million — destroying Weimar democracy and helping **Adolf Hitler's Nazi Party** seize power (1933).
3. **Japan:** Export collapse weakened civilian moderates, empowering **military expansionists** who used deficit spending and invaded Manchuria (1931).
4. **South Africa:** Severe drought + farm collapse worsened the **Poor White problem** investigated by the **Carnegie Commission (1927–1932)** — feeding Afrikaner nationalism — until South Africa abandoned the **Gold Standard in late December 1932**, sparking a massive gold-mining boom!

## Worked example: Answering "Was the New Deal a success?" (`Essay Stance`)

**Stance:** *The New Deal succeeded largely in **relieving human desperation and reforming banking and social welfare to preserve American democracy**, even though it **failed to achieve full economic recovery until World War II** and **marginalised many African Americans and women**.*

- **Paragraph 1 (Banking & Relief success):** Bank Holiday, FDIC, CCC, WPA restored trust and put millions to work.
- **Paragraph 2 (Long-term Reform):** Social Security and Wagner Act reshaped US society permanently.
- **Paragraph 3 (Economic Limits):** 1937 recession proved unemployment remained high (~14–19%) until WWII war orders.
- **Paragraph 4 (Social Limits):** AAA displaced Black tenant farmers; domestic/farm exclusions from Social Security.

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Claiming **"the New Deal ended the Great Depression in 1935"** — the **1937–1938 recession** proved full recovery only came with **World War II rearmament (1939–1941)**!
> - Saying the **New Deal turned the USA into a communist/socialist country** — private banks, factories, and profit stayed intact; FDR saved *regulated capitalism*.
> - Forgetting **South Africa's connection** — abandoning the **Gold Standard (Dec 1932)** and the **Carnegie Commission on Poor Whites (1932)** link Topic 2 directly into Topic 4 (Afrikaner nationalism)!

## Practice

```questions
Q: How did FDR's **Emergency Banking Act (9 March 1933)** and **FDIC** end the banking crisis? (2)
A: By declaring a temporary **Bank Holiday** to inspect banks, reopening only financially sound banks, and introducing **federal deposit insurance (FDIC)** so ordinary savers trusted banks again and stopped panic runs.
---
Q: Why did the **US Supreme Court** strike down the **NRA (1935)** and **AAA (1936)**? (2)
A: Conservative judges ruled that both laws gave the federal president and agencies **unconstitutional power** over local state commerce and farming that exceeded Washington's authority.
---
Q: Contrast the impact of the 1929 Great Depression on **Germany** and **South Africa**. (4)
A: In **Germany**, recalled US loans collapsed banks and fuelled mass unemployment, discrediting democracy and helping **Hitler's Nazis** rise to power in 1933. In **South Africa**, farm distress deepened the **Poor White problem** studied by the **Carnegie Commission (1932)**, until leaving the **Gold Standard (Dec 1932)** triggered a major gold-mining boom.
```
