> **What you'll learn:** the critical difference between the **Wall Street Crash (October 1929)** as the *trigger* and the **long-term structural causes** of the **Great Depression (1929–1939)**, the devastating human impact (*bank runs, Hoovervilles, Dust Bowl, Bonus Marchers*), and why President **Herbert Hoover's** response failed.
>
> **Prerequisites:** 1920s overproduction, credit/margin buying, and laissez-faire capitalism.
>
> **Why this matters:** A classic Paper 1 trap is writing *"The Wall Street Crash was the sole cause of the Great Depression."* Examiners explicitly reward learners who distinguish **long-term structural causes** from **the October 1929 trigger**, and trace the vicious **downward spiral** that destroyed banks, factories, and families!

## The idea in plain language

A stock market crash by itself only wipes out paper wealth on Wall Street. So why did **October 1929** smash the entire American (and global) economy for a whole decade?

Think of a house built on **rotten wooden stilts** (**overproduction, unequal wages, fragile unregulated banks, tariff walls, and margin debt**). The **Wall Street Crash** was the **storm wind** that snapped the first stilt — knocking every other pillar down in a **domino effect**:

```steps
title: How the 1929 Crash triggered a 10-year Great Depression (Domino Chain)
step: **1. Black Thursday (24 Oct 1929) & Black Tuesday (29 Oct 1929):** Smart investors noticed slowing car/housing sales and started selling; panic exploded (**12.9 million shares** dumped on Thursday; **~16 million** on Black Tuesday). Share values collapsed!
step: **2. Margin Calls & Bank Failures:** Brokers demanded cash from margin buyers; ruined investors couldn't repay loans -> **small banks that had gambled depositors' savings in stocks ran out of cash**! Panicked families rushed to withdraw savings (**bank runs**), shutting **~650 banks in 1929** and **thousands more by 1933**.
step: **3. Spending & Factory Collapse:** With savings wiped out and confidence gone, consumers stopped buying cars/appliances -> factories slashed wages and laid off millions (**~25% unemployment** by 1933!).
step: **4. Global Contagion:** US banks urgently **recalled loans from Germany and Latin America** and hiked tariffs (`Smoot–Hawley Tariff`, 1930) -> world trade shrank dramatically!
```

## Social & Human Impact on Ordinary Americans (1929–1933)

Because **1920s America had NO national unemployment insurance, NO old-age pensions, and NO federal welfare**, losing a job meant instant destitution:
- **Mass Homelessness ("Hoovervilles"):** Evicted families built shacks out of scrap tin and cardboard in city parks, bitterly nicknamed **Hoovervilles** (with newspapers called *"Hoover blankets"*!).
- **Breadlines & Soup Kitchens:** Charities collapsed under demand; long queues stretched down blocks for free bread and soup; food riots erupted in cities.
- **Dust Bowl & Okies:** Severe drought plus careless 1920s over-ploughing turned Great Plains topsoil into blinding dust storms, driving hundreds of thousands of ruined farming families (*"Okies"*) west to California as migrant pickers.
- **African Americans Hit First & Hardest:** *"Last hired, first fired"* — Black unemployment ran far above the national average, while Southern relief boards often excluded Black families.
- **Bonus Marchers (1932):** Thousands of jobless WWI veterans camped peacefully in Washington demanding early payment of promised war bonuses; Hoover ordered troops under General MacArthur to clear their camp with tear gas and bayonets — sealing his electoral doom!

## Why Did President Herbert Hoover Fail?

| What Hoover Believed | What Hoover Actually Did | Why It Wasn't Enough |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rugged Individualism** | Refused direct federal cash relief to families, fearing it would destroy work ethic ("moral character"). | Local charities and cities were completely bankrupt by 1931! |
| **Voluntary Cooperation** | Urged businesses voluntarily not to cut wages or fire workers. | Firms facing bankruptcy ignored voluntary pledges immediately. |
| **Limited Late Action (`RFC`, 1932)** | Created the **Reconstruction Finance Corporation** to lend money *at the top* to banks and railways + Hoover Dam works. | Too little, too late — helped corporations (`"trickle-down"`) while starving families saw zero immediate relief. |

```compare
title: Trigger vs Root Causes (Never mix them up in exams!)
left: Immediate Trigger (October 1929)
- Loss of investor confidence -> panic selling on **Black Thursday (24 Oct)** and **Black Tuesday (29 Oct 1929)**.
right: Deeper Structural Causes (1920s)
- **Unequal income distribution** (rich saved/speculated; workers lacked spending power).
- **Industrial & farm overproduction**.
- **Unchecked margin speculation** & **weak unregulated banks** lacking deposit guarantees.
- **High tariffs** choking international trade and European debt repayments.
```

## Worked example: Explaining a Depression Graph or Photograph

**Source prompt:** *A 1932 photograph shows well-dressed unemployed men standing in a long street queue beneath a massive billboard reading: "WORLD'S HIGHEST STANDARD OF LIVING — There's no way like the American Way."*

1. *What **irony** does the photographer highlight? (2 x 2) (4)*
   - **Point 1:** The billboard boasts of the **"World's highest standard of living"** and celebrates the **"American Way"** of booming consumer capitalism.
   - **Point 2:** Yet directly beneath it stand **destitute, jobless citizens queuing in a breadline** during the Great Depression, exposing the stark gap between capitalist propaganda and lived poverty.

```tryit
title: Spot the logical flaw in this explanation
prompt: Read this student sentence and improve it in 2–3 clear lines:
> *"The Great Depression happened only because too many people bought shares on October 29, 1929, and President Hoover made no laws at all before leaving office."*
hint: Separate **underlying 1920s causes** from the **Oct 1929 trigger**, and mention **RFC (1932)** accurately!
answer: **Improvement:** While the **Wall Street Crash (24–29 October 1929)** triggered financial panic, the Great Depression was rooted in **1920s overproduction, unequal wealth, fragile unregulated banks, and margin debt**. Hoover *did* pass late measures like the **1932 Reconstruction Finance Corporation (RFC)** for banks, yet his refusal to provide direct federal relief left millions destitute.
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Writing that **Black Tuesday (29 Oct 1929) was the ONLY day** of the crash or the sole cause of the Depression — **Black Thursday (24 Oct)** launched the panic, and 1920s structural flaws turned the crash into a depression!
> - Claiming **Hoover did literally nothing** — he funded **Hoover Dam** and the **RFC (1932)**, yet opposed direct federal unemployment relief for citizens.
> - Forgetting **bank runs** — unregulated banks losing depositors' money wiped out even prudent non-investors' life savings!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Why did the **Wall Street Crash** hurt ordinary families who had NEVER bought a single share on the stock market? (3)
A: Many **banks had invested depositors' money in stocks or lent to speculators**; when share prices crashed, banks collapsed, wiping out ordinary families' savings, while collapsing consumer spending caused factories to lay off millions of workers.
---
Q: Explain what **Hoovervilles** and the **Bonus March (1932)** reveal about public attitudes toward President Herbert Hoover. (3)
A: Naming homeless shantytowns **Hoovervilles** showed widespread bitterness blaming Hoover's inaction for mass poverty; violently dispersing jobless **Bonus March WWI veterans** in 1932 convinced voters he cared more for banks than suffering citizens.
---
Q: Give THREE long-term structural weaknesses of the 1920s US economy that caused the Great Depression. (3)
A: 1. **Overproduction** in farming and factories outstripping consumer demand. 2. **Extreme income inequality** leaving ordinary workers unable to keep buying goods without debt. 3. **Unregulated banking & margin speculation** paired with **high protective tariffs** restricting global trade.
```
