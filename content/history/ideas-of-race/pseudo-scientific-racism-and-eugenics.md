> **What you'll learn:** how 18th–19th-century European writers invented **pseudo-scientific racial hierarchies** (*Linnaeus, Blumenbach, Camper, Samuel Morton*), how **Social Darwinism** (*Herbert Spencer*) distorted evolution to justify imperialism, the difference between **positive and negative eugenics** (*Francis Galton*), how these ideas were applied in the **USA, Namibia (Herero/Nama genocide 1904), Australia, and South Africa**, and how modern genetics (**Human Genome Project, 1990–2003**) proves race is a social construct, not a biological hierarchy.
>
> **Prerequisites:** basic European colonialism and source evaluation skills (recognising bias and propaganda).
>
> **Why this matters:** Topic 3 sets up the core question of Paper 1 Section A/B: **What happens when false "scientific" race theories become government policy and law?** Understanding the link between **Galton's eugenics**, **colonial Namibia (Eugen Fischer)**, **Australia**, and **Nazi Germany** is essential for both source-based and essay questions!

## The idea in plain language

Today we know that all human beings belong to **one single biological species** (*Homo sapiens*). Yet during the 18th and 19th centuries, European empires conquered Africa, Asia, the Americas, and Australia — and needed a moral excuse for slavery, land theft, and colonial violence.

Instead of admitting economic greed, some scholars claimed **"science" proved European superiority**. Historians call this **pseudo-scientific racism** (*false science disguised as objective measurement*). Once governments turned those false ideas into laws about who could marry, vote, immigrate, or have children, tragedy followed on a massive scale.

## How "Race Science" Was Built (1730s–1890s)

```steps
title: From human classification to pseudo-scientific hierarchy
step: **1. Artificial Categories (1730s–1795):** Swedish botanist **Carl Linnaeus** (1730s) divided humans into four colour-coded types tied to fake personality traits. German anatomist **Johann Blumenbach (1795)** classified five varieties and invented the term *"Caucasian"* (claiming European skulls were most "beautiful").
step: **2. Flawed Skull & Brain Measuring (1790s–1850s):** **Petrus Camper** used facial angles and **Samuel Morton** filled skulls with lead shot to claim brain-size differences — later exposed as deeply biased sampling and measurement error (**Friedrich Tiedemann** challenged these claims at the time!). **Nott and Gliddon (1854)** published racist skull charts ranking humans below apes.
step: **3. Social Darwinism (late 19th C):** Charles Darwin's *On the Origin of Species* (1859) studied plants and animals in nature (**natural selection**). British philosopher **Herbert Spencer** twisted this into **"survival of the fittest"** among human societies — claiming wealthy empires had a "natural right" to rule or wipe out colonised peoples! Human exhibitions (such as the tragic exploitation of **Saartjie Baartman** in Europe) treated Indigenous people as "specimens".
```

## Eugenics: Positive vs Negative (`Francis Galton, 1883`)

British polymath **Francis Galton** (Darwin's cousin) coined the word **eugenics** in **1883** (*"well-born"*), arguing that governments should deliberately control human breeding to "improve" the genetic stock — just like breeding prize cattle!

```compare
title: Positive Eugenics vs Negative Eugenics
left: Positive Eugenics ("Encourage the 'desirable'")
- Financial incentives, tax breaks, and **"Fitter Family" / "Better Baby"** contests at US state fairs encouraging middle-class white couples to have larger families.
- Later examples: Australia's **£5 maternity baby bonus (1912)** for white mothers and Nazi Germany's **Lebensborn** programme.
right: Negative Eugenics ("Eliminate the 'undesirable'")
- Laws blocking marriage across racial lines (**anti-miscegenation**), racist IQ tests blocking immigration, institutional lock-ups, and **compulsory sterilisation** of people labelled *"feeble-minded"* or *"criminal"*.
- Over **30 US states** passed sterilisation laws (roughly **65 000** forced sterilisations) — admired later by Nazi lawmakers!
```

## Practices Around the World Before 1945

- **United States:** Charles Davenport's **Eugenics Record Office** pushed racist immigration quotas (`1924`), Jim Crow segregation, forced sterilisations, and assimilation boarding schools for Native Americans.
- **Namibia (`German South West Africa`, 1904–1908):** When the **Herero and Nama** peoples resisted German land theft and cattle seizure (`Waterberg, Aug 1904`), German commander Lothar von Trotha issued an **extermination order (`Vernichtungsbefehl`, Oct 1904)** driving families into the Omaheke desert and concentration camps (including Shark Island). German scientist **Eugen Fischer** measured skulls of prisoners and mixed-race *Basters* at Rehoboth — later writing textbooks directly used by **Adolf Hitler** and the Nazis!
- **South Africa:** Early 20th-century segregation laws (*anti-miscegenation*, *Natives Land Act 1913*) and 1930s birth-control debates among white supremacists drew on eugenics fears of "racial mixing".

## What Modern Science Proved (`Human Genome Project, 1990–2003`)

Completed in **2003** (begun **1990**), the international **Human Genome Project** mapped human DNA and confirmed that **all human populations share roughly 99.9% genetic similarity**. While small genetic variations reflect geography and ancestry, **discrete biological "races" with fixed intelligence or moral character do NOT exist** — "race" is a **social and historical construct**, not a scientific hierarchy!

## Worked example: Exposing pseudo-scientific bias in a Source

**Source prompt:** *Read an 1890s colonial speech claiming: "Nature dictates that stronger races must govern weaker ones; European rule brings progress to backward continents."*
*Question: How does a historian evaluate the **reliability** and **purpose** of this source? (2 x 2) (4)*

1. **Purpose & Bias:** The source relies on **Social Darwinism** (`"stronger races"`, `"nature dictates"`) as an **ideological excuse** to justify violent land conquest and colonial exploitation.
2. **Reliability:** It is **unreliable as factual evidence** about colonised peoples because it reflects 19th-century European **pseudo-scientific racism**, though it is **useful** for showing how imperialists justified empire to voters back home!

```tryit
title: Spot 2 historical errors about Race Science & Genetics
prompt: Correct the mistakes in these two statements:
1. *"Charles Darwin invented eugenics to prove Africans were inferior, and the Human Genome Project proved this wrong in 2008."*
2. *"Negative eugenics gave cash bonuses to large families in Britain."*
hint: Check **WHO coined "eugenics" (1883)**, **WHEN the Human Genome Project finished (1990–2003)**, and **Positive vs Negative eugenics**!
answer: 1. **Francis Galton** coined **eugenics** (1883) and **Herbert Spencer** popularised *Social Darwinism* (distorting Darwin's biological theory); the **Human Genome Project ran from 1990 to 2003** (not 2008) and confirmed ~99.9% human genetic similarity.
2. Cash bonuses for approved families are **positive eugenics**; **negative eugenics** used marriage bans, forced sterilisation, and segregation to stop targeted groups from having children.
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Presenting **19th-century racial claims as biological facts** in your essay — always frame them clearly as **false pseudo-scientific theories** used to justify power!
> - Confusing **Darwin** (biology/natural selection) with **Herbert Spencer** (Social Darwinism) and **Francis Galton** (eugenics).
> - Overlooking **German South West Africa (Namibia, 1904)** — examiners often ask how colonial eugenics (like **Eugen Fischer's** work) fed directly into **Nazi racial policy**!

## Practice

```questions
Q: Why do historians describe 19th-century craniometry (skull measuring) as **pseudo-science**? (2)
A: Scientists like Samuel Morton cherry-picked samples, distorted measurements to match pre-existing racial prejudices, and falsely linked skull volume to intelligence or moral worth — claims disproven by modern biology and genetics.
---
Q: Distinguish between **positive eugenics** and **negative eugenics**, giving ONE historical example of each. (4)
A: **Positive eugenics** encouraged groups deemed "superior" to reproduce (e.g. *Fitter Family* contests in the USA or Germany's *Lebensborn*); **negative eugenics** prevented targeted groups from reproducing (e.g. forced sterilisation laws across 30+ US states or Germany's 1933 Sterilisation Law).
---
Q: Explain the link between the **1904 Herero and Nama genocide in Namibia** and later **Nazi racial ideology**. (2)
A: German colonial authorities pioneered racial extermination orders and concentration camps in Namibia, while German geneticist **Eugen Fischer** carried out racial studies there whose textbooks directly shaped Hitler's Nazi racial theories.
```
