> **What you'll learn:** how British colonisation of Australia (**First Fleet, 26 January 1788**) led to frontier dispossession and *"terra nullius"*, how **White Australia** immigration laws (`1901`) and **eugenics fears ("racial suicide")** shaped policy, how the **assimilation policy** forcibly removed tens of thousands of mixed-heritage Indigenous children (**Stolen Generations, c. 1910–1970**), and the long road to reconciliation (**Bringing Them Home report, April 1997** and Prime Minister Kevin Rudd's **National Apology, 13 February 2008**).
>
> **Prerequisites:** pseudo-scientific racism, Social Darwinism, and positive/negative eugenics.
>

If your school teaches Australia, you must connect **19th-century race theories ("dying race" myth + eugenics)** directly to **20th-century child-removal laws** and their lasting human trauma!

## The idea in plain language

For tens of thousands of years before 1788, **Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples** lived across Australia with rich languages, laws, and deep spiritual ties to the land (*Country*). When Britain's **First Fleet** landed at **Sydney Cove / Botany Bay on 26 January 1788**, British officials declared the continent **`terra nullius`** (*"nobody's land"*), ignoring Indigenous ownership completely!

Over the 19th and 20th centuries, Australian governments passed from **frontier violence and isolation on reserves** to an explicitly eugenicist plan: **absorb mixed-ancestry children into white society** so Indigenous identity would disappear entirely.

## Phase 1: Dispossession & the "White Australia" Policy (`1788–1901`)

1. **Colonisation & Population Collapse (1788–1888):** Britain shipped roughly 162 000 convicts to Australian colonies (including **South Australia, founded 1836**). Armed frontier clashes over sheep pasture, massacres, forced removals, and introduced European diseases (**smallpox**) devastated Aboriginal communities (historians estimate the Indigenous population fell sharply from pre-1788 levels by 1888).
2. **"Doomed Race" Myth (Social Darwinism):** Pseudo-scientists claimed Indigenous Australians were a *"primitive stone-age race"* naturally fated to die out before superior Europeans!
3. **Federation & White Australia (1901):** When six colonies formed the **Commonwealth of Australia on 1 January 1901**, one of the very first laws was the **Immigration Restriction Act (December 1901)** — the cornerstone of the **White Australia policy** — using a rigged European-language *"dictation test"* to keep out Asian and Pacific immigrants, alongside fears of *"racial decay / racial suicide"*.
4. **Importing White Children (`1912` & `1920–1967`):** To boost the white population, the government paid a **£5 baby bonus (1912)** to white mothers and later brought thousands of poor **British child migrants ("Lost Generation", 1920–1967)** to Australia under harsh institutional care.

## Phase 2: Assimilation & the Stolen Generations (`c. 1910–1970`)

By the 1920s–1930s, officials noticed that the **mixed-heritage Indigenous population was growing** rather than dying out. Alarmed by eugenic fears, state **"Protectors of Aborigines"** — notably **A.O. Neville** (Western Australia) and **Dr Cecil Cook** (Northern Territory), building on proposals by Baldwin Spencer (1913) and later restated by Paul Hasluck (1951) — adopted **biological and cultural assimilation** (*"breed out the colour"*!):

```steps
title: How the Stolen Generations policy operated
step: **1. Legal Removal Without Consent:** State laws made Chief Protectors the legal guardians of Aboriginal children, letting police and welfare officers remove babies and young children — often forcibly from grieving parents — simply because of their Aboriginal ancestry!
step: **2. Severing Identity & Culture:** Children were placed in distant **state dormitories, missions, or white foster homes**, given English names, forbidden to speak Indigenous languages, and told their parents were dead or did not want them.
step: **3. Exploited Labour & Trauma:** Girls were trained mainly as **unpaid/underpaid domestic servants**, boys as **farm labourers**; many suffered neglect, physical/emotional abuse, and lifelong loss of family, land, and identity. (Estimates run around **100 000 children** removed between c. 1910 and 1970.)
```

```compare
title: Early Reserve/Segregation Policy vs 1930s Assimilation Policy
left: Protection / Reserve Era (late 1800s)
- Assumed Indigenous Australians were a **"dying race"**.
- Policy aimed to "smooth the dying pillow" by confining people on remote government reserves and church missions.
right: Assimilation Era (1910s–1971)
- Triggered when officials realised the **mixed-ancestry population was rising**.
- Forcibly **removed light-skinned / mixed-heritage children** to train them as white-assimilated workers and encourage marriage into white society until Indigenous traits vanished.
```

## Phase 3: Resistance, Inquiry & Apology (`1967–2008`)

- **Indigenous Resistance & Policy End:** Aboriginal leaders and parents fought removals throughout (hiding children, writing petitions, the **1967 Referendum** counting Indigenous Australians fully in the census); official assimilation removals ended around **1970–1971**.
- **Bringing Them Home Report (`April 1997`):** Australia's Human Rights Commission tabled the landmark *Bringing Them Home* inquiry documenting the removals as a **violation of human rights and cultural genocide**.
- **National Apology (`13 February 2008`):** Prime Minister **Kevin Rudd** delivered a historic formal **Apology to the Stolen Generations** in Parliament (*"For the pain, suffering and hurt... we say sorry"*) — followed by a separate apology in 2010 to British child migrants ("Lost Generation").

## Worked example: Analysing an Assimilation-Era Official Source

**Source snippet:** *A 1937 resolution by Australian Chief Protectors stated: "The destiny of the natives of aboriginal origin, but not of the full blood, lies in their ultimate absorption by the people of the Commonwealth."*

1. *Explain what **"ultimate absorption"** meant in practice for mixed-ancestry children. (2 x 2) (4)*
   - **Point 1:** It meant **forcibly removing mixed-heritage children** from their Aboriginal mothers and placing them in white institutions, missions, or foster homes.
   - **Point 2:** It sought to **erase Indigenous languages, culture, and family ties** ("breed out" Aboriginal ancestry) so they assimilated into lower-tier white society.

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Writing **1936** for South Australia (founded **1836**) or **2009** for Kevin Rudd's apology (**13 February 2008**!).
> - Treating Indigenous Australians merely as passive victims — include **Indigenous resistance** (hiding children, petitions, 1967 referendum, and testimony in *Bringing Them Home*).
> - Confusing **Stolen Generations** (Indigenous Australian children forcibly removed c. 1910–1970) with **"Lost Generation"** (poor British child migrants sent to Australia 1920–1967).
