> **What you'll learn:** how **Adolf Hitler** consolidated a Nazi dictatorship (`1933–1934`), how **Nazi racial ideology and eugenics** (*Herrenvolk, Lebensborn, Sterilisation Law 1933, Operation T4*) turned pseudo-science into state murder, the escalating persecution of **Jews** (*1933 boycotts -> Nuremberg Laws 1935 -> Kristallnacht 1938 -> ghettos -> Final Solution 1941–1945*) and other targeted groups (**Sinti/Roma, disabled people, political/religious opponents, homosexuals**), **human choices** (*perpetrators, bystanders, resisters, rescuers*), and the **Nuremberg Trials (`1945–1946`)**.
>
> **Prerequisites:** pseudo-scientific racism, Social Darwinism, and positive/negative eugenics.
>
> **Why this matters:** Nazi Germany is the sharpest demonstration of Topic 3's key question: **how pseudo-scientific race theory plus unchecked dictatorship escalated step by step from discrimination to industrial genocide.** Examiners also expect clear distinctions between **1933–1939 persecution** and **1941–1945 Final Solution**!

## The idea in plain language

The **Holocaust** (*Shoah*) did **not** start with gas chambers on Day 1 in 1933. Hitler used **legal decrees, relentless propaganda, and escalating exclusion** step by step — testing what ordinary citizens would accept as *"normal"*:
1. **1933–1934:** Destroy democracy and build a one-party police state.
2. **1933–1939:** Strip **German Jews** of citizenship, jobs, and property while targeting disabled people, **Sinti and Roma**, and opponents using **eugenics laws**.
3. **1939–1945 (WWII):** Trap millions in walled **ghettos**, launch mobile shooting squads (**Einsatzgruppen**), and build **extermination camps** (**Final Solution**).

## Step 1: Consolidating the Nazi Dictatorship (`1933–1934`)

- **30 January 1933:** President Hindenburg appointed **Adolf Hitler** Chancellor legally amidst the Great Depression crisis.
- **27 February 1933 (`Reichstag Fire`):** Nazis blamed communists, suspended civil liberties, and arrested political rivals.
- **23 March 1933 (`Enabling Act`):** Passed (`441–94`) giving Hitler power to pass laws **without Parliament** -> banned all rival political parties and trade unions!
- **30 June–2 July 1934 (`Night of the Long Knives`):** Hitler's **SS** (under **Heinrich Himmler**) murdered rival SA Stormtrooper leader Ernst Röhm and political critics, winning German army loyalty.
- **2 August 1934:** Upon Hindenburg's death, Hitler merged President + Chancellor into **Führer**, supported by **Gestapo** secret police and Joseph Goebbels's **propaganda** ministry.

## Step 2: Building a Eugenics & Racial State (`1933–1939`)

Believing Germans belonged to an *"Aryan master race"* (**Herrenvolk**) threatened by "inferior" groups and inherited illness (drawing on eugenics theorists **Fischer, Bauer, Lenz**), the regime enacted **both positive and negative eugenics**:

```compare
title: Nazi Positive vs Negative Eugenics & Targeted Groups
left: Positive Eugenics ("Pure Aryan Births")
- **Lebensborn (founded December 1935):** SS-run maternity homes encouraging "racially pure" SS families to have multiple children, plus rewards (`Mother's Cross`) for large Aryan families.
right: Negative Eugenics & Persecution
- **Sterilisation Law (`14 July 1933`):** Forced sterilisation of ~300 000–400 000 people labelled with hereditary conditions.
- **Operation T4 (`begun 1939`):** Secret **euthanasia** killing of disabled children and adults (`70 273` documented gassings before public protests pushed killings underground!).
- **Other Targeted Groups:** **Sinti and Roma**, **homosexuals**, **Jehovah's Witnesses**, **political dissidents** (communists/socialists), and **"asocials"** imprisoned in early camps like **Dachau**.
```

### Escalation Against Jews (`1933–1939`)
- **1933–1934:** Shop boycotts, firing Jews from civil service, universities, law, and media; antisemitic school lessons and *Der Stürmer* propaganda caricatures.
- **Nuremberg Laws (`15 September 1935`):** Stripped Jews of **German citizenship** and forbade marriage/relations between Jews and non-Jewish Germans (`"protection of German blood"`), defining Jewishness by **grandparents' ancestry** (pseudo-racial, not faith!).
- **Kristallnacht (`9–10 November 1938`):** Coordinated nationwide pogrom burned hundreds of synagogues, smashed ~7 500 Jewish businesses ("Night of Broken Glass"), killed near 100 people, and arrested **~30 000 Jewish men** into concentration camps — followed by **Aryanisation** (confiscating remaining Jewish property/businesses).

## Step 3: World War II & the "Final Solution" (`1939–1945`)

```steps
title: From conquest in Poland to industrial extermination
step: **1. Ghettos (`1939–1941`):** Invading Poland trapped millions of Jews in overcrowded, starving walled **ghettos** (Warsaw, Lodz).
step: **2. Einsatzgruppen & Gas Vans (`1941`):** Following the June 1941 invasion of the USSR, **SS mobile killing squads (`Einsatzgruppen`)** carried out mass shootings (`Babi Yar`), while **Chelmno** began gassing victims in vans (**8 December 1941**).
step: **3. Wannsee & Extermination Camps (`1942–1945`):** After the **Wannsee Conference (Jan 1942)** coordinated Europe-wide deportation, purpose-built death camps — **Auschwitz-Birkenau, Treblinka, Sobibor, Belzec, Majdanek** — murdered victims rapidly with **Zyklon-B** gas or carbon monoxide, murdering **about 6 million Jews** alongside hundreds of thousands of Roma, Soviet POWs, and others.
step: **4. Resistance, Choices & Liberation (`1943–1945`):** People made vastly different choices: **perpetrators** (camp guards, bureaucrats), **bystanders** (silent neighbours), **resisters** (**Warsaw Ghetto Uprising, April 1943**, partisans), and **rescuers** (villages like **Le Chambon-sur-Lignon**, diplomats, ordinary hidings). Soviet troops liberated **Auschwitz (27 January 1945)**; British/Allied forces liberated **Bergen-Belsen**.
```

## Step 4: Justice & Legacy (`Nuremberg Trials, 1945–1946`)

- **Nuremberg Trials (`20 Nov 1945 – 1 Oct 1946`):** Allied judges prosecuted surviving Nazi leaders for **War Crimes** and **Crimes Against Humanity** (**retributive justice** — *"just following orders"* was rejected as a legal defence!).
- **Link to South Africa:** Whereas Nuremberg used **retributive punishment** for top perpetrators, post-apartheid South Africa later chose **restorative justice** through the **Truth and Reconciliation Commission (TRC, 1995)** — a classic comparative question!

## Worked example: Analysing Choices & Propaganda in Source-Based Questions

**Source prompt:** *Why did most ordinary German citizens remain **bystanders** rather than active resisters between 1933 and 1945? (2 x 2) (4)*

1. **Fear & Police Terror:** The **Gestapo** and concentration camps punished political dissent or hiding Jews with imprisonment or execution, creating an atmosphere of constant fear and mutual suspicion.
2. **Relentless Propaganda & Gradual Normalisation:** Controlled schools, radio, and newspapers normalised antisemitic stereotyping over years, convincing many Germans either to support Nazi recovery or ignore gradual exclusion of neighbours.

```tryit
title: Put 4 key Nazi dates in order & catch a common confusion
prompt: Arrange these 4 events chronologically and explain ONE common confusion about **#D**:
`(A) Kristallnacht` | `(B) Nuremberg Laws` | `(C) Enabling Act` | `(D) Warsaw Ghetto Uprising`
hint: 1933 -> 1935 -> 1938 -> 1943 (watch 1943 Ghetto Uprising vs 1944 Polish Warsaw Uprising!).
answer: **Order:** **(C) Enabling Act (23 Mar 1933)** -> **(B) Nuremberg Laws (15 Sep 1935)** -> **(A) Kristallnacht (9–10 Nov 1938)** -> **(D) Warsaw Ghetto Uprising (19 Apr 1943)**.
*Common confusion:* The **Warsaw Ghetto Uprising (April 1943)** was armed Jewish resistance inside the ghetto — distinct from the later Polish Home Army **Warsaw Uprising (1944)**!
```

> **Common mistakes**
>
> - Claiming **Nazi gas chambers operated in 1933** — early camps (Dachau, 1933) were prison/labour camps for political opponents; **extermination camps and the Final Solution began in 1941–1942**!
> - Saying **ONLY Jews were murdered** — always acknowledge **Sinti and Roma, disabled victims (Operation T4), Soviet POWs, political opponents, and gay men** alongside the ~6 million Jewish victims.
> - Judging **Nazi propaganda posters** as neutral historical facts instead of **biased state persuasion** revealing Nazi stereotyping!

## Practice

```questions
Q: How did the **Nuremberg Laws (15 September 1935)** turn antisemitism into a pseudo-racial law rather than a religious dispute? (2)
A: The laws stripped German Jews of citizenship and banned intermarriage based on **grandparents' blood ancestry**, meaning even converted or non-practising Jews were targeted purely on Nazi racial classification.
---
Q: Distinguish between a **perpetrator**, a **bystander**, and a **rescuer** during the Holocaust, giving one historical example of resistance or rescue. (3)
A: A **perpetrator** actively planned or carried out persecution/murder (e.g. SS/Einsatzgruppen); a **bystander** witnessed injustice and stayed silent or compliant; a **rescuer** risked their life to save victims (e.g. villagers in **Le Chambon**, France) — alongside armed Jewish **resisters** in the **Warsaw Ghetto Uprising (April 1943)**.
---
Q: What legal principle did the **Nuremberg Trials (1945–1946)** establish regarding **Crimes Against Humanity**? (2)
A: Individual leaders and officials are personally accountable under international law for atrocities — **"just following orders"** is NOT an acceptable legal defence (**retributive justice**).
```
