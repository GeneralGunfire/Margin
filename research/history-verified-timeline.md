# History Grade 11: verified timeline (legacy five topics)

Companion to history-depth.md. Built in a dedicated pass after the Via Afrika guide check.

## Method and honest limits
- **What was done:** for each event I ran a web search restricted to reliable domains (Britannica, South African History Online, the Nelson Mandela Foundation, US Holocaust Memorial Museum, Yad Vashem, UN, Library of Congress, US National Archives and State Department history office, Federal Reserve History, National Museum of Australia, AIATSIS, the Australian Human Rights Commission, History.com and World History Encyclopedia) and recorded the pages that returned and what the search summary said they state.
- **What this is NOT:** I did not open and read each full page. The status comes from the search tool's summary of those pages, so a row marked VERIFIED means two independent publishers' pages were returned and the summary reported agreement. Spot-check any date you will publish. Wikipedia was used only for the earlier spot-check in resources-history-via-afrika.md and never as a source here.
- **Status rules:** VERIFIED = two or more independent publishers agree. UNVERIFIED = one publisher only (including SAHO-only rows, because SAHO pages often repeat each other) or the event was not searched. CONFLICT = sources disagree, or the Via Afrika guide differs; the verified value and the guide's value are both shown.
- **Not reached:** 92 of the 172 entries (mostly lesser dates in Topics 4 and 5, 19th-century European dates, and some US New Deal details) were not confirmed by two sources in this pass and stay UNVERIFIED. They are listed in the tables. Automated searching of Google-style engines was blocked, so everything here went through one search tool in about 60 queries.
- Sources are listed as URLs; no source text is copied.

## Julian (Old Style) and Gregorian (New Style) dates for Russia
Russia used the Julian calendar until February 1918 (31 January 1918 was followed by 14 February 1918). In the 20th century the Julian calendar ran 13 days behind. Convention used in the Date column: **O.S.** = Julian (Russian calendar at the time), **N.S.** = Gregorian (the West). A date with no tag is the same in both or already Gregorian (from 14 February 1918). Revolution names come from O.S. dates: "February" revolution = 23 February-3 March O.S. = 8-16 March N.S.; "October" revolution = 24-26 October O.S. = 6-8 November N.S. Where a source gives only one calendar I state which and add the other from the 13-day rule (marked as general knowledge in the Notes).

**Counts:** 56 VERIFIED, 24 CONFLICT, 92 UNVERIFIED (of 172 entries).
