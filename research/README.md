**FIVE THINGS TO CHECK FIRST**
1. **History: which Grade 11 curriculum applies in 2027.** The legacy five topics (Russia, USA, race, nationalisms, apartheid) are what 2024 papers and the 2023/24 ATP use. DBE published a DRAFT amended CAPS in March 2026 (comment closed 19 May 2026) with entirely different Gr 11 topics (Atlantic World, Haiti, southern African kingdoms, Scramble for Africa). No implementation date found. See history.md.
2. **Physical Sciences: CAPS text vs the 2023/24 ATP.** Optics, wavefronts/diffraction, water chemistry and the lithosphere are in the CAPS text but NOT in the ATP and NOT in the EC 2024-2026 exams. Default to the ATP topics unless your school says otherwise. See physical-sciences.md and past-papers-pure-maths-physical-sciences.md.
3. **Pure Maths: ATP term order differs from the 2011 CAPS pace setter,** and Measurement (surface area/volume) is examined (9-11 marks) although the ATP calls it "revision". See pure-maths.md.
4. **Business Studies: two 150-mark, 2-hour papers (ATP and EC 2024 paper), not CAPS's single 300-mark paper;** term sequencing also differs. See business-studies.md.
5. **AP Maths: needs your IEB outline** before anything beyond your Algebra and Calculus notes can be planned. See ap-maths.md.

Other flags: Tourism (CAPS says seven domestic market segments, the ATP says five; sustainability appears as Gr 10 recovery content in the Gr 11 plan); Afrikaans FAL and English HL set texts unknown; LO admission-point and helpline facts need a 2027 check.
