# Pass 6 extras: exam strategy, glossary, key formulas/facts, Grade 11 to Grade 12 bridge

Written in my own words from the subject files in this folder. Anything not backed by a CAPS/ATP/paper reference there is general study advice and is labelled "general". Formulas and definitions are standard school-level content; check against the learner's textbook before publishing. AP Maths is not covered (no syllabus). Resources not included here.

## PURE MATHEMATICS
**Exam strategy (general, plus patterns seen in EC 2023-2026 papers)**
- Paper 1 (150) and Paper 2 (150): about 45 marks of functions every year in Paper 1; Q1 equations/inequalities is large (25-26). Do easy marks first: exponents, patterns, probability, statistics.
- Always show substitution into formulas (marks are awarded for method). Keep full calculator accuracy; round only at the end.
- Circle geometry: write a reason in brackets after every statement; learn the exact wording of each theorem.
- Trig: write the quadrant and reduction step; give general solutions with "k ∈ Z".
- Check each answer for sense (lengths positive, probabilities between 0 and 1, rate values reasonable).
**Glossary**: rational exponent; surd; discriminant; nature of roots; completing the square; critical value; quadratic sequence; second difference; asymptote; axis of symmetry; average gradient; inclination; reduction formula; identity; general solution; cyclic quadrilateral; tangent-chord theorem; nominal rate; effective rate; depreciation; mutually exclusive; independent events; ogive; variance; standard deviation; outlier.
**Key formulas**: x = (−b ± √(b²−4ac))/2a; Δ = b²−4ac; Tn = an²+bn+c (2a = second difference); d = √((x2−x1)²+(y2−y1)²); m = (y2−y1)/(x2−x1); y−y1 = m(x−x1); tan θ = m; sin²θ+cos²θ = 1; tan θ = sin θ/cos θ; sine rule a/sin A = b/sin B; cosine rule a² = b²+c²−2bc cos A; area = ½ab sin C; A = P(1+i)^n; A = P(1+i/m)^(mn); i_eff = (1+i/m)^m − 1; A = P(1−in); A = P(1−i)^n; P(A or B) = P(A)+P(B)−P(A and B); σ = √(Σ(x−x̄)²/n).
**Bridge to Gr 12**: functions → inverses, logarithms, then calculus (gradient at a point becomes the derivative); number patterns → arithmetic/geometric sequences and series; finance → annuities and loans; trig → compound angles and 3D; analytical geometry → circle equations and tangents; probability → counting principles; statistics → regression and correlation.

## PHYSICAL SCIENCES
**Exam strategy (general + EC 2024-2026 paper structure)**
- Each paper has a 20-mark multiple-choice section; answer all. Paper 1 is mechanics-heavy; Paper 2 is stoichiometry and acid-base heavy.
- Write definitions/laws exactly (Newton's laws, Coulomb's law, Faraday's law, limiting reagent). Learn them word for word from the textbook.
- Calculations: write the formula, substitute with units, give the answer with units and the correct significant figures; draw a free-body diagram first.
- Investigation questions: name independent, dependent and controlled variables; write hypothesis and conclusion.
**Glossary**: resultant; component; free-body diagram; net force; friction; normal force; inertia; action-reaction pair; weight; electrostatic force; electric field; magnetic flux; induced emf; resistance; power; electronegativity; polar; intermolecular force; hydrogen bond; ideal gas; activation energy; exothermic; endothermic; empirical formula; limiting reagent; percentage yield; acid; base; conjugate pair; oxidation; reduction.
**Key formulas** (verify against the exam data sheet): Rx = R cos θ; Ry = R sin θ; Fnet = ma; f_s,max = μ_s N; f_k = μ_k N; F = G m1 m2/r²; F = k Q1 Q2/r²; E = F/q = kQ/r²; ε = −N ΔΦ/Δt; Φ = BA cos θ; R = V/I; P = VI = I²R = V²/R; W = Pt; n = m/M; n = V/Vm; c = n/V; % yield = actual/theoretical × 100; p1V1 = p2V2; V1/T1 = V2/T2; p1V1/T1 = p2V2/T2; pV = nRT (check Gr 11 scope).
**Bridge to Gr 12**: Newton's laws + momentum/impulse, projectile motion, work-energy; electrostatics → capacitance not included, electric circuits add internal resistance; Faraday's law → generators/motors/AC; chemistry: IMF → organic chemistry; stoichiometry/energy → reaction rate and equilibrium; acids and bases → pH and titrations; redox → electrochemistry. Gr 11 Newton, electrostatics, circuits and intermolecular forces, stoichiometry and energy are examined again in Gr 12 (CAPS Table 10).

## ENGLISH HOME LANGUAGE
**Strategy (general + CAPS paper structure)**: Paper 1 (70): read each question's mark allocation; answer in own words; summary within the word limit. Paper 2 (80): plan literary essays; use PEEL; quote briefly. Paper 3 (100): choose the essay topic that gives you most to say; check format of transactional texts; leave 10 minutes to edit. Orals: practise aloud; structure speeches (opening, three points, close).
**Glossary**: register; tone; mood; theme; irony; satire; metaphor; simile; personification; imagery; connotation; denotation; concord; thesis; PEEL; reflective/discursive/argumentative essay; transactional text.
**Key facts**: Paper marks 70/80/100/50 (CAPS); year mark 25% SBA + 75% exam; Gr 11 essay types: reflective, discursive, argumentative; 10 poems.
**Bridge to Gr 12**: longer literary essays (poetry 12 poems), external NSC exam, full-length prepared and unprepared speeches; set works announced by DBE circular.

## HISTORY
**Strategy**: choose questions you know best (3 per paper: ≥1 source-based, ≥1 essay); for sources: extract with quotes, explain, then judge usefulness (purpose, author, audience, date); for essays: answer the exact question, build a line of argument with evidence in PEEL paragraphs, conclude by answering.
**Glossary**: communism; capitalism; Marxism-Leninism; NEP; Stalinism; collectivisation; New Deal; Great Depression; eugenics; racism; nationalism; Volk; segregation; apartheid; Freedom Charter.
**Key facts**: Paper 1: Russia, USA, ideas of race; Paper 2: two nationalism case studies + apartheid (legacy). 50 marks per question, 150 per paper. Draft amended CAPS may replace content: see history.md.
**Bridge to Gr 12**: Cold War, independent Africa, civil society protests, the coming of democracy (1994); essay and source skills are the same but weighted 30/40/30 by cognitive level.

## BUSINESS STUDIES
**Strategy**: Section A short items first (30); in direct/indirect sections link answers to the scenario; essays: introduction, headings, evaluation/own opinion, conclusion; use mark allocation to decide how many points to write (about one point per mark).
**Glossary**: micro/market/macro environment; lobbying; merger; takeover; franchising; outsourcing; Memorandum of Incorporation; prospectus; Gantt chart; creative thinking; professionalism; ethics; marketing mix; mass/batch/job production; quality control; team dynamics; LRA; BCEA; EEA; COIDA.
**Key facts**: four knowledge areas (environments, ventures, roles, operations); EC 2024 format: two papers of 150 marks (30 + 2×40 + 40 per paper); essays 40 marks (32 facts + 8 insight). CAPS also describes one 300-mark paper: confirm for your school.
**Bridge to Gr 12**: finance function, investments, insurance, management and leadership, business plan, legislation in more depth; 300-mark external exam.

## TOURISM
**Strategy**: Section A short questions (40) cover all topics; learn countries/capitals and attractions on maps; practise currency conversions (rand ÷/× rate); write itineraries with day, transport, accommodation, activity; use real examples in answers.
**Glossary**: inbound/outbound; gateway; long/short-haul; ACSA; GDS; CRS; DTGS; SADC; RETOSA; heritage; SAHRA; multiplier effect; above/below-the-line; itinerary; carbon footprint.
**Key facts**: SBA 25% + PAT 25% + written 50% (200 marks); PAT in two phases; exam sections A 40, B 20, C 50, D 50, E 40 (mapping UNVERIFIED).
**Bridge to Gr 12**: world heritage sites, world icons, tour budgets and documents, time zones, professional image, global events and forms of payment.

## LIFE ORIENTATION
**Strategy**: read the scenario and answer for the person in it; give advice with reasons; Section C questions need extended, structured answers; keep the PET log up to date.
**Glossary**: goal-setting; APS; bursary; learnership; SETA; civil society; rule of law; accountability; degradation; mitigation; adaptation; risk behaviour; gender roles; power relations.
**Key facts**: year mark from 5 tasks (80+20 marks per term); exams of 80 marks (20/30/30); no external exam in Gr 11.
**Bridge to Gr 12**: careers/study skills deepened, citizenship, final exam set provincially (80 marks).

## RELIGION STUDIES
**Strategy**: Paper 1: 50-mark short section compulsory, choose 2 source-based; Paper 2: argue a position from a religious perspective and critique it; define terms exactly; stay respectful and balanced.
**Glossary**: syncretism; ecumenism; proselytisation; symbol; myth; ritual; functionalist theory; conflict theory; theocracy; secularism; co-operative model; inter-religious dialogue.
**Key facts**: Gr 11 exams are two 2-hour papers of 150 marks; tests 100 marks (30 source, 30 short, 40 extended writing).
**Bridge to Gr 12**: internal differentiation within religions, central teachings and normative sources, annual social problems set in exam guidelines.

## AFRIKAANS EERSTE ADDISIONELE TAAL

**Woordelys**: werkwoord; selfstandige naamwoord; byvoeglike naamwoord; voegwoord; voorsetsel; omgekeerde woordorde; bysin; dubbele ontkenning; tydsvorme; lydende vorm; beeldspraak; opsomming; leesbegrip; kontekstuele vrae.
**Kernfeite**: Vraestel 1 80, Vraestel 2 70, Vraestel 3 100, Mondeling 50 (300); SGA 25% + eksamen 75%.
**Brug na Graad 12**: eksterne eksamen, langer transaksionele tekste, meer gedigte en verhale.
