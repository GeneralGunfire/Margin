## 1. Subject overview
Business Studies is about how businesses operate, grow and behave ethically. CAPS organises all grades into four knowledge areas: Business Environments, Business Ventures, Business Roles, Business Operations (each ~25% of the final exam). Gr 10 introduces environments, ventures and basic functions; Gr 11 deepens each (challenges of environments, company form of ownership, action plans, marketing, production, team dynamics, human resources); Gr 12 adds finance functions, investment, management and leadership, quality, and the legal aspects in more depth.
Split: no Algebra/Geometry-style split. I tag topics with CAPS knowledge area: **[ENV]** Business Environments, **[VEN]** Business Ventures, **[ROLE]** Business Roles, **[OPS]** Business Operations (tags are my own mapping, UNVERIFIED against CAPS section 3.1).

## 2. Assessment (CAPS Gr 11)
- Year mark 100 (25%) = converted from 650 marks: T1 Assignment 50 + Test 100; T2 Presentation 50 + Mid-year exam 300; T3 Project 50 + Test 100. Final exam 300 marks (75%). Total 400.
- Mid-year: one 3-hour paper, 300 marks, topics as per ATP (Terms 1-2). Final: one 3-hour paper of 300 marks.
- Final exam content weighting (Gr 10-12): Business Environment 25%, Business Ventures 25%, Business Roles 25%, Business Operations 25% (as read from the table; applies to the grade's own content).
- Cognitive levels: basic thinking 30%, moderately high 50%, higher-order 20%. About 10% of exams are problem-solving questions.
- Tests: two tests per year (Terms 1 and 3), at least 1 hour for 100 marks, same test for all classes in a grade, must meet exam standard.
- Project is mandatory (one per year); presentation may be written or oral; forms preferred: projects, oral presentations, case studies, reports, tests, data responses, exams.
- Gr 12 final: one 3-hour, 300-mark paper covering the full scope (external). Gr 12 trial exam is 300 marks.
- Exam question types (typical, from the format seen): short questions (multiple choice/matching/terminology), case-study based questions, and essay-type questions. Exact Gr 11 section marks UNVERIFIED (CAPS table showed the Gr 10 split 30/90/80 only).

## 4. Term outline (CAPS Gr 11 ATP)

### Term 1 (10 wks). Assessment: assignment (50) + test (100); term total 150. Whole term's content must be covered for the test.
1. **[ENV] Influences on business environments (Week 1):** control factors: most control over micro (mission, objectives, structure, resources, culture), less over market (customers, suppliers, competitors, regulators, allies, unions), least over macro (economic, technological, political); ways to be involved in the macro environment where beneficial.
2. **[ENV] Challenges of the business environment (Week 2):** challenges in micro (difficult employees, lack of vision, management skills, strikes, unions), market (competition, supply shortages, changing consumer behaviour, demographics, psychographics, socio-cultural factors), macro (income levels, political changes, legislation, labour restrictions, micro-lending, globalisation, social values).
3. **[ENV] Adapting to challenges (Week 3):** information management; strategic responses; mergers, takeovers, acquisitions and alliances; organisation design and flexibility; direct influence; social responsibility; lobbying, networking, power relations (examples: hedging against inflation, bargaining between management and unions).
4. **[ENV] Contemporary socio-economic issues (Weeks 4-5):** impact on operations and productivity (income, inflation, crime, ethical misconduct, population growth, illiteracy, skills shortages, resource depletion, dumping, piracy, strikes); business solutions; intellectual property (copyright, patent, trade mark); industrial relations (strikes, go-slows, lockouts; Labour Relations Act purpose; trade unions: history, roles, functions).
5. **[VEN] Business sectors (Week 6, ~1 hour):** links between primary, secondary and tertiary enterprises.
6. **[VEN] Benefit of a company over other forms of ownership (Week 6-7):** recap characteristics, advantages and disadvantages of forms of ownership; benefits and challenges of a company vs sole trader, partnership, close corporation, co-operative (including taxation); formation of a company: Memorandum of Incorporation, name, incorporation and commencement, prospectus.
7. **[VEN] Avenues of acquiring a business (Week 8):** franchising, outsourcing, leasing: advantages, disadvantages, contractual implications (royalties, legalities).
8. Revision and term test (Weeks 9-10).
(Gr 11 Summary ATP table also lists "Impact and challenges of contemporary socioeconomic issues" and "Business sectors benefits of a company versus other forms of ownership" in Weeks 4-7.)

### Term 2 (10 wks). Assessment: presentation (50) + mid-year exam (300); term total 350
1. **[ROLE] Creative thinking and problem solving (Week 1, ~2 h):** applying creative thinking to business problems; routine vs creative thinking, mental blocks, idea generation; assessing creative solutions against reality.
2. **[ROLE] Stress, crisis and change management (Week 1, ~2 h):** concepts; managing stress; crisis and change; adapting to change (unemployment, retrenchment, globalisation, affirmative action).
3. **[VEN] Transforming a business plan into an action plan (Weeks 2-3):** planning tools: Gantt charts or Work Breakdown Structure with timelines and responsibilities; project planning; may be done collaboratively.
4. **[VEN] Setting up / starting a business venture (Weeks 4-5):** start a venture based on an action plan; acquiring funding (equity capital, loans/debt, other sources).
5. **[ROLE] Professionalism and ethics (Week 6, ~2 h):** definitions and differences; theories and principles; applying them in business; King Code of Governance 2009 as resource.
6. **[ROLE] Presentation of business information (Week 6, ~2 h):** verbal and non-verbal, tables, graphs, diagrams, posters, handouts; respond professionally to questions.
7. Revision (Weeks 7-8); mid-year exam (Weeks 9-10). Project for Term 3 is given in Term 2.

### Term 3 (10 wks). Assessment: project (50) + test (100); term total 150
1. **[VEN] Entrepreneurial qualities and success factors (Week 1):** assess a business against qualities (risk-taking, perseverance, management); reflect on a venture.
2. **[ROLE] Citizenship roles and responsibilities (Week 2):** how citizens contribute through business to community development; business community; role of business, civil society, NGOs, CBOs.
3. **[OPS] Marketing function (Weeks 3-6):** marketing activities; product policy (development, design, packaging, trade marks); pricing policy; distribution policy (channels, intermediaries); marketing communication; marketing in the formal and informal sectors; use of technology; imports and exports; foreign marketing and its alignment with production.
4. **[OPS] Production function (Weeks 7-9):** systems (mass, batch, job); production planning (planning, routing, scheduling, loading) and control (dispatching, follow-up, inspection, corrective action); safety management (Occupational Health and Safety Act, workplace safety policy); quality control (SABS, quality management systems); production costs (total cost of production calculations).
5. Revision (Week 10).

### Term 4 (10 wks). Assessment: final exam 300 marks
1. **[ROLE] Team dynamics and conflict management (Weeks 1-2):** team development stages (forming, storming, and onwards: full list not extracted); team dynamics theories applied to case studies; conflict management.
2. **[OPS] Introduction to Human Resources (Weeks 3-4):** HR activities (recruitment, selection, interviewing, contracts, induction, salaries, benefits); Labour Relations Act 66 of 1995, Basic Conditions of Employment Act 75 of 1997, Employment Equity Act 55 of 1998, Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act 61 of 1997; employment contract legalities; personal tax link; employee benefits (pension, medical, other).
3. Revision (Weeks 5-6); end-of-year exam (Weeks 7-10 as per CAPS table).
"Recap" in the ATP means the content is also examinable.
