## Exam answer skills (all topics)
- Question types: short items (matching, terminology, multiple choice), case studies with direct and indirect questions, essay-type questions with headings; verbs: list, name, explain, discuss, evaluate, advise, recommend.
- Essay format: introduction, body with sub-headings and "discuss/explain/evaluate" points, conclusion, and often a mark for own opinion; use business examples from the case study.
- Mistakes: listing without explanation; not linking to the scenario; repeating points; ignoring mark allocation (1 mark ≈ 1 fact/explained point).

## Term 1 topics
**1. Influences on business environments [ENV]**
- Covers: micro (mission, objectives, structure, resources, culture: most control), market (customers, suppliers, competitors, regulators, allies, unions: no control, opportunities and threats), macro (economic, technological, political and others: least control).
- Examined: classify factors, explain control levels; 4 to 8 marks.
- Mistakes: placing unions or competitors in the micro environment; thinking the business controls the market environment.
- Lessons: L1 "Three environments"; L2 "Control and involvement".
- Visuals: concentric-circles diagram.
**2. Challenges of the business environments [ENV]**
- Covers: challenges per environment (difficult employees, lack of vision; competition, supply shortages, changing consumer behaviour, demographics; income changes, legislation, globalisation, labour restrictions).
- Lessons: L1 "Micro challenges"; L2 "Market and macro challenges".
**3. Adapting to challenges [ENV]**
- Covers: information management; strategic responses; mergers, takeovers, acquisitions, alliances; flexible organisation design; social responsibility; lobbying, networking, power relations.
- Mistakes: mixing merger and takeover; no example from the scenario.
- Lessons: L1 "Strategies"; L2 "Lobbying and networking".
**4. Contemporary socio-economic issues [ENV]**
- Covers: effect on operations (inflation, crime, ethical misconduct, skills shortages, resource exhaustion, dumping, piracy, strikes); business solutions; intellectual property (copyright, patent, trade mark: nature and definition); industrial relations (strikes, go-slows, lockouts), Labour Relations Act (purpose), trade unions (history, roles, functions).
- Examined: cases on piracy and strikes; define each IP right; 8 to 15 marks.
- Mistakes: confusing patent and trade mark; strike vs lockout; listing the Act's sections.
- Lessons: L1 "Socio-economic issues"; L2 "Intellectual property"; L3 "Labour relations and unions".
**5. Business sectors [VEN]**
- Covers: links between primary, secondary and tertiary enterprises (supply chains).
- Lesson: L1 "Linking sectors" (visual flow).
**6. Company vs other forms of ownership [VEN]**
- Covers: recap of sole trader, partnership, close corporation, co-operative, private and public company; benefits and challenges of a company incl. taxation; formation (Memorandum of Incorporation, name, incorporation, commencement, prospectus).
- Mistakes: mixing private and public company features; mixing registration documents.
- Lessons: L1 "Forms of ownership recap"; L2 "Why a company?"; L3 "Forming a company".
- Visuals: comparison table (forms × features), company-formation flowchart.
**7. Avenues of acquiring a business [VEN]**
- Covers: franchising, outsourcing, leasing; advantages, disadvantages, contract implications (royalties, legalities).
- Lessons: L1 "Franchising"; L2 "Outsourcing and leasing".

## Term 3 (CAPS) topics
**14. Entrepreneurial qualities and success factors [VEN]**
- Covers: assess a business against qualities; critical reflection on a venture.
- Lesson: L1 "Assessing a venture".
**15. Citizenship roles and responsibilities [ROLE]**
- Covers: business contribution to community and social development; role of business, civil society, NGOs, CBOs.
- Lesson: L1 "Business and community".
**16. Marketing function [OPS]**
- Covers: marketing activities; product policy (development, design, packaging, trade marks); pricing policy; distribution (channels, intermediaries); marketing communication; formal and informal sectors; technology; imports/exports and foreign marketing alignment with production.
- Examined: 15 to 30 marks across a paper; identify the policy from a scenario; recommend strategy.
- Mistakes: confusing pricing and product policy; naming a channel without explaining.
- Lessons: L1 "Marketing activities"; L2 "Product and pricing"; L3 "Distribution and communication"; L4 "Foreign marketing".
- Visuals: marketing mix diagram; distribution channel diagram.
**17. Production function [OPS]**
- Covers: production systems (mass, batch, job), planning (planning, routing, scheduling, loading) and control (dispatching, follow-up, inspection, corrective action), safety management (OHS Act), quality control (SABS), total cost of production calculations.
- Examined: classify the system; calculations (total costs); 15 to 30 marks.
- Mistakes: mixing planning and control; arithmetic errors on cost totals.
- Lessons: L1 "Production systems"; L2 "Planning and control"; L3 "Safety and quality"; L4 "Costing".
- Visuals: process flow diagrams; cost table.

- Lessons: L1 "HR activities"; L2 "Recruitment to induction"; L3 "Labour legislation"; L4 "Salaries, tax and benefits".
