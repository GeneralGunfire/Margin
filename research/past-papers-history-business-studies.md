## HISTORY (legacy Gr 11 CAPS), EC Nov 2024
| Paper | Section A: source-based (3 questions, 50 marks each) | Section B: essays (3 questions, 50 marks each) |
|---|---|---|
| P1 (150 marks) | Q1 Communism in Russia (the impact of Lenin's New Economic Policy), Q2 Capitalism in the USA (the New Deal's response to the Great Depression), Q3 Ideas of race (pseudo-scientific race theories: Nazi/Australian case not confirmed) | Q4 Communism in Russia (case study: Stalin), Q5 Capitalism in the USA, Q6 Ideas of race (case study) |
| P2 (150 marks) | Q1 Nationalism in South Africa: African, Q2 Nationalism in South Africa: Afrikaner, Q3 Apartheid South Africa (impact of intensified resistance, 1940s-1960s) | Q4 African nationalism, Q5 Afrikaner nationalism, Q6 Apartheid South Africa: resistance |
- Format: learners answer THREE questions per paper: at least one but not more than two from Section A (source-based, addendum with sources) and at least one but not more than two from Section B (essays); Section A total 100, Section B total 50, grand total 150 (P1). Essay marking uses a matrix (50 marks).
- Findings: (1) CONFIRMS the legacy five-topic Gr 11 (Russia, USA, race, nationalisms with African and Afrikaner as the EC case-study choices, apartheid) and the Paper 1 / Paper 2 split in the 2023/24 ATP. (2) The Middle East and Ghana case studies are not in the EC paper (schools choose any two). (3) The draft amended CAPS topics (Atlantic World, Haiti, southern African kingdoms, Scramble for Africa) do NOT appear, as expected for 2024. (4) The "Ideas of race" Q3 and Q6 emphasise a case study; which case (Australia or Nazi Germany) the EC paper used was not confirmed. FIXES: history.md updated to note the EC 2024 structure.

## BUSINESS STUDIES (CAPS), EC Nov 2024: TWO papers of 150 marks, 2 hours each
| Paper | Section A (compulsory, 30 marks, 20 min) | Section B (answer any TWO of THREE, 40 marks each, 70 min) | Section C (answer ONE of TWO essays, 40 marks, 30 min) |
|---|---|---|---|
| P1: Business Environments and Business Operations | Q1 objective-type items (30) | Q2 Business environments; Q3 Business operations (includes quality management, TQM); Q4 Miscellaneous topics | Q5 Business environments (socio-economic issues); Q6 Business operations (human resources) |
| P2: Business Ventures and Business Roles | Q1 objective-type items (30) | Q2 Business ventures; Q3 Business roles; Q4 Miscellaneous topics | Q5 Business ventures (transforming a business plan into an action plan); Q6 Business roles (creative thinking and problem solving) |
- Essay marking: 40 marks = 32 for facts + 8 for insight.
- Findings: (1) CONFIRMS the 2023/24 ATP format of two 150-mark, 2-hour papers (CAPS's single 300-mark 3-hour final is NOT what this province used). (2) CONFIRMS the ATP term sequencing: P1 covers Terms 1-2 content areas (environments + operations incl. marketing/production, human resources, socio-economic issues) and P2 covers ventures + roles (action plan, creative thinking, entrepreneurial and team content). (3) HR (Term 4 in CAPS) sits in P1, so the final exam covers all Gr 11 topics across the two papers. (4) "Miscellaneous topics" questions draw on several topics together. FIXES: business-studies.md now states that the EC 2024 paper follows the ATP format (two papers) and the four knowledge areas split as above; the 25%-per-area weighting in the CAPS table is NOT visible directly (each paper is 50% of the marks and two areas).
