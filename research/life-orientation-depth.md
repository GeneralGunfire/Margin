# Life Orientation Grade 11 — PASS 2: DEPTH (companion to life-orientation.md)

Numbering follows life-orientation.md Section 4. LO is application-based; "examined" remarks follow the CAPS exam outline (Section A 20 short, Section B 30 scenario/source, Section C 30 application: advice, decisions, extended writing). Health, legal and admission specifics change; anything time-sensitive is UNVERIFIED until Pass 3/5. No exam questions copied. Resources: none verified yet. Lessons L1 to L5.

## Writing and answer skills for LO
- Section C: give advice with reasons, weigh options, link to the learner's own life; short paragraphs; use the scenario's details.
- Mistakes: moralising without reasons; one-word answers where explanation is required; ignoring the scenario.

## 1. Goal setting and relationships (Development of the self) — Term 1
- Covers: short/medium/long-term goals; steps in goal-setting; problem solving, perseverance; prioritising life goals (family, marriage, parenting, career, relationships); values, choices and goals; relationship types and their effect on well-being; rights and responsibilities; media influence on beliefs about relationships.
- Prereqs: Gr 10 self-awareness and goal setting.
- Examined: scenario: advise a character on a goal plan; explain how values affect choices; 6 to 15 marks.
- Mistakes: vague goals; ignoring obstacles; assuming media portrayals are accurate.
- Lessons: L1 "Setting and planning goals"; L2 "Values and choices"; L3 "Healthy and harmful relationships"; L4 "Media and relationships".
- Visuals: goal ladder; relationship wheel.

## 2. Admission and funding for further studies (Careers) — Term 1
- Covers: NSC requirements for certificate, diploma and degree; Admission Point Scores and programme requirements; evaluating options; bursaries, loans, scholarships, learnerships, SETAs; obligations of financial agreements.
- Key: APS calculation methods differ by institution (UNVERIFIED for 2027); NSC pass levels (30%/40%/50% bands: confirm in Pass 3).
- Examined: calculate/interpret an APS from a mark list, compare funding options; 6 to 12 marks.
- Mistakes: using last year's APS table; ignoring subject requirements (e.g. Mathematics for some degrees); not reading bursary conditions.
- Lessons: L1 "NSC pass types and admission"; L2 "APS"; L3 "Funding and obligations".
- Cross-link: Pure Maths (percentages, averages).

## 3. Democracy and human rights (participation, structures, sport) — Term 1
- Covers: public participation, petitions, governance, law-making, rule of law, transparency, representation, accountability; structures (national, provincial, local government; traditional authorities; parties; civil society); NGOs, CBOs, faith-based organisations, learner councils; how structures work (constitutions, elections, mandates, lobbying, advocacy, meetings); role of sport in nation building and spectator/participant behaviour.
- Examined: explain how citizens participate; analyse a sport-behaviour scenario; 6 to 15 marks.
- Mistakes: confusing the three spheres of government; "democracy = voting only".
- Lessons: L1 "How democracy works"; L2 "Government spheres and structures"; L3 "Civil society participation"; L4 "Sport and nation building".
- Visuals: government structure diagram; participation ladder.

## 4. Study skills — Term 2
- Covers: how learning happens; study styles vs strategies; exam-writing skills and the assessment process; time management and annual study plan; study, health and fitness goals.
- Examined: design a study plan from a scenario; evaluate a strategy; 6 to 10 marks.
- Mistakes: copying a timetable that ignores sleep/breaks; confusing style (preference) with strategy (approach to a task).
- Lessons: L1 "How I learn"; L2 "Strategies"; L3 "Exam technique"; L4 "Planning the year".
- Visuals: weekly planner template.

## 5. Environmental responsibility — Term 2
- Covers: ill-health from environmental issues (food production, farming methods), degradation hazards (erosion, pollution, radiation, floods, fires, wind, loss of open space), resource depletion (fishing, firewood, land), personal responses (attitudes, safety, first aid, disaster coping), climate change causes/impact/mitigation/adaptation, a community service action.
- Examined: explain causes and effects; propose mitigation; 6 to 12 marks.
- Mistakes: mixing mitigation (reduce causes) and adaptation (cope with effects); only global solutions, no personal/community actions.
- Lessons: L1 "Environmental health risks"; L2 "Degradation and depletion"; L3 "Climate change"; L4 "Community action".
- Cross-link: Tourism (sustainability), Physical Sciences (greenhouse effect).

## 6. Healthy lifestyle and risk — Term 3
- Covers: facets of a balanced lifestyle; accidents; risky behaviour (personal safety, road use, substance abuse, sexual behaviour, pregnancy, teenage suicide, diet, STIs, HIV and AIDS, peer pressure); socio-economic factors; positive influences; impact of unsafe practices; responsibility and seeking support; nutrition.
- Examined: scenario advice; list consequences; support services (confirm current helplines in Pass 3).
- Mistakes: judgemental language; unrealistic advice; out-of-date statistics.
- Sensitive content: suicide, sexual health, substance abuse: needs careful, age-appropriate framing (flag for content team).
- Lessons: L1 "Balanced lifestyle"; L2 "Accidents and road safety"; L3 "Substance and sexual risk"; L4 "Peer pressure and getting help"; L5 "Nutrition".

## 7. Careers: applications, CV, interviews, self-knowledge — Term 3
- Covers: reading adverts; application letters/forms; CV and testimonials; managing meetings and projects; interview skills; ethics; expectations vs reality; suitability audit; skilled/semi-skilled/unskilled work; studies needed for careers.
- Examined: write/critique a CV or letter; interview preparation; 8 to 15 marks. Term 3 project (80 marks) often on careers/lifestyle (topic school-set).
- Mistakes: generic CVs; errors in contact details; unprofessional email addresses; not tailoring to the advert.
- Lessons: L1 "Reading job adverts"; L2 "CV and letter"; L3 "Interviews"; L4 "Suitability audit".
- Cross-link: English HL transactional writing; Business Studies HR.

## 8. Gender roles and power — Term 4
- Covers: effects of gender roles on health and well-being; unequal power relations (physical abuse, incest, cultural practices such as different mourning periods, domestic and sexual violence/rape, sexual harassment at work); health effects; addressing inequality.
- Examined: analyse a scenario; propose solutions; 6 to 12 marks.
- Sensitive content: abuse and sexual violence: careful framing and support resources (flag for content team).
- Lessons: L1 "Gender roles"; L2 "Power and abuse"; L3 "Addressing inequality and getting help".

## 9. Religions, beliefs and moral dilemmas — Term 4
- Covers: contributions of diverse religions and belief systems to a harmonious society; clarifying own values; analysing dilemmas (right to life, euthanasia, cultural practices, economic and environmental issues); respecting differing opinions.
- Mistakes: promoting one view; no reasoning from values.
- Lessons: L1 "Religions and society"; L2 "Moral dilemmas".
- Cross-link: Religion Studies.

## 10. Physical Education (all terms, PET)
- Covers: programmes to improve fitness (T1); self-designed/indigenous/community games with umpiring, administration and leadership (T2); recreational/relaxation leadership (T3); modified traditional/non-traditional sports (T4); safety in each.
- Assessment: PET 20 marks per term: frequency (10) + movement performance (10); also tested in exams (safety, fitness concepts, leadership).
- Lessons: sample practical plan per term with safety checklist and fitness test recording (a theory sheet for exams).
- Visuals: fitness log; rules/diagrams of games (images optional).
