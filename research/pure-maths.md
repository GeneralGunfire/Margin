## 1. Subject overview
Mathematics (not Maths Literacy). Grade 11 extends Grade 10 algebra to rational exponents, surds and quadratic equations/inequalities, adds quadratic number patterns, new function families with a horizontal shift parameter p, trigonometric reduction formulae and graphs, circle geometry theorems, sine/cosine/area rules, compound decay and effective rates, three-event probability, and spread in statistics. Grade 12 then adds calculus, series, polynomials, inverses and logs, compound-angle trig, regression and counting. Time: 4.5 hours a week (CAPS time allocation).

## 4. Term outline (CAPS Gr 11 pace setter; weeks as per CAPS time allocation table, approximate where the table layout was ambiguous)

### Term 1 (11 weeks). Assessment: investigation/project (≥50 marks) + test
1. **[ALG] Exponents and surds (~3 wks)**
   - 1.1 Laws of exponents for rational exponents, x^(p/q) = q-th root of x^p
   - 1.2 Simplifying expressions with rational exponents
   - 1.3 Solving exponential equations with rational exponents
   - 1.4 Adding, subtracting, multiplying, dividing simple surds
   - 1.5 Solving simple equations involving surds (checking for extraneous solutions)
2. **[ALG] Equations and inequalities (~3 wks)**
   - 2.1 Completing the square
   - 2.2 Quadratic equations: factorisation and the quadratic formula
   - 2.3 Nature of roots (discriminant): real/non-real, rational/irrational, equal/unequal
   - 2.4 Quadratic inequalities in one unknown, with graphical interpretation
   - 2.5 Simultaneous equations: one linear, one quadratic
   - 2.6 Word problems leading to quadratic equations
3. **[ALG] Number patterns (~2 wks)**
   - 3.1 Patterns with constant second difference; quadratic general term Tn = an² + bn + c
   - 3.2 Finding the general term, using the first and second differences
4. **[GEO] Analytical geometry (~3 wks)** (listed in the pace setter at the end of Term 1)
   - 4.1 Equation of a line through two points
   - 4.2 Equation of a line through a point, parallel or perpendicular to a given line
   - 4.3 Inclination of a line, tan θ = m; angle between lines
   - (Gr 12 adds circles and tangents; not Gr 11.)

### Term 2 (11 wks, incl. 3 wks mid-year exams). Assessment: assignment/test (≥50) + mid-year exam
5. **[ALG] Functions (~4 wks)**
   - 5.1 Parabola y = a(x+p)² + q: effect of a, p, q
   - 5.2 Hyperbola y = a/(x+p) + q: effect of a, p, q; asymptotes
   - 5.3 Exponential y = a·b^(x+p) + q (b>0, b≠1): effect of parameters
   - 5.4 Average gradient between two points; intuitive idea of gradient at a point (leads to Gr 12 calculus)
   - 5.5 Determining equations from graphs; interpreting sketches
6. **[GEO] Trigonometry (~4 wks)**
   - 6.1 Point-by-point plotting of sin, cos, tan on [-360°; 360°]
   - 6.2 Effect of k in y = sin kx, cos kx, tan kx; effect of p in sin(x+p) etc.; sketch with at most two parameters
   - 6.3 Identities: tan θ = sin θ/cos θ, sin²θ + cos²θ = 1 (derive and use)
   - 6.4 Reduction formulae (180° ± θ, 360° ± θ, -θ, 90° ± θ)
   - 6.5 Proving identities; values for which an identity is undefined
   - 6.6 Trigonometric equations, general solutions and solutions in an interval
   - Note: pace setter puts Trig after Functions in T2.

### Term 3 (10 wks). Assessment: two tests (≥50 marks, 1 h each)
7. **[GEO] Measurement (~1 wk)** — CAPS lists it as a topic row with 1 week; the curriculum statement text was not recoverable from the extract. UNVERIFIED: probably revision of Gr 10 surface area/volume (the pace setter places measurement in Gr 10 T3 and Gr 11 T3 with 1 week).
8. **[GEO] Euclidean geometry (~3 wks)**
   - 8.1 Revise Gr 10 work; tangent ⟂ radius as an axiom
   - 8.2 Theorem: line from centre ⟂ chord bisects the chord; converse (perpendicular bisector passes through centre)
   - 8.3 Theorem: angle at centre = 2 × angle at circumference
   - 8.4 Theorem: angles in the same segment equal
   - 8.5 Theorem: opposite angles of a cyclic quadrilateral supplementary
   - 8.6 Tangents from an external point equal
   - 8.7 Tangent-chord theorem (angle in alternate segment)
   - 8.8 Riders using theorems and converses (converses cannot be asked as proofs)
9. **[GEO] Trigonometry: sine, cosine, area rules (~2 wks)**
   - 9.1 Proof and use of the sine rule, cosine rule, area rule
   - 9.2 2D problems (3D is Grade 12)
10. **[ALG] Finance, growth and decay (~2 wks)**
    - 10.1 Straight-line and reducing-balance depreciation (A = P(1-in), A = P(1-i)^n)
    - 10.2 Nominal and effective interest rates; different compounding periods
    - 10.3 Multi-stage timeline problems (rate changes, withdrawals)
11. **[ALG] Probability (~2 wks)**
    - 11.1 Addition rule, complementary rule, mutually exclusive events
    - 11.2 Dependent and independent events; product rule
    - 11.3 Venn diagrams with two and three events; contingency tables
    - 11.4 Tree diagrams for consecutive or simultaneous (not necessarily independent) events

### Term 4 (10 wks). Assessment: test (≥50) + final exam (2 × 150 marks)
12. **[GEO] Statistics (~3 wks)**
    - 12.1 Histograms, frequency polygons
    - 12.2 Ogives (cumulative frequency curves)
    - 12.3 Variance and standard deviation (ungrouped data, calculator)
    - 12.4 Symmetric and skewed data
    - 12.5 Identifying outliers (scatter plots and box-and-whisker)
13. Revision (~3 wks); final exams (~3 wks)
