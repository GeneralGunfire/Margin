## A. Language in context (Paper 1, 70 marks; Test 1, 35 marks)
**A1. Comprehension (reading for meaning)**
- Covers: literal, inferential and evaluative questions on a written text; vocabulary in context; tone, purpose, audience; fact vs opinion; bias.
- Key: answer in own words when asked; quote only when told; refer to line numbers; mark allocation shows how many points.
- Prereqs: Gr 10 comprehension; vocabulary.
- Examined: a passage with ~5 to 8 questions; typical verbs: identify, explain, comment on, quote, in your own words; 20 to 25 marks within Paper 1 (share UNVERIFIED).
- Mistakes: copying the whole sentence; ignoring the mark allocation; answering the question before last; confusing tone with mood.
- Ideas: annotate a news editorial; compare two views on the same topic.
- Lessons: L1 "Question types"; L2 "Inference and tone"; L3 "Vocabulary in context"; L4 "Timed comprehension practice".
- Visuals: annotated-text template; question-type colour code.
**A2. Visual literacy (adverts, cartoons)**
- Covers: reading images, text and layout; persuasive techniques; stereotypes; target audience; visual devices (colour, framing, symbolism).
- Examined: advert/cartoon section in Paper 1; "explain the effect of the visual", "how does the caption..."; 10 to 20 marks (UNVERIFIED).
- Mistakes: describing instead of analysing; ignoring the caption; claiming "it is funny" without explaining the irony.
- Lessons: L1 "Reading adverts"; L2 "Cartoons: irony and satire".
- Visuals: sample adverts/cartoons with annotation layers (images need licensing, UNVERIFIED).
**A3. Summary writing**
- Covers: identify main points in a passage, write in own words, within the word limit; sentences or bullet/numbered points as instructed.
- Key: select points, paraphrase, no repetition, keep word count.
- Examined: 5 to 10 marks; counting words; marks for points and language.
- Mistakes: lifting sentences; including examples; exceeding the limit; adding opinions.
- Lessons: L1 "Finding the main ideas"; L2 "Paraphrase and compress".
**A4. Language structures and conventions (grammar, editing, register)**
- Covers: parts of speech, tenses, concord, active/passive, direct/indirect speech, punctuation, figures of speech, register and style, editing a text for errors, dictionaries and vocabulary.
- Examined: editing a passage, correcting sentences, using figures of speech; 15 to 25 marks (UNVERIFIED).
- Mistakes: subject-verb agreement with collective nouns; comma splices; apostrophes; confusing homophones; tense shifts.
- Lessons: L1 "Concord and tenses"; L2 "Punctuation"; L3 "Active/passive and reported speech"; L4 "Register and style"; L5 "Editing practice".
- Visuals: error-spotting widget; punctuation cheat sheet.

## B. Literature (Paper 2, 80 marks; Task 6 and Test 2, 35 marks each: contextual 10 + literary essay 25)
(Set texts are the school's; skills below apply to any novel, drama, poem or short story.)
**B1. Contextual (extract) questions**
- Covers: understanding an extract in the whole text; character, setting, theme, language; "explain how this extract reflects…".
- Examined: 10 to 20 marks per text depending on paper design (UNVERIFIED).
- Mistakes: retelling the plot; not linking to the whole text; no evidence/quote.
- Lessons: L1 "Reading an extract"; L2 "Answering contextual questions".
**B2. Literary essay (novel/drama and poetry)**
- Covers: planning an argument about theme/character/technique; introduction with thesis; PEEL paragraphs (Point, Evidence, Explain, Link); conclusion. Lengths from CAPS: poetry ~200-250 words (Gr 11), novel/drama longer (exact count UNVERIFIED).
- Examined: 25 marks per essay; assessed on content and language; topics typically ask "to what extent…", "discuss".
- Mistakes: summary instead of argument; no quotations; ignoring the question wording; no conclusion.
- Lessons: L1 "Reading the question"; L2 "Planning and thesis"; L3 "PEEL paragraphs"; L4 "Poetry essays"; L5 "Timed essay".
**B3. Poetry (10 poems at Gr 11)**
- Covers: imagery, figures of speech, sound devices (alliteration, assonance, rhyme), tone and mood, structure, diction; how form supports meaning.
- Mistakes: naming a device without effect; paraphrasing; ignoring structure.
- Lessons: L1 "Imagery and figures"; L2 "Sound and rhythm"; L3 "Tone, mood, structure".
**B4. Novel / drama study (set text)**
- Covers: plot, character, setting, conflict, themes, narrative viewpoint, symbolism; for drama: stage directions, dramatic irony, dialogue, soliloquy.
- Prereqs: Gr 10 novel/drama study.
- Lessons: sequence planned per chapter/act once the text is known.
- Visuals: character relationship map; plot timeline (diagram tool).

## C. Writing and presenting (Paper 3, 100 marks; Task 2 essay 50; Task 3 transactional 25)
**C1. Essays: reflective, discursive, argumentative (Gr 11)**
- Covers: reflective (personal insight), discursive (balanced issue exploration), argumentative (position with evidence); structure, tone, cohesion, language use.
- Examined: one essay of 50 marks in an exam (choice of topics); marks for content/planning, language/style, structure.
- Mistakes: narrative instead of reflective; no clear line of argument; weak paragraphing; informal register; no editing.
- Lessons: L1 "Choosing and planning a topic"; L2 "Reflective essay"; L3 "Discursive essay"; L4 "Argumentative essay"; L5 "Revising and editing".
**C2. Transactional texts**
- Covers (CAPS Gr 11 list): friendly/formal letters (request, complaint, application, business), letters to the press, CV and covering letter, obituary, agenda and minutes, formal/informal report, review, newspaper or magazine article, speech, dialogue, interview.
- Examined: one text of ~25 marks (Paper 3 has a long and a short text section, split UNVERIFIED); marks for format/features, content, language.
- Mistakes: wrong format (address layout, subject line, minutes headings); wrong register; missing key features; excess length.
- Lessons: one lesson per family: L1 "Letters"; L2 "CV and covering letter"; L3 "Agenda and minutes"; L4 "Reports, reviews and articles"; L5 "Speeches, dialogue, interviews".
- Visuals: format templates (layout diagrams).
**C3. Process writing**
- Covers: planning, drafting, revising, editing, proof-reading, presenting; self- and peer-editing checklist.
- Lessons: L1 "Process writing checklist".

## D. Listening and speaking (Paper 4 Orals 50; oral tasks each term)
- Covers: listening for comprehension (15 marks), prepared speech (10 marks), unprepared speech (15 marks); debate, panel discussion, meeting procedures, storytelling/praise poems, vote of thanks, interview/dialogue.
- Key: structure (opening, body, close), eye contact, tone, pace, content; for listening: note-taking.
- Mistakes: reading a script; no structure; speaking too fast; ignoring audience.
- Lessons: L1 "Preparing a speech"; L2 "Delivery"; L3 "Unprepared speech"; L4 "Debates and panels"; L5 "Listening skills".
- Visuals: speech planning frame; marking-rubric checklist.
