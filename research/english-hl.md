## 1. Subject overview
Home Language English is taught as a language, a literature and a writing course at an advanced level. Grade 11 sits between Gr 10 (narrative/descriptive/argumentative essays; foundations of literary analysis) and Gr 12 (external NSC exam, longer literary essays, 12 poems). In Gr 11 the essay genres become reflective, discursive and argumentative, literary essays lengthen, and exams become three written papers plus an oral. Carry-over: close reading, literary terminology, formal register, editing, speeches.

## 2. Assessment (CAPS, Grade 11)
- Year mark: SBA 25% + end-of-year exam 75%. Exam weights: Paper 1 Language in context 17.5%, Paper 2 Literature 20%, Paper 3 Writing 25%, Paper 4 Orals 12.5% (CAPS promotion mark rules).
- Term 1: Task 1 oral (listening for comprehension 15 marks, OR prepared speech 10, OR unprepared speech 15); Task 2 essay (50 marks): reflective, discursive or argumentative; Task 3 transactional text (25 marks); Task 4 Test 1 (35 marks): comprehension, summary, language structures and conventions.
- Term 2: Task 5 oral; Task 6 literature (35 marks): contextual questions 10 + literary essay 25; Task 7 mid-year exam (250 marks): Paper 1 language in context 70, Paper 2 literature 80, Paper 3 writing 100 (Paper 3 may be written in May/June).
- Term 3: Task 8 oral; Task 9 Test 2 (35 marks): literature, contextual 10 + literary essay 25.
- Term 4: Task 10 oral; Task 11 final exam (300 marks): Paper 1 language 70, Paper 2 literature 80, Paper 3 writing 100, Paper 4 orals 50.
- Orals per year: one listening for comprehension, two prepared speeches, one unprepared speech.
- Text lengths (CAPS table, read from a garbled layout; approximate): Gr 11 literary essay on poetry ~200-250 words; novel/drama literary essays longer (Gr 10 300-350; Gr 12 400-450 as a guide, Gr 11 likely 350-400, UNVERIFIED).
- Grade 12: external NSC with Paper 1 (language), 2 (literature), 3 (writing), 4 (orals); see CAPS Section 4 and DBE papers.

## 4. Term outline (CAPS Gr 11 teaching plan, weeks in two-week blocks, 40-week year)
All terms repeat the same four strands. Order within terms below is as the plan lists it.

### Term 1 (weeks 1-10). Assessment: oral, essay (50), transactional (25), test 1 (35)
- **Listening and speaking:** listening for comprehension; debate (features, planning, researching, practising); prepared/unprepared speech.
- **Reading and viewing:** key features of texts and parts of a book including literary genres; reading for comprehension (vocabulary development and language use; sentence structures; strategies for written texts); interpretation of visual texts (adverts, cartoons, pictures); summary writing; literature study throughout (4 hours per block).
- **Writing and presenting:** transactional texts: friendly/formal letters (request, complaint, application, business), letters to the press, CV and covering letter, obituary, agenda and minutes; then report/review/newspaper or magazine article; then speech/dialogue/interview. Each uses process writing (plan, draft, revise, edit, proof-read, present).
- **Language structures and conventions:** register, style and voice; word choice; sentence construction; paragraph writing; punctuation and spelling; format and features of the chosen text.
- **Literature:** study of set text(s); (genres: novel, drama, poetry; short stories for enrichment only).
- Essay: one reflective/discursive/argumentative essay for formal assessment.

### Term 2 (weeks 11-20). Assessment: oral, literature task (35), mid-year exams (weeks 19-20)
- **Listening and speaking:** dialogue/interview/discussions/conversation; prepared/unprepared speech; panel discussion; informal discussions.
- **Reading and viewing:** comprehension (vocabulary, sentence structures); summary writing; interpretation of visual texts; literature study.
- **Writing:** transactional speech/dialogue/interview; 1 × reflective/discursive/argumentative essay; report/review/newspaper or magazine article.
- Mid-year exam preparation: Paper 1 language in context, Paper 2 literature, Paper 3 writing.

### Term 3 (weeks 21-30). Assessment: oral, Test 2 on literature (35)
- **Listening and speaking:** meetings and meeting procedures; storytelling and/or praise poems; prepared/unprepared speech.
- **Reading and viewing:** comprehension; interpretation of visual texts; summary; literature study.
- **Writing:** transactional letters, CV, obituary, agenda and minutes; 1 × reflective/discursive/argumentative essay; speech/dialogue/interview.

### Term 4 (weeks 31-40). Assessment: oral, final exam (300 marks: Papers 1-4)
- **Listening and speaking:** introducing a speaker / vote of thanks; informal discussions; debate/panel; report/review (prepared speech).
- **Reading and viewing:** comprehension strategies for visual texts; vocabulary and sentence structures; summary writing; literature study; exam preparation for Paper 1 (comprehension, summary, language structures and conventions).
- **Writing:** essay (reflective/discursive/argumentative); letters, CV, minutes; report/review/article.
- Final exams weeks 37-40 approximately (the plan shows exams at weeks 35-40 in the Gr 12 layout; Gr 11 exact weeks UNVERIFIED).
