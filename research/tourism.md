## 1. Subject overview
Tourism studies the industry that delivers travel experiences: sectors (transport, accommodation, attractions), map work and tour planning, attractions, culture and heritage, sustainability, domestic/regional/international tourism, foreign exchange, marketing, and communication and customer care. Gr 10 introduces tourists, modes of transport, accommodation, SA provinces and attractions, map terminology, sustainable tourism; Gr 11 extends to transport services in SA, the domestic tourism growth strategy, SADC and its attractions, itineraries, foreign exchange, marketing techniques and customer care/careers; Gr 12 adds world heritage sites, world icons, tour budgets/documents/time zones, professional image and employment, global events.

## 2. Assessment (CAPS Gr 11)
- Total 400: SBA 25% (Terms 1-3 each 100 = 300, converted to 100); PAT 25% (PAT 1 and PAT 2, 50 each = 100); written exam 50% (200 marks, 3 hours). 100 + 100 + 200 = 400 ÷ 4.
- Term 1 formal: test 75% + project OR field-trip assignment 25%. Term 2: test 25% + mid-year exam 75%; hand in PAT phase 1. Term 3: open-book test 25% + test 75%; hand in PAT phase 2. (2023/24 ATP names: Task 1 skills task, Task 2 March controlled test, Task 3 mid-year, Task 4 data-handling task, Task 5 September controlled test; PAT marks carried over to Term 4.)
- Gr 11 exam structure (CAPS table, Gr 10/11/12 columns; Gr 11 read as the middle column): Section A short questions (all topics) 40; Section B map work and tour planning, foreign exchange 20; Section C tourism attractions, culture and heritage tourism, marketing 50; Section D tourism sectors, sustainable and responsible tourism 50; Section E domestic/regional/international tourism, communication and customer care 40. Total 200. (Column mapping UNVERIFIED.)
- Cognitive levels: lower/middle/higher order table exists in CAPS Section 4 (higher order ~20 marks per the extract); full percentages not extracted.
- Gr 12 external exam is 3 h, 200 marks, same section format; only Gr 12 content assessed.
- PAT is done in two phases; Gr 12 PAT is externally set; resources (maps, magazines, brochures) are the school's responsibility.
- Four hours per week, 10-week terms (CAPS; ATP shows 11 weeks in some terms).

## 4. Term outline (CAPS Gr 11 with 2023/24 ATP order)

### Term 1. Test + field-trip assignment/project
1. **Tourism sectors: transport services in South Africa**
   - Airports, airlines and airport operations: gateways, inbound/outbound, domestic/regional/intercontinental flights, long/medium/short-haul, chartered flights, SA international and national airports (ACSA), airline timetables, airport and aircraft terminology, check-in and boarding procedures, baggage allowances, safety procedures, aircraft seating, technology at airports
   - The tourism bus industry: coaches, minibuses, sightseeing/open-top buses, bus schedules
   - The tourism train industry: commuter (Metrorail) vs tourist trains; luxury trains (Blue Train, Rovos Rail, Shongololo, Premier Classe); train terminology
   - The Gautrain: features, technology, routes, benefits for tourism
   - The luxury cruise liner industry: port, terminal, cabins, decks, facilities
   - (Car rental is listed under Term 1 in CAPS Gr 11 overview and appears in the ATP at the start of Term 2.)
2. Revision and March controlled test; field-trip assignment (attraction, establishment or destination linked to Gr 11 curriculum).

### Term 2. Test + mid-year exam (+ PAT phase 1)
3. **Tourism sectors: car rental** (conditions, packages, insurances CDW/TLW/SCDW/STLW/PAI, additional and incidental costs, payment options)
4. **Domestic, regional and international tourism: Domestic Tourism Growth Strategy (DTGS):** concept; objectives (sustainable growth, transformation, empowerment and job creation); ways to meet them (more expenditure, reduce seasonality, geographic spread, increased volumes, events, safety)
5. **DTGS market segments:** CAPS lists seven segments (young and upcoming; independent young couples and families; striving families; well-off homely couples; home-based low-income couples; older families with basic needs; golden active couples), the seven products (city breaks, mountain escapes, coastal getaways, bush retreats, countryside meanders, cultural discoveries, event wonders), segment statistics. DISAGREEMENT: the 2023/24 ATP wording says "five domestic market segments" and "DTGS 2012-2020". I trust the CAPS text for the examined content, but flag that a newer DTGS version may apply (CAPS itself says "latest version"). UNVERIFIED.
6. **Culture and heritage tourism:** South African cultural uniqueness (folklore, dress, cuisine, gumboot dancing, kwaito, sangomas, Ndebele art, Zulu dances, San, festivals); how diversity promotes inbound and domestic tourism; heritage bodies (SAHRA logo and functions, provincial agencies, heritage permits and protection regulations).
7. **Foreign exchange:** currencies and codes (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, AUD, ZAR), exchange rate fluctuations, bureaux and banks, multiplier effect of tourist spending, reading a currency rate sheet, converting currencies to and from rand (two decimals).
8. **Marketing (ATP places it here):** above-the-line and below-the-line promotional techniques; matching promotion to customer; marketing budget costs.
9. Revision; mid-year exam. PAT phase 1 handed in.

### Term 3. Open-book test + test (+ PAT phase 2)
10. **Map work and tour planning: the tour itinerary:** concepts (itinerary, logical tour planning, scheduled tours); factors; types; writing an itinerary (day, time, transport, accommodation, attractions, activities, optional budget).
11. **Regional tourism:** SADC and member countries with capitals on a map; gateways (road, air, water; main airport, harbour, border post); RETOSA; advantages of regional tourism.
12. **Tourism attractions: main attractions in SADC countries** (with * for World Heritage Sites in CAPS): Angola (Kissama), Botswana (Okavango, Chobe, Tsodilo*), DRC (Kahuzi-Biega*, Virunga*, Salonga*), Lesotho (Maluti, pony trails, Katse Dam, Sani Pass), Madagascar (Tsingy*, Royal Hills*), Mauritius, Mozambique (Cahora Bassa, Lake Niassa, Bazaruto, Island of Mozambique*), Namibia (Fish River Canyon, Etosha, Namib, Skeleton Coast), Malawi, Seychelles, Swaziland (Eswatini), Tanzania (Kilimanjaro*, Serengeti, Zanzibar*, Ngorongoro*), Zambia (Victoria Falls, Kariba, Kafue), Zimbabwe (Victoria Falls, Hwange, Great Zimbabwe*, Matopo*, Mana Pools*). Use latest SADC membership; country names may need current names (e.g. Eswatini): UNVERIFIED against newest CAPS.
13. **Communication and customer care (early):** Global Distribution Systems (Amadeus, Galileo, Worldspan) and central reservation systems (e.g. Micros-Fidelio Opera); (service and service excellence concepts per ATP).
14. Revision and September controlled test; data-handling task; PAT phase 2.

### Term 4. Final exam (200 marks)
15. **Communication and customer care:** customer care for foreign tourists (respect for traditions, effective cross-cultural communication, benefits of excellence); customer complaints (verbal/written, value of complaints, the six steps: listen, ask questions caringly, apologise without blaming, solve immediately, offer something, thank the customer); managing quality service (strategies: performance management, quality checks, surveys, peer reviews, training; how services in one sector affect another).
16. **Tourism sectors: job and career opportunities** (transport, hospitality, attractions, events and conferences, tourism services, travel trade); requirements and inherent qualities of a chosen sector; personality type; **entrepreneurial opportunities** (concept, skills, identifying products/services, starting a tourism business).
17. **Sustainable and responsible tourism (ATP recovery of Gr 10 content in Term 4):** sustainability concept; three pillars; environmental, social, economic impacts; responsible tourist behaviour; good environmental practices; global warming, carbon footprint, causes and consequences, how the industry can reduce its footprint. The CAPS Gr 11 overview table leaves this topic empty for Gr 11; the ATP includes it as Gr 10 recovery. Examinability in Gr 11 papers is UNVERIFIED.
18. Revision; November exam.
