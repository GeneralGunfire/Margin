## 1. Transport services in South Africa (Tourism sectors) — Term 1
**1.1 Airports, airlines and airport operations**
- Covers: air-travel terms (gateway, inbound/outbound, domestic, regional, intercontinental, long/medium/short-haul, chartered); SA international/national airports and ACSA; airline timetables; airport and aircraft terminology; check-in, boarding, baggage allowances; aircraft seating; airport technology; "iTravel" digital documents.
- Prereqs: Gr 10 modes of transport.
- Examined: short definitions; read a timetable; match term to meaning; locate airports on a map; 6 to 15 marks.
- Mistakes: confusing inbound/outbound or gateway/destination; misreading 24-hour times and time zones; mixing landside and airside.
- Lessons: L1 "Air-travel terminology"; L2 "SA airports and airlines"; L3 "Airport procedures and technology"; L4 "Reading timetables".
- Visuals: SA map of airports; sample flight timetable; airport layout diagram.
**1.2 Bus, train, Gautrain and cruise-liner industries**
- Covers: types of tourist buses and schedules; commuter vs tourist trains, luxury trains and rail terminology; Gautrain features, technology, routes and benefits; cruise terms and facilities.
- Mistakes: confusing Metrorail with tourist trains; outdated route/price facts.
- Lessons: L1 "Tourist buses and trains"; L2 "Gautrain"; L3 "Cruise liners".
**1.3 Car rental**
- Covers: rental conditions (age, licence, creditworthiness, contract); packages; insurances (CDW, TLW, SCDW, STLW, PAI); additional and incidental costs; payment options.
- Examined: calculate a rental cost; explain insurance types; 6 to 10 marks.
- Mistakes: confusing standard with super waivers; forgetting airport surcharges and fuel charges in a total.
- Lessons: L1 "Rental conditions and packages"; L2 "Insurance and extra costs".

## 2. Domestic, regional and international tourism — Terms 2 to 3
**2.1 Domestic Tourism Growth Strategy (DTGS)**
- Covers: purpose; objectives; ways to meet them (more expenditure, reduce seasonality, spread geographically, increase volumes, use events, improve safety).
- Mistakes: mixing the strategy with segments; outdated strategy version (CAPS says "latest version").
- Lessons: L1 "What and why of the DTGS"; L2 "Objectives and ways to meet them".
**2.2 Domestic market segments and products**
- Covers: seven segments per CAPS (young and upcoming; independent young couples and families; striving families; well-off homely couples; home-based low-income couples; older families with basic needs; golden active couples), segment statistics, seven products (city breaks, mountain escapes, coastal getaways, bush retreats, countryside meanders, cultural discoveries, event wonders). ATP says "five"; see tourism.md.
- Lessons: L1 "Segments and profiles"; L2 "Matching products to segments".
- Visuals: profile cards; segment × product matrix.
**2.3 Regional tourism: SADC**
- Covers: member countries and capitals on a map (latest membership); gateways (road, air, water; main airport, harbour, border post); RETOSA; advantages for SA and the region.
- Examined: label a SADC map; give capitals and gateways; 6 to 10 marks.
- Mistakes: confusing capitals (e.g. administrative vs legislative); outdated country names (e.g. Swaziland → Eswatini); border-post errors.
- Lessons: L1 "SADC map and capitals"; L2 "Gateways"; L3 "RETOSA and advantages".
- Visuals: SADC map with clickable countries (map/graph library).

## 3. Culture and heritage tourism — Term 2
- Covers: South African cultural uniqueness (folklore, dress, cuisine, gumboot dancing, kwaito, sangomas, Ndebele art, Zulu dances, San, festivals) and how diversity promotes inbound and domestic tourism; heritage bodies (SAHRA logo/functions, provincial agencies); heritage permits and protection regulations (structures older than 60 years, archaeological/palaeontological sites, meteorites, shipwrecks, burial grounds).
- Examined: explain importance of culture for tourism; SAHRA functions; 6 to 12 marks.
- Mistakes: stereotyping cultures; mixing SAHRA with UNESCO (UNESCO is Gr 12).
- Lessons: L1 "Cultural uniqueness"; L2 "Heritage bodies"; L3 "Heritage protection rules".

## 4. Foreign exchange — Term 2
- Covers: local vs foreign currency, exchange rate, bureaux and banks, major currencies and codes; multiplier effect; reading a rate sheet; converting to and from rand.
- Key: amount in rand = foreign amount × rate (rand per unit); foreign amount = rand ÷ rate; use the bank's rate as provided in the question; two decimals.
- Examined: 4 to 8 marks; conversion tables; usually a rate sheet in the paper.
- Mistakes: multiplying instead of dividing; mixing buying and selling rates (selling/buying rates are Gr 12 content: UNVERIFIED in Gr 11 sheet usage); rounding early.
- Lessons: L1 "Currencies and rates"; L2 "Converting to rand"; L3 "Converting from rand"; L4 "Multiplier effect".
- Cross-link: Pure Maths (ratios, percentage).
- Visuals: conversion calculator; example rate sheet (original, not copied).

## 5. Tourism attractions: SADC — Term 3

- Lessons: L1 "Southern Africa attractions"; L2 "Central and East attractions"; L3 "Islands and Indian Ocean"; L4 "Map practice".
- Visuals: SADC attraction map with photos (image licensing UNVERIFIED).
