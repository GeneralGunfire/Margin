## 1. Subject overview
Afrikaans EAT is a language-learning course for learners whose home language is not Afrikaans. It develops four skills: **Luister en praat**, **Lees en kyk**, **Skryf en aanbied**, **Taalstrukture en -konvensies**, plus a literature component (Letterkunde). Gr 10 consolidates basic structures; Gr 11 widens vocabulary, text types and literary analysis; Gr 12 is an external exam (Vraestel 1-3 plus orals).
Time: not extracted, UNVERIFIED (FAL is 4.5 hours per week in CAPS time allocation tables generally, taken from the Tourism CAPS table for Gr 10-12 "First Additional Language 4.5", which is a different document; confirm in Pass 2).
Split: no Algebra/Geometry-type split; I use the four CAPS skills.

## 2. Assessment (CAPS Gr 11)
- SBA 25% + end-of-year exam 75% (62.5% written exam + 12.5% oral). Gr 10-11: 2 tests, 7 tasks, 1 mid-year exam per CAPS Table 1.
- Term 1: Task 1 oral (luisterbegrip 10 OR voorbereide toespraak 20 OR one of voorbereide hardoplees / onvoorbereide toespraak / informele praat in groepe 20); Task 2 Skryf essay (50 marks): Gr 11 narrative/descriptive/argumentative/reflective (verhalend, beskrywend, beredeneerd/argumenterend, bespiegelend); Task 3 Skryf long transactional text (30 marks): letters (vriendskaplik, formeel: versoek/klagte/aansoek/sake/bedanking/simpatie/gelukwensing), brief aan die pers, CV and dekbrief, huldeblyk, agenda and notule, verslag, resensie, koerantberig, tydskrifartikel, toespraak, dialoog, onderhoud; Task 4 Toets 1 (40 marks): Taal in konteks (leesbegrip, opsomming, taalstrukture en -konvensies).
- Term 2: Task 5 oral; Task 6 Letterkunde kontekstuele vrae (35 marks); Task 7 mid-year exam (250): Vraestel 1 Taal in konteks 80; Vraestel 2 Letterkunde 70; Vraestel 3 Skryf 100 (may be written in May/June).
- Term 3: Task 8 oral; Task 9 Skryf short transactional text (20 marks): advertensie, dagboekinskrywing, poskaart, uitnodiging, vorm invul, instruksies, aanwysings, strooibiljet, plakkaat, e-pos; Task 10 Toets 2 (40 marks): Taal in konteks OR Letterkunde kontekstuele vrae (35).
- Term 4: Task 11 end-of-year exam (300): Vraestel 1 (2 h) Taal in konteks 80; Vraestel 2 (2 h) Letterkunde 70; Vraestel 3 (Gr 11: 2.5 h) Skryf 100; Vraestel 4 Mondeling 50 (one voorbereide toespraak, one luisterbegripsoefening and one of hardoplees/onvoorbereide toespraak/informele gesprek).
- Cognitive levels per task (CAPS Table 3): levels 1-2 (letterlik, herorganisasie) 40%; level 3 (afleiding) 40%; levels 4-5 (evaluering, waardering) 20%.
- Gr 12: external exam, same three papers + oral; Gr 12 papers weighted 20% (P1), 17.5% (P2), 25% (P3), 12.5% (oral) per CAPS (the document lists P1/P2 weightings in a way that needs rechecking: UNVERIFIED).

## 4. Term outline (CAPS/ATP skills grid; weeks in 2-week blocks)

### Term 1
- **Luister en praat:** introduce yourself; listen for comprehension (luisteroefening); listen to a summary (hoofgedagtes, opskrif); onderhoud (procedures/role play); prepared reading aloud (teks uit voorgeskrewe letterkunde, gedig, dramagedeelte), group discussion on visual texts (spotprente: konteks, doel, illustrasie, handeling); listening to a poem or song.
- **Lees en kyk:** aandagtige lees (leesstrategieë): read visual/information texts, key facts, make inferences, fact vs opinion; letterkunde study: discuss features and effectiveness of literary text(s), theme/message, plot in drama/roman/kortverhaal, beeldspraak in a poem, characters, retoriese middele; extended independent reading; read an onderhoud; read instructions/directions.
- **Skryf en aanbied:** informatiewe paragraaf, verslag/verklaring (e.g. ooggetuieverslag), creative paragraph/essay in response to a literary theme, punte-form summary, verhalende opstel with characters, brief aan die pers; process writing (beplanning, konsep, hersiening, redigering, proeflees, aanbieding).
- **Taalstrukture en -konvensies:** spelreëls en spelpatrone; woordvorming (hersien); selfstandige en byvoeglike naamwoorde; voornaamwoorde; deelwoorde; voegwoorde (groep 1, 2, 3); punktuasie and skryftekens; direkte en indirekte rede; thesaurus and dictionary use; woordeskat in konteks; correcting learners' own errors.
- Formal: Taak 1 luisterbegrip; Taak 2 opstel; Taak 3 lang transaksionele teks; Taak 4 Toets 1.

### Term 2
- **Luister en praat:** group discussion of a text (kopkaart/visual); prepared speech (mondelinge aanbieding); meeting (vergadering) and debate procedures (standpunt).
- **Lees en kyk:** visual texts; letterkunde texts (karakter, verteller, intrige, tyd, ruimte, taal en styl; compare and contrast); newspaper/magazine reading on pollution/other issues; critical reading; independent reading.
- **Skryf en aanbied:** rigtingaanwysings; describe a character and background; agenda and notule of a meeting; magazine advertisement; process writing.
- **Taalstrukture:** spelreëls; voornaamwoorde; enkelvoudige sin; skryftekens; voegwoorde; figuurlike taal en retoriese middele (e.g. vergelyking, personifikasie); metataal.
- Formal: Taak 5 prepared speech; Taak 6 Letterkunde kontekstuele vrae; Taak 7 mid-year exam (Papers 1-3).

### Term 3
- **Luister en praat:** panel discussion; theme/issue discussion in texts (short story/roman); prepared formal researched speech; listening to a drama reading; reading aloud.
- **Lees en kyk:** theme/motief in literary texts; reading for comprehension; compare register, style and tone; advertisements; huldeblyk; rhetorical devices; enrichment text; letters to the press.
- **Skryf en aanbied:** a series of arguments/voorstel/mosie; beredeneerde/bespiegelende opstel; kennisgewing; formal letter (navraag); descriptive text; brief aan die pers.
- **Taalstrukture:** spelling (meervoude); tydsvorme; saamgestelde sinne; homoniem/homofone; denotasie and konnotasie; dictionary/thesaurus; veronderstellings.
- Formal: Taak 8 oral; Taak 9 short transactional text; Taak 10 Toets 2.

### Term 4
- Revision, exam preparation for Vraestel 1-3, final oral (Taak 10 label in CAPS Gr 11 table), Taak 11 exam. Skills as above. Content details not extracted, UNVERIFIED.
